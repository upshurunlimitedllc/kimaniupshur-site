About Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Kimani Upshur, M.Ed., is a Christian author, educator, and founder of Upshur Unlimited LLC. He is the creator of The Made Simple Framework, a growing collection of books that makes complex ideas understandable and useful in everyday life.

Upshur Unlimited LLC was founded on one principle: deliverance and redemption by Jesus Christ. That is Kimani's own story: his past was not all good, but God was not finished with him — and people who knew that past may be surprised at what he puts into the world now. He tells that testimony in his book Man Sees the Sin, God Sees Within — judged by man, known by God (1 Samuel 16:7). Man sees the sin — God sees within.

With a growing catalog of 1,000+ books and 301 free faith-based apps, Upshur Unlimited LLC is committed to making biblical education, practical wisdom, and valuable knowledge accessible to readers everywhere.

Grounded in his Christian faith, Upshur approaches learning, leadership, decision-making, and personal growth with an emphasis on biblical wisdom, integrity, discernment, service, and respect for others. He believes knowledge should be used responsibly and that faith in Jesus Christ provides the foundation for a life of purpose.

His writing explores systems thinking, strategy, psychology, technology, economics, and practical problem-solving, alongside books that directly address Scripture, Christian living, and spiritual growth. Through the Made Simple series, he seeks to help readers think clearly, make wise decisions, and apply what they learn with humility and compassion.

His mission is to make knowledge accessible while encouraging readers to pursue truth, serve others, and honor God in every area of life.
