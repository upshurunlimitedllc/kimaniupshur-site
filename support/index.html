Support Our Mission

Upshur Unlimited LLC publishes Christian books, children's and teen resources, audiobooks, and Bible films — and gives a large part of that work away free. Salvation is free at every retailer. Hundreds of faith apps are free. The daily devotional is free. They stay free whether or not anyone ever contributes a cent.

Some readers, churches, and businesses choose to help carry the cost of that free work. If that is you, thank you. This page is the one place to do it — voluntarily, in the open, with everything stated plainly.

How Support Is Used

Voluntary support and project sponsorship help fund:

  • Free Salvation distribution, in English and Spanish
  • New Christian children's and teen books
  • Bible films and educational videos
  • Audiobook production, so more titles can be heard as well as read
  • International-language editions of proven titles

Support pays production and distribution costs only: editing and review, cover and media production, narration, translation review, hosting, and platform delivery.

Stated Plainly

Two things we state plainly: Upshur Unlimited LLC is a for-profit company, and support given here is not tax-deductible. And no contribution is ever required to access any free material — free means free, with no account, no email, and no obligation.

Sponsor a Free Resource

Choose a named free resource and help fund its continued free distribution — the resource stays free for everyone; sponsorship is appreciation, never a toll.

  • Keep Salvation free — free distribution of the complete edition, in English and Spanish, at every retailer and by direct download.
  • Sponsor a children's or teen title's free edition — new Made Simple Through Christ titles for ages 6–12 and 13–18.
  • Underwrite a Bible film's free release — cinematic Bible productions released free on YouTube.
  • Fund a Spanish edition's free release — professionally reviewed translations of proven titles.

To sponsor a named project, use any support button on this page and name the project in the note where the payment path offers one — designation is by declaration in this first version, and we say so honestly rather than pretending precision. With a sponsor's written permission, a “Supported by” line (name or business, no amounts) may appear on the resource's page.

Project Sponsorship

Businesses, churches, and ministries can sponsor a named project area: free Salvation distribution · Christian children's & teen books · Bible films & educational videos · audiobook accessibility · international-language resources.

Sponsorship levels:

Sponsor at $100 Sponsor at $250 Sponsor at $500

Recognition, at the sponsor's choice and always optional: a “Supported by [name]” credit line on the sponsored title's product page and in its front/back matter · recognition in the project's release announcement and on this page · a completion report with the delivered work linked.

Recognition is acknowledgment only. Sponsorship carries no editorial control, no content approval rights, no endorsement of the sponsor's products or services, and no guaranteed exposure, placement, or audience figures. Project funding pays production and distribution costs only, and Upshur Unlimited LLC reports to each sponsor what their funding produced — outcomes, not impressions promised in advance. Sponsorships are accepted only where the sponsor's identity and line of business are consistent with a Christian publisher's public witness; we decline sponsorships that would require content changes, exclusivity over free resources, or any condition on what we publish. Free resources remain free to the public regardless of sponsorship. Every arrangement is confirmed in writing before any funds move.

Questions about sponsorship: reach us through the contact page.

Stated plainly, once more: Upshur Unlimited LLC is a for-profit company. Voluntary support and sponsorship given here are not tax-deductible. And no contribution is ever required to access any free material — free means free.
