The Christian Homeschool Bible Curriculum — A Genesis-to-Revelation Journey for Ages 6–12

The Christian Homeschool Bible Curriculum by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed. (Upshur Unlimited LLC) is a complete Bible curriculum for homeschool families, walking children through the whole Bible — Genesis to Revelation — in 48 units across twelve months. Ten months are complete now: 40 units, every one written, independently reviewed for doctrinal and editorial quality, and ready. Production continues month by month until the journey is finished.

Our foundation

This curriculum is built on Scripture itself. Every unit teaches the Bible’s own text — King James Version — read aloud, studied at each child’s level, and applied at the family table. Children do not only learn Bible stories; they learn the Bible’s own storyline: God’s creation, man’s fall, God’s promise, His covenant people, His Son. Every Christian unit carries the gospel at its centre, because the Bible does.

Who it is for

Homeschool families with children ages 6–12. Every unit is written at three levels — ages 6–8, 9–10, and 11–12 — so one family can teach one passage together at one table, with work fitted to each child.

How each unit works

Each unit includes a parent/teacher guide · read-aloud teaching from the passage · worksheets for each age level · hands-on activities · an assessment with answer keys · a closing prayer · a take-home activity. A running “Promise Line” of memory verses ties the whole Bible story together, unit by unit.

Learning objectives

By the end of the full journey, a child will be able to:

  1. retell the Bible’s storyline in order, from Creation to the early church and the promise of Christ’s return;
  2. locate and read the passages behind each unit in their own Bible;
  3. state what each passage teaches about God and about us;
  4. carry a growing line of memorised Scripture; and
  5. connect the Old Testament’s promises to their fulfilment in Jesus Christ.
