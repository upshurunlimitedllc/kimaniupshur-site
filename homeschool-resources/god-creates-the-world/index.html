God Made Everything (Creation)

A Bible Lesson for Kids (Ages 6–12) · Upshur Unlimited LLC Bible Lesson Library

Scripture: Genesis 1:1–2:3 (KJV)

Big Idea: God is the Creator of everything — He made the world, He made us in His image, and everything He made was very good.

Memory Verse: "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth." — Genesis 1:1 (KJV)

You Will Need: Seven blank cards or sheets of paper (one per day), crayons or markers, Bibles for older kids.

Opening Activity — "Who Made That?" (5 minutes)

Hold up a few things one at a time — a book, a shoe, a drawing. Ask each time: "Who made this?" (A person did.) Then point outside: "Who made the sky? The trees? You?" Say: "Things don't make themselves. Today we start at the very beginning — the first words of the Bible tell us exactly who made everything."

Bible Teaching (10–15 minutes)

The Bible opens with the greatest sentence ever written: "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth" (Genesis 1:1, KJV). Before anything existed, God was there — and God spoke, and things came to be.

On the first day, God said, "Let there be light," and there was light. God divided the light from the darkness and called them Day and Night. On the second day, God made the sky. On the third day, God gathered the waters so dry land appeared, and He made the grass, the plants, and the trees with their seeds. On the fourth day, God set the sun, the moon, and the stars in the sky to give light and to mark seasons, days, and years. On the fifth day, God filled the waters with living creatures and the sky with birds. On the sixth day, God made the animals that live on the land — and then God made His crowning work: people. "So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them" (Genesis 1:27, KJV).

God gave the people a charge: to fill the earth and care for His creation. Then God looked at everything: "And God saw every thing that he had made, and, behold, it was very good" (Genesis 1:31, KJV). On the seventh day, God rested from His work — not because He was tired, but because the work was finished and good.

The Point: Everything that exists exists because God made it. God did not find materials lying around; He created heaven and earth by His word and His power. And the most important thing He made is people: we are made in God's own image, which means we are made to know Him, to reflect Him, and to be loved by Him. You are not an accident. You are God's creation, made on purpose.

The Application: Because God made everything, everything belongs to Him — including us. So we treat His creation with care, we treat every person as someone made in God's image, and we thank the Maker for His gifts. When you see something beautiful this week, remember: God made that — and God made me, too.

Discussion Questions

  1. What did God use to create? (His word — He spoke, and it was so.) What does that show us about God's power?
  2. What did God say about His finished creation? Why do you think the Bible repeats "it was good"?
  3. What does it mean that people are made "in the image of God"? How should that change how we treat each other?
  4. God rested on the seventh day. He wasn't tired — so what was He showing us?
  5. What is your favorite thing God made? What does it tell you about Him?

Hands-On Activity — "Seven Days Creation Cards" (10–15 minutes)

Give out the seven cards. Each child (or pair) draws one day of creation: 1) light and darkness, 2) sky, 3) land, seas, and plants, 4) sun, moon, and stars, 5) birds and sea creatures, 6) animals and people, 7) God's rest (draw something peaceful). Under each drawing, write the day number. Then line the cards up in order and retell the week together, ending with Genesis 1:31: "it was very good."

Closing Prayer

"Dear God, You made the heaven and the earth, and You made us in Your image. Thank You for Your beautiful, very good creation. Help us to know You, our Maker, and to care for what You have made. In Jesus' name, amen."

Take-Home Family Activity

Take a "Creator Walk" together this week: walk outside and try to find something God made from each day of creation (light, sky, a plant, the sun or stars, a bird, an animal, and each other). Thank God out loud for each one. Memory verse for the week: "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth" (Genesis 1:1, KJV).
