Peace, Be Still (Jesus Calms the Storm)

A Bible Lesson for Kids (Ages 6–12) · Upshur Unlimited LLC Bible Lesson Library

Scripture: Mark 4:35–41 (KJV)

Big Idea: Jesus is Lord over all creation — even the wind and the sea obey Him, and He is with us in every storm.

Memory Verse: "What manner of man is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?" — Mark 4:41 (KJV)

You Will Need: A basin of water and a simple paper boat (or a leaf) for the hands-on activity, Bibles for older kids.

Opening Activity — "The Storm Voice" (5 minutes)

Have the kids make a storm with their voices and hands: fingers pattering for rain, hands rubbing for wind, a stomp for thunder. Get louder... then hold up a hand and say, "Peace, be still." Silence. Say: "A storm obeyed Jesus's voice faster than that. Let me show you."

Bible Teaching (10–15 minutes)

That evening, after a long day of teaching, Jesus said to His disciples, "Let us pass over unto the other side" of the sea. They took Him in the boat just as He was, and other little boats were with them. Jesus was asleep in the back of the boat, on a pillow (Mark 4:38).

Then a great storm of wind arose, and the waves beat into the ship until it was filling with water. These were experienced fishermen — they knew this sea — and they were afraid. They woke Jesus and cried: "Master, carest thou not that we perish?" (Mark 4:38, KJV).

Jesus arose. He rebuked the wind, "and said unto the sea, Peace, be still. And the wind ceased, and there was a great calm" (Mark 4:39, KJV). Not a slow dying-down — a great calm, all at once, at His word.

Then Jesus asked His disciples: "Why are ye so fearful? how is it that ye have no faith?" (Mark 4:40, KJV). And the disciples, now more amazed by Jesus than they had been afraid of the storm, said to one another: "What manner of man is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?" (Mark 4:41, KJV).

The Point: The storm revealed who Jesus is. Wind and waves obey no ordinary man — they obey their Maker. Jesus is not only a teacher or a prophet; He is the Lord of creation, with authority over the wind and the sea. And notice: the storm never meant Jesus didn't care. He was in the boat the whole time. His question to the disciples was not "why did you wake me?" — it was "why did you fear, when I am with you?" One honest note: Jesus' presence does not promise that every hard thing in our lives will stop the moment we pray. Sometimes He calms the storm; sometimes He calms His children and stays with them in it until it passes. Either way, we are never in the boat alone.

The Application: Storms come into our lives too — frightening news, a move, sickness, trouble at school. Feelings of fear are human; even fishermen feared. But we are never alone in the boat. The same Jesus who spoke "Peace, be still" is with His people today, and nothing in creation is stronger than He is. We can bring Him our fears — like the disciples did — and trust the One the wind and sea obey.

Discussion Questions

  1. Why were the disciples so frightened, even though Jesus was in the boat with them?
  2. What did Jesus do first — calm the disciples or calm the storm? Why do you think He spoke to the sea before He spoke to them?
  3. "Even the wind and the sea obey him." What does this miracle prove about who Jesus is?
  4. Jesus asked, "Why are ye so fearful?" What is the difference between feeling afraid and having no faith?
  5. What is a "storm" in your life right now where you need to remember Jesus is in your boat?

Hands-On Activity — "Great Calm" (10 minutes)

Float the paper boat in the basin. The kids make "wind" by blowing gently, then harder, until the boat rocks in the waves. Then the teacher reads Mark 4:39 aloud — "Peace, be still" — and everyone stops at once and watches the water settle into a great calm. Say: "The water didn't calm down slowly. It obeyed. The same voice that calmed that sea is the voice of our Savior."

Closing Prayer

"Dear God, thank You that Jesus is Lord over the wind and the sea, and that He is with us in every storm. When we are afraid, help us to trust Him. In Jesus' name, amen."

Take-Home Family Activity

Read Mark 4:35–41 together at bedtime. Each family member names one "storm" — something worrying — and the family prays about each one, thanking God that Jesus is in the boat. Memory verse for the week: "What manner of man is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?" (Mark 4:41, KJV).
