Why are these books free?

Because we would rather earn a reader than rent one. These books are complete, professionally published books — the same author, the same publisher, the same care as everything else in the bookstore — given away so new readers can meet the work without risk.

The rest of the bookstore is paid, and those books go deeper: fuller systems, longer treatment, whole libraries on a single subject. Each free book’s page points to the paid titles that continue its subject, clearly labeled as paid bookstore books. Nothing here is a discount trick: a free book never shows a struck-through price, a fake “sale,” or a countdown. Free means free.
