The Corner Booth

A Ten-Book Christian Young Adult Series

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

The Corner Booth is a ten-book Christian young adult series about six friends — Marcus, Jayden, Sofia, Priya, Omar, and Grace — and the corner booth at Mabel's Diner where they tell the truth, keep each other's confidences, and learn grace that starts with Christ.

New to the booth? Start with Book 1, Making the Team, and read in order. Each book follows the six friends through one more season of school, family, friendship, and faith — and each is rooted in a passage of Scripture.
