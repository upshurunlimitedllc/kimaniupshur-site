Calm in the Chaos

Anxiety, Worry, and the God Who Holds You

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

Some nights everything gets loud — the spinning mind, the worry that will not take the night off. Calm in the Chaos speaks to anxious teenagers in twelve chapters and three movements. What Worry Is names it honestly: the spinning mind, what Jesus actually said about worry, why worry is not planning, and how your body keeps score. What God Says answers it from Scripture: 'Do not be anxious about anything,' the cares you are meant to throw on the God who cares for you, and the peace that stands guard. What You Can Do gets practical: the God who never panics, praying it out loud, being still, telling someone you trust, and taking one day at a time. Scripture is quoted from the ESV, and the book closes with a personal invitation to give your life to Jesus Christ, with the Sinner's Prayer. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
