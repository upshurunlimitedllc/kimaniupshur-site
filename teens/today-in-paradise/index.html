Today in Paradise

Luke 23 and the Thief Who Had Nothing Left to Offer

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

He had no time left to prove anything and nothing in his hands — only one sentence of faith: "Lord, remember me." Jesus answered with a promise bigger than the request.

Today in Paradise: Luke 23 and the Thief Who Had Nothing Left to Offer stays close to five verses — Luke 23:39–43 — and takes them slowly across twelve chapters. Teenagers stand at the place called Calvary and hear the two men crucified beside Jesus answer Him in opposite ways. One railed and demanded rescue. The other told the truth — about himself: "we indeed justly." We receive, he said, the due reward of our deeds. And about Jesus: "this man hath done nothing amiss." Then he did the one thing that mattered: he trusted Jesus with his future. This book is plain about why that man was saved. Not by a religious performance, and not by finding a formula of words — saved by faith in Jesus Christ, confessed out loud with empty hands: "saved, not by the mechanics of calling, but by the Lord he called on." Jesus' answer is then taken at full size, word by word — today, with Me, in paradise. The final chapters hold the balance the passage itself holds: it is not too late for the one who comes at the end, and that mercy is never a reason to wait. If you have ever thought you waited too long, wandered too far, or ruined too much, this book was written with you in mind. Every chapter ends with one step to take — a Do, a Reflect, or a Pray. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a personal invitation to give your life to Jesus Christ. For teenagers ages 13-18.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
