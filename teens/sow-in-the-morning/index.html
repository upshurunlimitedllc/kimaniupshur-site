Sow in the Morning

Ecclesiastes 11 and Working Without Guarantees

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

You study, you try out, you apply, you start the project — and nobody hands you a signed promise about how any of it will turn out. Ecclesiastes 11 does not solve that problem. It teaches you how to work inside it.

Sow in the Morning: Ecclesiastes 11 and Working Without Guarantees walks through Ecclesiastes 11:1–6 verse by verse across twelve chapters. Teenagers hear the Preacher refuse both lies — that effort is a vending machine, and that uncertainty is a reason to quit — and give his counsel instead: "Cast thy bread upon the waters." Give a portion to seven, and also to eight. Stop watching the wind: "He that observeth the wind shall not sow." And take the chapter's centre at full strength: "In the morning sow thy seed, and in the evening withhold not thine hand: for thou knowest not whether shall prosper, either this or that, or whether they both shall be alike good." The book is honest about what the passage does not promise: no single effort is guaranteed to prosper, and chapter 6 of the book expressly refuses to turn Ecclesiastes 11:6 into a success formula. What it builds instead is a way of working — breadth instead of betting everything on one throw, diligence in both halves of the day, generosity risked before the outcome is visible, doing the work with your might, committing your works to God, and remembering your Creator — while the results belong to God, who sees the whole field. You do not need omniscience to begin. You need seed, a field, and a morning. Every chapter ends with one step to take — a Do, a Reflect, or a Pray. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a personal invitation to give your life to Jesus Christ. For teenagers ages 13-18.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
