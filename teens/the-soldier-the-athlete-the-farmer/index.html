The Soldier, the Athlete, the Farmer

2 Timothy 2 and Three Pictures of Endurance

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

"Thou therefore endure hardness, as a good soldier of Jesus Christ." Paul handed Timothy three ordinary workers — and one curriculum for a strong, disciplined life.

The Soldier, the Athlete, the Farmer: 2 Timothy 2 and Three Pictures of Endurance stays on 2 Timothy 2:3–7, inside Paul's prison letter to a young man he loved like a son. The letter's first chapter is the inheritance received — a sincere faith that lived first in Timothy's grandmother and his mother; its second chapter is the inheritance worked, and this book works it across twelve chapters. The soldier endures hardness, refuses to be entangled with the affairs of this life, and aims to please him who chose him. The athlete strives lawfully or loses the crown, temperate in all things. The farmer — the husbandman of the King James text — "must be first partaker of the fruits": labour before harvest. Single-mindedness, rule-keeping, and patient labour under one discipline, ending where Paul himself ends, with a course finished. The book stays on its verses; the verse just before them is noted once, for adjacency only. Each chapter ends with one small response: something to do, something to reflect on, or a prayer to pray. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a personal invitation to give your life to Jesus Christ. For teenagers ages 13-18.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
