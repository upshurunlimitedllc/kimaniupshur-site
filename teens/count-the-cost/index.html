Count the Cost

Luke 14 and the Arithmetic of Following Jesus

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

Great multitudes were already following Jesus when He turned around and made following Him sound expensive.

Count the Cost: Luke 14 and the Arithmetic of Following Jesus takes Jesus' hardest invitation at full strength across twelve chapters. Teenagers sit down — the way Jesus told the parables, sitting down first — and count with Him: the builder who counts the cost before he lays a foundation, and the tower that stands unfinished while the onlookers mock; the king who counts his ten thousand against the twenty thousand coming against him, and sends an ambassage to desire conditions of peace; and the sentence the book will not soften, standing as its thesis: "whosoever he be of you that forsaketh not all that he hath, he cannot be my disciple." The book refuses to turn His words into budgeting advice, and refuses to turn them into a scolding either: Jesus was not thinning the crowd for sport — He loved it too much to let anyone follow Him on false information. The passage is closed whole, salt and all. Every chapter ends with one step to take — a Do, a Reflect, or a Pray. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a personal invitation to give your life to Jesus Christ. For teenagers ages 13-18.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
