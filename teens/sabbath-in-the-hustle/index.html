Sabbath in the Hustle

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

Sabbath in the Hustle: Rest Is Resistance speaks to teenagers who wake up already behind — over-scheduled, over-stimulated, and over-pressured, in a culture where the grind promises fulfillment and delivers fatigue. Its Introduction, Tired Is the New Normal, presents Sabbath as God's gift of rest, 3,500 years old: not a boring rule but a weekly rebellion against the grind, built on Jesus' invitation, “Come to me… and I will give you rest,” and on the conviction, “I am not what I produce.” Twelve chapters follow in three movements the book itself names: Movement One: The Invitation (The Invitation; Rest Is Older Than the Command; The Commandment That's a Gift; Made for You), Movement Two: The Lie (The Grind Is a Lie; Martha, Put the Phone Down; Seek First), and Movement Three: The Practice (The Rest That Remains; The Shepherd's Pace; Waiting Is Strength; Designing Your Sabbath; Rest Is Resistance). Every chapter ends with Your move this week, and the book closes with a Conclusion, a Closing Prayer, and A Personal Invitation: Give Your Life to Jesus Christ, with The Sinner's Prayer. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
