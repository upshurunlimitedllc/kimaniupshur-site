Prisoners of Hope

Zechariah 9 and the King Who Comes Lowly

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

"Turn you to the strong hold, ye prisoners of hope." God gave that name to captives in a pit where there was no water — and He meant it as good news.

Prisoners of Hope: Zechariah 9 and the King Who Comes Lowly is built on Zechariah 9:9–12, spoken first to a small, tired people home from exile — free on paper, and still living like prisoners. The chapter announces a King: He comes lowly, riding upon an ass, bringing salvation and speaking peace — then it turns from the King to the captives and calls them prisoners of hope. Not people who feel optimistic about a release date, but people whose identity has been spoken by a covenant: God has acted, God has promised, and He declares He will render double for what the pit years took. Around that anchor the book gathers the Bible's own hope texts — "The LORD is my portion," the anchor of the soul, "in his word do I hope," and the charge "Be of good courage, and he shall strengthen your heart, all ye that hope in the LORD" — and closes on the chapter's own wonder: "how great is his goodness." Each chapter ends with one small response: something to do, something to reflect on, or a prayer to pray. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a personal invitation to give your life to Jesus Christ. For teenagers ages 13-18.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
