Discretion Shall Preserve Thee

Proverbs 5 and the Ways That Are Before the Eyes of the LORD

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

Proverbs 5 does not open with the danger. It opens with a request.

Discretion Shall Preserve Thee: Proverbs 5 and the Ways That Are Before the Eyes of the LORD walks through Proverbs 5 in twelve chapters, beginning upstream, where attention is trained: "My son, attend unto my wisdom, and bow thine ear to my understanding:" Teenagers are shown lips that drop as an honeycomb and an end that is bitter as wormwood, and the father's counsel: remove thy way far from her, drink waters out of thine own cistern, and remember that the ways of man are before the eyes of the LORD, and he pondereth all his goings. The warning is carried at the text's own weight — no graphic detail, and no shaming of readers who have stumbled — because discretion guards attention long before any moment of crisis, and the LORD who sees is the reason to attend. For teenagers ages 13-18.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
