Faith Comes by Hearing

Romans 10 and the Chain from Sent to Believing

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

Your faith arrived through people. Paul draws the whole supply line — and you are standing somewhere on it.

Faith Comes by Hearing: Romans 10 and the Chain from Sent to Believing walks Paul's chain link by link, in his own order, across twelve chapters: someone is sent; someone preaches; someone hears; someone believes; someone calls on the name of the Lord. Teenagers start where Paul starts — at the hidden end: "how shall they preach, except they be sent?" — and walk the questions in both directions until the whole line stands at once, down to the conclusion Paul built it for: "So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God." The book is careful about what it is: not a history of how the Bible traveled through the centuries, but transmission in a single generation — mouth to ear, person to person. And it turns the chain toward the reader at the end: you heard from someone, and if you have believed, you are a link for someone else. Every chapter ends with one step to take — a Do, a Reflect, or a Pray. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a personal invitation to give your life to Jesus Christ. For teenagers ages 13-18.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
