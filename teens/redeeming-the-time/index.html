Redeeming the Time

Ephesians 5 and Buying Back the Hours the Days Are Stealing

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

Redeeming the Time — Ephesians 5 and Buying Back the Hours the Days Are Stealing — names a feeling every teenager knows: you pick up your phone to check one thing — one message, one score, one video — and when you look up, the light outside has changed and the afternoon is gone, taken one small scroll at a time by apps and feeds built to harvest your attention. This book is not here to shame you for feeling that pull; your hours are wanted, and you are not weak for noticing. Long before the attention economy existed, Paul told the Ephesians to walk circumspectly, "redeeming the time, because the days are evil" (Ephesians 5:16). Redeem is a buying word: to pay what it costs to get something out of someone else's hands and back into your own. These chapters teach what it looks like to buy your hours back on purpose. The book closes with a personal invitation to give your life to Jesus. For teenagers ages 13-18.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
