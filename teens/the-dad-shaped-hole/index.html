The Dad-Shaped Hole

When Dad Isn't There

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

The empty chair has a shape — and this book names it gently. For teens wrestling with an absent father, it walks through the stories we tell ourselves, what the research says, the anger and the grief, and the hard chapter on forgiveness. Then it turns toward hope: the Father who is there, finding fathers, breaking the cycle, and becoming whole. Honest, tender, and never preachy.
