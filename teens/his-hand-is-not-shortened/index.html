His Hand Is Not Shortened

Isaiah 59 and the Separation Sin Makes

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

You have prayed and heard nothing back — and the silence afterward felt like a verdict.

His Hand Is Not Shortened: Isaiah 59 and the Separation Sin Makes begins by removing the wrong answer before it gives the right one: "Behold, the LORD's hand is not shortened, that it cannot save; neither his ear heavy, that it cannot hear:" God's power is not the problem, and neither is His hearing. Something else has come between, and Isaiah names it honestly, at its real weight, without flinching and without cruelty. Across twelve chapters the book carries the chapter's catalogue of sin, its truth fallen in the street, and its missing intercessor — and refuses to leave the reader standing at the barrier. The weight of the book falls on the promise: "And the Redeemer shall come to Zion, and unto them that turn from transgression in Jacob, saith the LORD." The distance is real, but it is not the whole story, and it is not the end of the story. For teenagers ages 13-18.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
