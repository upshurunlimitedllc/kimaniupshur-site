With the Contrite

Isaiah 57 and the High and Lofty One Who Dwells Low

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

"I dwell in the high and holy place, with him also that is of a contrite and humble spirit." The High and Lofty One names two addresses in one breath — eternity, and a crushed, humble heart.

With the Contrite: Isaiah 57 and the High and Lofty One Who Dwells Low is built on Isaiah 57:15–21, for the teenager who assumes God keeps company only with impressive people — polished prayers, clean records, lives that look like they belong near a holy place. Isaiah overturns the picture: God does not visit the low place occasionally, out of pity. He says He dwells there, to revive the spirit of the humble and the heart of the contrite ones. Across twelve chapters the book works through what contrite means — not crushed beyond repair — the difference between sorry and sorrow, and the one God looks toward, setting Isaiah 57 carefully beside the sacrifice of Psalm 51 and the audit of Isaiah 58. It keeps its passage whole: the way prepared, the promise that God will not contend forever, a people seen, healed, and led by God Himself, and "Peace, peace to him that is far off, and to him that is near" — and it does not soften the honest warning that closes the chapter, that there is no peace to the wicked. Each chapter ends with one small response: something to do, something to reflect on, or a prayer to pray. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a personal invitation to give your life to Jesus Christ. For teenagers ages 13-18.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
