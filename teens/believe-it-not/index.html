Believe It Not

Matthew 24 and the Christ Who Does Not Come by Rumour

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

The announcement always comes with directions — and Jesus quoted it in advance so His disciples would recognize it the moment it arrived.

Believe It Not: Matthew 24 and the Christ Who Does Not Come by Rumour stays inside Matthew 24:23–27 across twelve chapters, beginning with the warning itself: "Then if any man shall say unto you, Lo, here is Christ, or there; believe it not". Teenagers learn why Christ's return needs no leak, no link, and no insider: He is not in the desert and not in the secret chambers, and when He comes it will be as the lightning — public, unmistakable, and seen by all. The test taught is Christ's own description of His return, not media savvy; the feeds and phones are only the doorway the old rumour walks through now, and the longing it runs on is answered with watchfulness, not fear. For teenagers ages 13-18.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
