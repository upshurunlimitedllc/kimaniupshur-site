Unstoppable

Paul's Story for Teens

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

Unstoppable tells the true story of Paul for teenagers — the church's worst enemy, knocked flat by light on the Damascus road, who became its greatest missionary. Built on the triad Called. Changed. Unstoppable., it follows Saul the persecutor through his blindness and new beginning, then through midnight singing in a Philippian jail, a résumé of beatings and shipwrecks, and a final storm at sea. It closes with Paul's own secrets for a life that will not quit: not ashamed of the gospel, strong in weakness, and a six-word mission statement that even death could not defeat. Written in straight talk for teens ages 13-18, with one honest "Your move this week" practice in every chapter.
