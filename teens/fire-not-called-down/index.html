Fire Not Called Down

Luke 9 and the Village That Would Not Receive Him

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

Jesus was refused a bed for the night. His disciples asked for fire. He rebuked the disciples.

Fire Not Called Down: Luke 9 and the Village That Would Not Receive Him stays inside six verses — Luke 9:51–56 — and walks them slowly across twelve chapters. Teenagers who have been refused, mocked, or frozen out meet the question this scene actually answers: not why they did it, but what you are allowed to do back. James and John had their answer ready — “Lord, wilt thou that we command fire to come down from heaven, and consume them, even as Elias did?” — and Jesus turned the rebuke on His own injured friends: “Ye know not what manner of spirit ye are of. For the Son of man is not come to destroy men’s lives, but to save them.” The book will not soften that sentence, and it will not teach swallowing mistreatment in silence either: Jesus left the village, and the chapters teach the difference between vengeance, which is not yours, and leaving — including leaving well, and telling a trusted adult — which often is. The road runs from a Samaritan village to a school hallway and the fire in your thumb. Every chapter ends with one step to take — a Do, a Reflect, or a Pray. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a personal invitation to give your life to Jesus Christ. For teenagers ages 13-18.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
