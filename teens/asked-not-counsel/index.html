Joshua 9 preaches by what it does not say. Asked Not Counsel: Joshua 9 and the Treaty the Leaders Never Prayed About follows the Gibeonites into Israel's camp — the old sacks, the cracked wine bottles, the patched shoes, the mouldy bread, and a story polished to match every prop. Israel's leaders did almost everything right: they asked questions, they voiced a suspicion, they handled the evidence themselves. And then the narrator writes the sentence this whole book is built on: they "asked not counsel at the mouth of the LORD." One step was missing, and it was not an inspection step — it was prayer. The oath, once sworn, held — "We have sworn unto them by the LORD" — and the deceivers became "hewers of wood and drawers of water". A Cognition book about the step before the signature: checking matters, and this book teaches you to check — but evidence can be inspected and still lie when the inspectors never ask God. For teenagers ages 13-18.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
