The Last Tape

The Corner Booth, Part Two, Book 8

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

The tournament again: Eastview, Westbrook, and Northgate at the end of the road — the way it is always Northgate. But this year the tape matters as much as the games. Trey Holloway's short, honest film is being priced by the market in real time, and Marcus's own senior tape is the column his house never had to count on. Senior Night. The last home game in Andre's jacket, which no longer fits in any direction. And a final that asks both boys the same question from opposite ends: what do you do with an ending you cannot control? Book Eight of The Corner Booth: Part Two. Booth Notes: Philippians 3:13–14.
