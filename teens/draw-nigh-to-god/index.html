Draw Nigh to God

James 4 and the Way Back From Drifting

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

Drifting rarely feels like leaving. Nobody decides one morning to be far from God — the anchor comes up quietly, and one day the shore is much farther away than you thought.

Draw Nigh to God: James 4 and the Way Back From Drifting is built on five verses — James 4:6–10 — across twelve chapters, written for believers who have wandered rather than departed. Teenagers walk the way back in James's own order. It begins with grace bigger than the drift: "he giveth more grace." Then the steps: "Submit yourselves therefore to God." "Resist the devil." And the promise standing in the middle of the passage: "Draw nigh to God, and he will draw nigh to you." Cleanse your hands, purify your hearts, be double minded no more, and take sin seriously enough to mourn over it — until the passage reaches its second promise: "Humble yourselves in the sight of the Lord, and he shall lift you up." One honesty runs through the whole book: the promises are given exactly as strong as James states them — no stronger, no weaker — and that is strong enough. James does not write off the people who had drifted; he hands them directions home. If prayer has gone thin and the shore looks far, these chapters are for you: read them slowly, with your Bible open to James 4, and walk the way back one honest step at a time. Every chapter ends with one step to take — a Do, a Reflect, or a Pray. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a personal invitation to give your life to Jesus Christ. For teenagers ages 13-18.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
