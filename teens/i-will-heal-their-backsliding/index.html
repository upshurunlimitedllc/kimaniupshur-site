I Will Heal Their Backsliding

Hosea 14 and God's Free Love for a Returning People

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

"I will heal their backsliding, I will love them freely." That is God's answer, in His own voice, to a people who kept wandering away from Him.

I Will Heal Their Backsliding: Hosea 14 and God's Free Love for a Returning People is written for the teenager who has fallen into the same pattern again and has begun to wonder whether God is tired of hearing their name. The whole book stays inside Hosea 14, the last chapter of Hosea's prophecy — nine verses, twelve chapters. God invites a backsliding people to return, and He even teaches them the words to bring. The book follows the chapter in its own order: the false rescues that will not save us, the promise spoken in God's own voice, and then a garden full of pictures — dew, lily, olive, grain, and vine — for the life that grows after a return, closing with the chapter's short, wise epilogue about the road home. Hosea's earlier chapters are not retold; the reception is the point — what God says over a person who comes back to Him. Each chapter ends with one small response: something to do, something to reflect on, or a prayer to pray. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a personal invitation to give your life to Jesus Christ. For teenagers ages 13-18.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
