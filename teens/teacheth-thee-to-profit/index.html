Teacheth Thee to Profit

Isaiah 48 and the Peace That Was Missed

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

There are two verses in Isaiah 48 where God introduces Himself the way a teacher introduces himself on the first day of school — except that He names His saving work before He names His teaching work.

Teacheth Thee to Profit: Isaiah 48 and the Peace That Was Missed is built on that order: "I am the LORD thy God which teacheth thee to profit, which leadeth thee by the way that thou shouldest go." Thy Redeemer first. The Teacher second. His commandments were never a ladder for earning His love; they are the channel of the peace He prepared. Across twelve chapters the book holds both halves of the passage together: the tenderest regret in the Bible, "O that thou hadst hearkened to my commandments! then had thy peace been as a river, and thy righteousness as the waves of the sea:", and the open door — the God who says it is still speaking, still teaching, still offering the very peace that was missed. Profit here means God's teaching for His student's own good; nothing in this book promises prosperity. For teenagers ages 13-18.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
