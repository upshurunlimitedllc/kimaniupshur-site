Real Friends

Choosing Friendships That Build You

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

Real Friends — Choosing Friendships That Build You — works through twelve chapters in three movements (Friends Shape You; What Real Friends Look Like; Building Your Circle) on the book's stated triad, Choose. Sharpen. Stay.: the Elevator Test, the Friend Audit and the Slow Drift give way to Walk With the Wise, Iron Sharpens Iron, friends who tell you the truth, and David and Jonathan, and then to finding your people, being the friend you'd want, ending friendships well, and why two are better than one; each chapter starts with a Bible verse, takes about ten minutes, and ends with one 'Your move this week' step, Scripture quotations are from the ESV Bible, and the book closes with 'A PERSONAL INVITATION: GIVE YOUR LIFE TO JESUS CHRIST' and The Sinner's Prayer. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
