Into His Marvellous Light

1 Peter 2 and a People Called Out of Darkness

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

Before Peter tells scattered people who they must become, he tells them who they already are.

Into His Marvellous Light: 1 Peter 2 and a People Called Out of Darkness stays inside one declaration — 1 Peter 2:9–10 — and walks through it word by word across twelve chapters. Teenagers meet Peter's first readers as Peter met them: strangers scattered across five provinces, carrying costs for following Jesus that their neighbours never had to pay. To those readers — and to any teenager whose identity is handed to them by a feed, a group chat, or a bad season — Peter speaks God's own names over His people: "ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people," a people God has called "out of darkness into his marvellous light," people who "in time past were not a people, but are now the people of God," and who have obtained mercy. The book keeps Peter's order, because the order is the point: the status comes first — given by God, not earned by behaving differently — and the praises His people shew forth come after, as overflow. Every chapter ends with one step to take — a Do, a Reflect, or a Pray. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a personal invitation to give your life to Jesus Christ. For teenagers ages 13-18.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
