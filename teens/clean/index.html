Clean

Winning the Battle for Your Mind

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

Clean — Winning the Battle for Your Mind — is an honest guide for teens on lust and pornography: not crude, not graphic, just honest. It is organized in three movements under the triad Guard. Flee. Live., and every chapter starts with a verse from the Bible — the anchor, not decoration — and ends with 'Your move this week,' one concrete thing to do. The Battle Is Real: you are not alone, and Romans 8:1 separates no condemnation from conviction; temptation runs a four-move play out of James 1:14–15 — desire, lure, bite, decay; the 'everyone looks' claim is a lie; and lust carries real costs (Galatians 6:7–8). God's Way Out: make a covenant with your eyes after Job 31:1; flee like Joseph (1 Corinthians 6:18); run your thoughts through the Philippians 4:8 filter; and take hold of the way of escape God promises in 1 Corinthians 10:13. Walking Clean: practical guards — the phone charged outside the bedroom, filters, breaking the late-alone-bored triangle — confession and rising after a fall (1 John 1:9; Psalm 51), and telling a safe person (James 5:16). The closing frame says it plainly: clean is a direction, not a destination (2 Corinthians 5:17) — and if the struggle is bigger than a book, the book itself directs you to a trusted adult. Twelve chapters. Scripture quotations are from the ESV. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
