Taking Thoughts Captive

2 Corinthians 10 and Warfare for the Mind

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

Taking Thoughts Captive — 2 Corinthians 10 and Warfare for the Mind — starts from a fact of teenage life: your mind is not quiet ground. On any ordinary school day hundreds of thoughts move through it — what a friend meant by that look, whether you are falling behind, whether God is pleased with you — and some of them are fortified lies that have lived in you so long they feel like furniture. Paul did not call that territory weather; he called it warfare. Across twelve chapters the book works through 2 Corinthians 10: the war is not against people but against arguments, including especially the ones your own mind builds and defends, and thoughts can be measured, cast down, and taken captive into obedience to Christ. The first skill is embarrassingly simple: turn the fog into a sentence, because a sentence can be examined. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a personal invitation to give your life to Jesus Christ. For teenagers ages 13–18.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
