Things the Spirit Teacheth

1 Corinthians 2 and Wisdom That Is Not of This World

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

Two people read the same chapter of the Bible in the same room. One finds it alive — obvious, personal, almost addressed to them. The other finds it flat. Same book, same translation, same evening. What is different?

Things the Spirit Teacheth: 1 Corinthians 2 and Wisdom That Is Not of This World stays inside 1 Corinthians 2 across twelve chapters, because that chapter is the Bible's own explanation of how the Bible gets understood. Paul arrived at Corinth "not with excellency of speech or of wisdom," determined to know one thing among them — "save Jesus Christ, and him crucified" — and the faith of his hearers came to stand not in the wisdom of men but in the demonstration of the Spirit and of power. Teenagers follow the hidden wisdom the rulers of this world did not know, and then the chapter's most famous sentence, quoted whole: "Eye hath not seen, nor ear heard, neither have entered into the heart of man, the things which God hath prepared for them that love him." The very next verse is the book's heartbeat: God hath "revealed them unto us by his Spirit" — "for the Spirit searcheth all things, yea, the deep things of God." Knowing God has a source, and the source is a Teacher — who teaches in words "not in the words which man's wisdom teacheth, but which the Holy Ghost teacheth," until the chapter can end where it ends: "we have the mind of Christ." No technique for manufacturing experiences is promised here; what is offered is sturdier — an account of the Teacher, His source, His method, and His syllabus. Every chapter ends with one step to take — a Do, a Reflect, or a Pray. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a personal invitation to give your life to Jesus Christ. For teenagers ages 13-18.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
