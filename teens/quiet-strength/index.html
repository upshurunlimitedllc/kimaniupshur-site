Quiet Strength

God Uses Quiet Kids Too

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

Quiet Strength begins with a lie — that quiet is a problem to fix — and answers it with three words: Still. Strong. Sent. In twelve chapters across three movements, God's Quiet Picks, Quiet Is Not Broken, and Quiet, Deployed, it follows Moses, slow of speech and chosen to face Pharaoh; Elijah, burned out in a cave and met by God in a low whisper; Jesus, who withdrew to desolate places to pray; and David, who calmed and quieted his soul — then shows what quiet strength is: listening as a strength, words that carry weight, rest that returns instead of retreat, and power that comes not by might but by God's Spirit. Each chapter ends with one small 'Your move this week' practice, and the book closes with a Closing Prayer, 'A PERSONAL INVITATION: GIVE YOUR LIFE TO JESUS CHRIST,' and THE SINNER'S PRAYER. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
