The Second June

The Corner Booth, Part Two, Book 10

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

Three of the six graduate this time. Grace comes home with a finished freshman year in her bag and sits in the audience where five people once were as loud as five people were allowed to be — and this June it is her turn to be loud. Jayden and Omar, the two who stay, walk the last week of school as the table's remaining students. On the last Friday, the six are in the booth together — the graduate, the three new graduates, and the two who'll be back in September — and the table does what it does: it hands itself on, not with a ceremony, but with a practice. Mabel's envelope stays in its drawer. The seat stays open. September is coming for somebody. Book Ten of The Corner Booth: Part Two. Booth Notes: Psalm 145:4.
