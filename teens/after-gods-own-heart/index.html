After God's Own Heart

David's Whole Story for Teens

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Teenagers • Ages 13–18

David's story is bigger than a sling and a giant, and this book tells all of it. After God's Own Heart walks teenagers through David's whole life in twelve chapters and three movements. The Shepherd King follows the overlooked shepherd boy from the fields to the giant, the best friend, and the cave. The Long Road runs from the crown and the dancing king to the chapter David would have torn out: at ease in the palace, the fall, and Nathan's finger pointed at his chest — 'You Are the Man.' Broken and Restored closes with the broken song of repentance and the verdict God never took back: a man after His own heart. Rooted in Scripture (ESV), it shows teens that God's verdict on a life is not written by its worst chapter — and it closes with a personal invitation to give your life to Jesus Christ, with the Sinner's Prayer. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
