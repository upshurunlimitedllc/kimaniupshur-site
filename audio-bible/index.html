Where the project stands

The Audio Bible is now in production. Work has begun with the Gospel of Matthew in English and Spanish, and every language below will carry the complete Bible — 39 books of the Old Testament and 27 books of the New Testament — as production reaches it. English listeners will be able to choose from three translations: the King James Version (our primary English track), the Berean Standard Bible, and the World English Bible. Spanish is the Reina-Valera 1909.

Nothing is available to play yet. Only finished, verified recordings are published here — as each book is completed and published, its chapters appear on its language page, ready to stream or download. No book is ever listed as available before it truly is.
