The Corner Booth Part Two

The Complete Series — All Ten Novels in One Volume

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

The Corner Booth: Part Two — The Complete Series brings all ten Corner Booth: Part Two novels together in one volume for teen readers ages 13–18 — the second year of six friends and the corner booth where their lives keep intersecting, told in series order: Ask Me in August · Three Hours Away · The Read · The Polished Draft · Rosa's Chair · Still Here · For Now · The Last Tape · The Extra List · The Second June.

Each of the ten novels is complete: its own chapters, Booth Notes, and Gospel Invitation back matter. Across the second year, Omar's deferred promise comes due and his Negotiated Peace is tested by the state robotics run; Grace starts college three hours away while the table keeps her seat open on purpose; Marcus runs a senior season on everyone else's map until he finds which parts of the dream are his; Priya's polished draft asks its unanswered question through a December of deadlines; Sofia translates for the grandmother whose chair has directed the Reyes house; Jayden turns "I'm still here" from a sentence into a practice; Mabel's "for now" expires as the diner's future is priced; the last tape and Senior Night ask what you do with an ending you cannot control; Layla's extra list feeds whoever God sends in the Cedar House's fullest spring; and in the second June, three of the six graduate and the table hands itself on — stories about friendship, courage, and faith for the teenage years, where every hard moment is met honestly and every book closes with an invitation to know Jesus Christ.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
