Seasons Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Life does not move in a straight line; it moves in seasons — and Seasons, subtitled A Biblical Guide to Trusting God in Every Season of Life, argues that winter is not the absence of God's plan but part of it, doing underground work. Opening with “There is a time for everything, and a season for every activity under the heavens” (Ecclesiastes 3:1) and God's promise of seedtime and harvest (Genesis 8:22), twelve chapters begin with what seasons actually are, the God who changes times and seasons (Daniel 2:21), Jesus as Lord of every season, and the Holy Spirit as seasonal guide. The book then walks through winter, the dormant season; spring, the planting season; summer, the growing season; and autumn, the harvest season, followed by the pruning season, transitions when seasons change, and faithfulness when you feel out of season. The dormant seed, the book insists, is not dead, and the pruned branch is not punished. It ends by fixing hope on the season that never ends, where God wipes away every tear (Revelation 21:4). Chapters close with a “One Clear Step,” an appendix gives straight answers on seasons, and a closing prayer asks God to show what faithfulness looks like in the reader's present season. Scripture quotations are from the New International Version. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
