Shame

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Shame says not “I did something bad” but “I am something bad” — and Shame, subtitled A Biblical Guide to Walking Out of Hiding Into Grace, traces that voice to the garden, where Adam and Eve hid and God called, “Where are you?” (Genesis 3:9). Fourteen chapters name the difference between shame, guilt, and conviction, return to the garden to show why we hide, and proclaim no condemnation in Christ Jesus. They linger with the woman caught in adultery, to whom Jesus said, “Neither do I condemn you” (John 8:1–11), present confession as the way out of hiding, and set the Accuser against the Advocate. The book then works through shame about your past, body shame answered by being fearfully and wonderfully made (Psalm 139:14), shame in marriage and parenting, and what to do when others shame you. It closes by replacing lies with truth, finding healing in the light of community, helping others out of shame, and living unashamed — in Paul's words, “I am not ashamed of the gospel, because it is the power of God that brings salvation to everyone who believes” (Romans 1:16). Each chapter ends with an action step, an appendix gives straight answers about shame, and a closing prayer surrenders shame, past, and fear to God. Scripture quotations are from the New International Version. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
