Love

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Love — A Biblical Guide to Loving Like Jesus — begins with a correction: love is not what you feel, but what you give. It rests on two anchors: "Love is patient, love is kind" (1 Corinthians 13:4-8) and "Greater love has no one than this: to lay down one's life for one's friends" (John 15:13). Love, the book argues, is a decision and an action — and it is grown, not manufactured: "the fruit of the Spirit is love" (Galatians 5:22). Fourteen chapters work that definition through real life: God is love, the Father's heart; Jesus laying down His life; love as the Spirit's fruit; and the two great commandments — love God first, and love your neighbor as yourself. The testing places follow: marriage, children and household, and enemies; then love in deed and in truth, forgiveness, speaking the truth in love, enduring when love is hard, and the final claim that love never fails. Each chapter ends with one action step. An appendix, "Straight Answers on Love," answers fourteen honest questions — whether love must be felt, whether enemies can really be loved, and how love differs from enabling. A conclusion built on the book's tagline — "Receive. Abide. Give." — and a closing prayer complete the volume. Scripture quotations are taken from the New International Version, unless otherwise noted. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
