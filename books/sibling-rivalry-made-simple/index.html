Sibling Rivalry Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Your children fight. Every day. The backseat, the kitchen, bedtime — the same arguments at fraying volume. And underneath your frustration sits a quieter fear: what if today's bickering becomes the silence at Thanksgiving twenty years from now? Sibling Rivalry Made Simple is the practical, Scripture-first guide to ending the cycle. In twelve short chapters, Kimani Upshur gives you a three-word method: Calm. Coach. Connect. Calm the moment — your voice is the thermostat. Coach the skills — apology, forgiveness, kind speech, none of them automatic. Connect your children as teammates instead of rivals. You'll learn to stop fights without yelling, why fairness doesn't mean sameness, how comparison poisons sibling love (and what to say instead), how to coach real apologies and daily forgiveness, how to write a peace treaty your kids help build, and how to discipline in a way that trains instead of breaks. Every chapter ends with reflection questions, action steps, and a prayer. Warm, direct, honest about how hard this is. No perfect-parent fantasies — just God's wisdom for your real house, and a long view worth building: siblings who are friends for life.
