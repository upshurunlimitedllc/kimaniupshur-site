Arkansas Penal Code Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Nobody reads the criminal code for fun. Most people meet it for the first time in the worst possible way. Arkansas Penal Code Made Simple translates the real Arkansas Code Annotated, Title 5 (Criminal Offenses) into plain language across twelve short chapters: the felony and misdemeanor grading system, homicide, assault and battery, sex offenses, property crimes, drug offenses, weapons law, fraud, DUI, legal defenses, and your rights from arrest through court. Clear, direct, and practical — education, not legal advice.
