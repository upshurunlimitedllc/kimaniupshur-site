Sanctification

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Faith & Doctrine

Sanctification — subtitled A Biblical Guide to Growing Holy Every Day — explains what it means to be made holy, day by day. Its opening is honest: you do not become holy in one prayer; you become holy in one thousand small surrenders, day after day, as the Holy Spirit does His daily work in you. Fourteen chapters work through the doctrine in order. Sanctification is God's will for the believer, not an optional extra, and it begins with being set apart: in position, the believer was made holy the day he was born again, and in practice he is becoming, in his daily conduct, what he already is in Christ. The book then follows the agents and the battlegrounds of that work — the Spirit who sanctifies you, the Word that washes, and the blood that set you apart — before turning to the life it produces: dying daily and putting off the old self, walking by the Spirit, presenting your body as a living sacrifice, and holiness carried into thought and heart, into speech, and into relationships. A chapter for when you stumble treats conviction, confession, and cleansing, and the book closes with finishing well and a closing prayer. Each of the fourteen chapters carries a short section headed Practice:, one concrete step to take into the week. All Scripture quotations are from the King James Version of the Bible unless otherwise noted. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
