Conscience Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Your conscience is God's alarm system. Is yours awake? Before anyone taught you right from wrong, something inside you already knew. That inner voice — the one that stings when you lie and rests when you tell the truth — is your conscience: the witness God installed in your soul. But consciences are not fixed. They can be wounded, dulled, even seared silent. And they can be awakened, cleansed, retrained, and guarded. In Conscience Made Simple, Kimani Upshur, M.Ed., walks you through the conscience as a faculty — what it is, how it was formed in your childhood, what wounds and sears it, and how God restores it. With twelve short, Scripture-first chapters, you will learn: What your conscience is and how it works as God's inner alarm How your childhood tuned it — and how God can retune it What a clear conscience feels like (nothing hidden, nothing owed) How to heal a wounded conscience and wake a seared one How the blood of Christ purifies your conscience from dead works How to retrain your alarm by renewing your mind with Scripture How to honor a weak conscience — yours and your brother's How to guard your conscience daily, by grace, for life Built around three unforgettable words — Awaken. Retrain. Guard. — each chapter includes a Scripture passage, warm pastoral teaching, three reflection questions, three practical application steps, and a written prayer. Read one chapter a day for twelve days, and finish with a conscience that rings true.
