Integrity

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Integrity, subtitled "A Biblical Guide to Wholeness When No One Is Watching," defines integrity as wholeness — the same person in private that you are in public, an integer rather than a fraction — and builds the whole book on two anchor passages, Proverbs 10:9 ("Whoever walks in integrity walks securely") and Psalm 15:1-2. Its introduction states plainly that the book is built on the God of the Bible — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — and that wholeness is the Holy Spirit's work in a surrendered heart, not moralism, reputation management, or willpower. Fourteen chapters walk the places integrity is tested: what it actually is, the blameless walk, wholeness in secret, Daniel's undivided heart, Joseph's unseen test, money, speech, the cost of doing right, the divided heart, repentance and restoration when you fail, integrity at home and at work, guarding the heart, and the reward of the upright. Every chapter opens with Scripture and ends with one action step; an appendix, "Straight Answers on Integrity," answers fourteen honest questions, and the book closes with a conclusion and a closing prayer. Scripture quotations are taken from the New International Version, as the book itself states. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
