Farlight: The Last Beacon

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

FictionFiction

Caleb Voss has kept the lamp at Farlight Station for nine years — the last crewed lighthouse at the edge of charted space. Colony convoys thread the Rift by his beam, steering by a light they can never reach. Then his long-range ear catches a pulse from beyond the last buoy: exact, metronomic, repeating every 47.3 seconds along the lane the colony ship Covenant took fifty years ago, before she vanished with four thousand souls asleep aboard. Edge Command’s orders are plain: log it, do not transmit, do not investigate, and let no vessel cross the edge. Caleb crosses anyway, riding out with freighter pilot Mara Quill into uncharted dark, past wreck fields and jackal raiders, toward whatever is knocking. With Psalm 27 on his lips — “The Lord is my light and my salvation” — Book One of the Beacon Saga begins.
