Unity

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Unity begins where Jesus prayed on the night before the cross: “that all of them may be one, Father, just as you are in me and I am in you” (John 17:20-21). Kimani Upshur, M.Ed. presents unity not as a church-growth strategy, uniformity, or watering down truth, but as oneness — different people held together by a bond stronger than their differences, summed up in Ephesians 4:4: one body, one Spirit. Because the Holy Spirit already creates that unity, believers are called to keep it (Ephesians 4:3), not manufacture it by avoidance, control, or leaving. Fourteen chapters, each opening with Scripture and ending with one action step, walk through the rooms of the united life: the prayer behind it all, one body and one Spirit, the church that shared everything, why unity is not uniformity, disagreement, peacemaking, gossip as unity's quiet enemy, forgiveness, every tribe and tongue, unity in the home and in the local church, when to part ways, and what the watching world will know. An appendix answers fourteen honest questions about unity, and the book closes with a prayer to the Father, through Jesus Christ, by the Holy Spirit. Along the way, the book returns to the pattern of oneness itself: a husband and wife becoming one flesh in Genesis 2:24, and the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit — three distinct Persons, one God — perfectly united from before time began. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
