The Armor of God Study Workbook

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

You are already in the fight. This workbook makes sure you don't fight it unarmed. The Armor of God Study Workbook is a guided companion to The Armor of God Made Simple by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed. Across 7 guided sessions through Ephesians 6:10–18, you'll take up each piece of the armor one at a time — the Belt of Truth, the Breastplate of Righteousness, Feet Fitted with the Gospel of Peace, the Shield of Faith, the Helmet of Salvation and the Sword of the Spirit — and finish praying in the Spirit, standing your ground. Each session follows the same simple rhythm: Read the passage slowly, Observe what it actually says, Apply it specifically and honestly to your life, and Pray it back to God. Every session ends with a callout to pray that session's armor piece in the free Armor of God Prayer app. Work at your own pace — one session a day, or one a week. There are no grades here. ---
