Made Simple: Bible Stories & Parables — The Complete Box Set

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Made Simple: Bible Stories & Parables — The Complete Box Set gathers ten complete books on the Bible's best-known stories and Jesus' parables into one volume: The Red Sea, The Talents, The Lion's Den, The Feeding of the Five Thousand, The Good Samaritan, The Road to Emmaus, The Exodus, The Prodigal Son, The Crucifixion, and The Fiery Furnace. The set's own front page states its terms plainly: ten books, one price. Together they move from Israel trapped between Pharaoh and the sea, through Daniel's courage and his friends in the furnace, to the miracles and parables of Jesus — stewardship in the Talents, neighbour-love in the Good Samaritan, the Father who runs to the returning son — and on to the cross and the risen Jesus opening the Scriptures on the Emmaus road. The constituent books share a deliberate pattern, stated in The Red Sea's 'How to Use This Book': read the opening Scripture first, then the teaching, then answer the reflection questions honestly, do the application steps this week, and close with the prayer at the end of the chapter. Scripture comes first in every chapter, and the teaching stays rooted in the passage in front of the reader. Read one book a day or work through the whole set in order — either way, each story is given its full biblical telling before its lesson is drawn. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
