Mystery Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Mystery starts from the question underneath every hard passage: when you read the Bible and hit a truth too big for your mind, what do you do — pretend you understand it, or panic that real believers should have it figured out? The book's answer is that in Scripture a mystery is not a riddle with no answer. It is a truth hidden for a time and now made known by God Himself, anchored in Ephesians 1:9 and Colossians 1:26. Its fourteen chapters work through what the Bible means by mystery, the mystery hidden for ages, Christ in you the hope of glory, the mystery of godliness, and why God hides things, then turn to the Spirit searching the deep things, why Jesus spoke in parables, the mystery of Israel and the church, and the mysteries of suffering, the incarnation, the resurrection body, and Christ's return. Scripture quotations are taken from the New International Version, as the book itself states. The book closes with chapters on mysteries we must not pretend to solve and walking by faith where you cannot see, a conclusion on the God who hides and reveals, an appendix of straight answers on mystery, and a closing prayer. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
