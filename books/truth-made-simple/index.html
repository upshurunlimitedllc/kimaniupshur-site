Truth

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Truth carries the subtitle “Anchored in God's Word When the World Says Truth Is Relative.” Its introduction begins with Pontius Pilate asking “What is truth?” in John 18:38 — with the Answer standing in the room — and sets the book's aim: not debate scripts or louder Christians, but anchored ones. Eleven chapters build that anchor. “Sanctify Them in the Truth” rests on John 17:17, that God's Word is truth; “I Am the Truth” on Jesus' claim in John 14:6; and “The Sum of Your Word Is Truth” on Psalm 119:160, urging readers to take the whole counsel of God rather than cherry-pick. The book then follows the Spirit of truth, answers the age of relativism and its “my truth” language, and proclaims that the truth will set you free, holding John 8:32 together with John 8:36 — if the Son sets you free, you will be free indeed. Later chapters face the truth about you, truth that divides and truth that unites, speaking the truth in love, and testing the spirits, before closing with walking in truth as a daily rhythm. Its Appendix of Questions and Answers also distinguishes first-tier truths, such as the Trinity and the deity of Christ, from matters where sincere believers differ, and a Closing Prayer completes the book. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
