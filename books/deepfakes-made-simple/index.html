Deepfakes Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Tech & AI

The phone rings, and it's your grandson's voice — scared, urgent, needing money fast. It sounds exactly like him.

It isn't him.

For the first time in history, seeing is no longer believing and hearing is no longer knowing. Deepfakes — AI-generated faces and voices — are fueling a new wave of scams, misinformation, and impersonation. And nobody gave you the new rules. Until now.

Deepfakes Made Simple is the plain-language safety manual for the synthetic-media era. In twelve short chapters, Kimani Upshur gives you a plain picture of what deepfakes are (no jargon, no fear-mongering), a map of where you'll meet them, and a three-word reflex for the suspicious moment: *Pause. Verify. Protect.

You'll learn the four classic scam plays and exactly what to say when one calls you, what to do if someone fakes you*, how to be the calm one in the family group chat, and how to build a family verification plan — code words, call-back habits, and all — in a single conversation. Honest about what detection can and can't do. Practical down to the scripts.

Warm, direct, and calm: this is the book for anyone with a phone, a parent, or a grandchild. The fakes are getting better. Your habits can get better faster.
