Perimenopause Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Perimenopause Made Simple — A Complete Guide to the Physical, Mental, and Life Changes of Perimenopause — begins where its readers begin: something is changing. Your sleep is different, your mood is different, your body is doing things it has never done before — and you are not imagining it. The book explains what perimenopause actually is, what it does to every dimension of life, and what can be done about it. It is an informational and educational guide, not medical advice: the author is not a physician, and the book directs readers to qualified healthcare providers for their own care. Twelve chapters cover the transition whole: what perimenopause is, the physical changes, and brain, mood, and mental health; sleep as the hidden central issue; relationships, sexuality, and intimacy; perimenopause at work; nutrition and physical health; and management options from lifestyle measures to hormone therapy. A chapter speaks directly to partners, family, and friends, another to navigating the healthcare system, and the closing chapters look at post-menopause and sum up what everyone should know — including that perimenopause is a natural biological transition, not a disorder, and that its symptoms are real. It serves women going through the transition, anyone who loves someone going through it, and anyone simply trying to understand it — replacing confusion and silence with vocabulary, understanding, and practical next steps. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
