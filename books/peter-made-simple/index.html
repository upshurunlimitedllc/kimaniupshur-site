Peter

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Bible People & Stories

Peter is the apostle for people who do not have it all together. This book tells Simon Peter's story straight from the Bible — the fisherman called by the sea, the disciple who walked on water and sank, who confessed Christ and then denied Him three times by a fire, who wept bitterly, was restored at dawn by the risen Jesus, and who preached at Pentecost and saw thousands come into the kingdom in a single day. Its arc is its tagline: stumbling, restored, bold. Built to be read slowly, one chapter a day, Peter moves through ten chapters — leaving the nets, walking on water, the rock confession, the rebuke, the sword in the garden, the denial, the run to the tomb, 'Feed My Sheep', the Pentecost sermon, and the prison that could not hold him. Each chapter follows the same pattern: Scripture first, teaching rooted in the text, reflection questions, application steps, and a prayer to the Father, through Jesus Christ, by the Holy Spirit. For everyday believers who have failed publicly and need to know that failure is not final when Jesus has the last word, Peter offers a testimony of grace — restoration not by willpower, but by the Holy Spirit. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
