Made Simple: Prayer & Worship — The Complete Box Set

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Box Sets & CollectionsBox Set

Made Simple: Prayer & Worship — The Complete Box Set gathers ten complete Made Simple books about meeting with God in one volume. Retreat opens the set with Jesus' own pattern of withdrawal — "Jesus withdrew — so should you" — and The Lord's Supper follows with the bread, the cup, and the promise until He comes. Worship is a biblical guide to giving God the glory He deserves, and Meditation a biblical guide to pondering God's Word until it feeds you. Silence teaches holy stillness — "Be still and know" — and Intercession teaches praying for others with power, standing in the gap. Communion returns to the Lord's Supper with its own call to remember, examine, and rejoice, and Baptism is a biblical guide to being buried and raised with Christ: one water, one moment, one new life. Pilgrimage treats the journey itself as worship, and Solitude closes the set as a biblical guide to withdrawing with God — alone, still, renewed. Together the ten books move through the practices of the devotional life: withdrawing, eating at the Lord's table, singing and bowing in worship, meditating on Scripture, keeping silence, praying for others, and being alone with God on purpose. Scripture quotations in most of the constituent books are from the New International Version. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
