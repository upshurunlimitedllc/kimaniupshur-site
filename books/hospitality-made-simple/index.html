Hospitality

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Hospitality by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed. — published as Hospitality Made Simple, 'A Biblical Guide to Opening Your Home and Heart' — begins with a simple conviction: somebody near you is eating alone tonight, and God's answer is not a program but your front door. The book separates hospitality from entertaining — entertaining performs to impress, hospitality welcomes to love — and grounds the practice in Romans 12:13, 'Practice hospitality,' from the Greek philoxenia, love of strangers. It presents hospitality as the gospel in furniture form: the Father welcomed us while we were still sinners (Romans 5:8), Jesus set His table for outcasts, and the Holy Spirit makes the believer's home an embassy of the kingdom. Fourteen chapters cover welcoming strangers (Hebrews 13:2), hospitality without grumbling, the table Jesus set, the great banquet, and Abraham's tent, then the practical barriers: a small home, a tight budget, a busy schedule, and perfectionism, plus the lonely, the widow, and the fatherless, the holiday table, and hospitality as a household calling. Each chapter ends with one clear action step for this week. An appendix answers fifteen straight questions, and the book closes with a conclusion and a prayer. Scripture quotations are from the New International Version. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
