Priorities Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Personal Growth

Priorities Made Simple: How to Get Clear on What Matters — and Build Your Life Around It opens with a diagnosis: you already know what matters most, and you keep running out of time for it — and that is not a time problem or an effort problem but a clarity problem. Most people are not lazy, the book argues; they are unclear, so they default to what is immediate, visible, or requested by others, and end their weeks busy while the work, relationships, and goals that matter most stay crowded out. In an introduction and twelve chapters, the book defines what a priority actually is, explains why most people's priorities are wrong — inherited and unexamined rather than chosen — and shows how to get clear on what actually matters and arrange it in a priority hierarchy. It then applies that clarity at work and in personal life, teaches the art of saying no, and connects priorities to time allocation, to the moments when everything feels urgent, and to relationships. The final chapters cover reviewing and resetting priorities and living a priority-driven life. Its foundation is stated plainly: your time is yours to direct, the choice of what matters most is yours to make, and closing the gap between what you value and what your life reflects is the work. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
