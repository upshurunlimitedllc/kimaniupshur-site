AI Bundle

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Box Sets & Collections

This bundle is built around the AI Made Simple omnibus edition — the complete AI framework — bound together with the thinking library that teaches you to use it well. Eighteen complete Made Simple volumes in one: Artificial Intelligence, Thinking, Clear Thinking, Critical Thinking, Cognitive Biases, First Principles Thinking, Decision Making, Mental Models, Systems Thinking, Signal vs Noise, Complexity, Feedback Loops, Strategy, Strategic Thinking, Competition, Leadership, Focus, and Time. The AI volumes run the full arc — what artificial intelligence actually is and how it learns, the tools and everyday applications, the art of prompting, building effective workflows, industry-specific applications, troubleshooting, and the risks, ethics, and future of AI, including what it means for your career. The thinking volumes train the other half of the work: clear judgment, honest reasoning, sound decisions, and strategy that holds up. One shelf, one purchase: the machines, explained — and the mind trained to stay in charge of them. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
