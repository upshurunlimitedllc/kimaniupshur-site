AI & Future Tech — The Box Set

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Box Sets & Collections

Seven complete books. One volume. Artificial intelligence, understood clearly and used well.

AI & Future Tech — The Box Set collects seven complete Made Simple guides to the technology that is reshaping work, learning, money, and power — each book in full, unabridged, in a single ebook.

Inside the set:

  • AI Tools Made Simple — What AI tools actually are (and are not), and how to use them for writing, productivity, and learning — with a clear-eyed chapter on their risks and limits.
  • AI and Education Made Simple — How teachers, students, and parents can use AI to learn faster and teach better, including academic integrity and a practical action plan.
  • Artificial Intelligence Made Simple — How AI works, where it came from, how machine learning learns, and what it means for business, work, and society.
  • Crypto and AI Made Simple — How artificial intelligence is changing the way we understand digital investing — systems first, hype filtered, no promises of wealth.
  • Make Money With AI Made Simple — The largest book in the set: eighteen chapters on AI writing, design, freelancing, content, consulting, digital products, and automation — grounded, step-by-step, and honest about the risks.
  • Prompt Engineering Made Simple — How to communicate with AI to get better answers, better writing, and better results — the skill every other book in this set depends on.
  • Who Controls The Future — Artificial intelligence, incentives, and the forces shaping tomorrow: who holds power in the AI era, and why clear thinking matters more than ever.

Every book follows the Made Simple approach: calm, clear, and grounded in how the technology actually behaves — no hype, no jargon for its own sake, and human judgment at the center throughout.

A note on this edition: This off-Amazon edition contains seven of the nine titles in the Amazon box set. AI and Money Made Simple and Synthetic Media Made Simple are Amazon-exclusive (KDP Select) titles and are available inside the Amazon edition of this box set.
