Nyxari: The Awakening

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

FictionFiction

Jonah Reyes, twenty-seven, was born in Tidehold's lower tiers and has never seen a sky — only Saturn, banded and gold above the ice of Enceladus. An ice-geologist on the Kestrel rig, he is helping sink a Deep Well toward the Heart Vent field when his array catches a paired pulse in the Vesper Field: forty-one seconds apart, and closing. It answers his calibration ping. It is, Vera Okafor confirms, a heartbeat. Helios, which sells forty thousand people their heat, wants tonnage and quiet. But beneath twenty kilometres of ice lies a black ocean, an ancient Warden, and the still-cities of the Nyxari — who have begun, at last, to listen back. Book One of the Saturn Cycle, written from a Christian worldview.
