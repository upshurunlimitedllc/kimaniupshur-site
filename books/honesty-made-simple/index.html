Honesty

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Honesty by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed. — published as Honesty Made Simple, 'A Biblical Guide to Truth-Telling That Sets You Free' — is built on the sentence Jesus spoke first: 'Then you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free' (John 8:32), paired with its requirement: 'put off falsehood and speak truthfully to your neighbor' (Ephesians 4:25). Biblical honesty, the book argues, is bigger than the absence of lying — it is a life with nothing hidden, built on truth that is firm enough to lean on (emet in Hebrew, aletheia, 'un-concealed', in Greek). Fourteen chapters move from what honesty actually is and the God who cannot lie, through why we lie — fear, pride, shame, control, and habit — and the myth of the little lie, into being honest with yourself, honest with God, honest in your home and at work, speaking the truth in love, integrity under pressure, and the freedom of a clear conscience. Each chapter closes with a headed 'Your Action Step', an appendix of 'Straight Answers on Honesty' takes up fourteen hard questions — Rahab, white lies, exaggeration, flattery — and the book ends with a closing prayer. Its tagline is the destination: True. Clear. Free. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
