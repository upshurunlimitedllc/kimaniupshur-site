Masks Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Masks Made Simple — A Biblical Guide to Being Known by God — begins with the exhaustion of performing: the Sunday-morning smile, the "I'm fine" that hides falling apart, and the masks worn for fear, shame, and approval. Its opening hook, "You're Exhausted from Performing — Here's How to Be Real," and its introduction, "The Fig Leaves," root that hiding in Genesis 3:8, while the description in the package recalls that God looks on the heart (1 Samuel 16:7; Psalm 139:1). Across ten chapters, the book names the masks Christians wear, looks at what Jesus did with mask-wearers, and moves through confession as the mask remover, vulnerability, masks in marriage, authentic community, and the healthy line between privacy and pretense. It closes with "Fully Known," "Becoming — From Performing to Being," a conclusion titled "Stop Performing. Start Becoming," and a Closing Prayer. Masks Made Simple is written for believers who want to be known rather than applauded — fully known by God, who already sees through every mask (Hebrews 4:13), and honestly known by safe people in real community. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
