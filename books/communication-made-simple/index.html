Communication Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Family & Relationships

Communication Made Simple starts where most communication actually breaks down: you said what you meant, and they heard something different. The meeting ends without agreement, the email is forwarded out of context, and the conversation meant to solve a problem creates two more. Kimani Upshur argues that clarity does not happen automatically — it is produced by specific skills that can be learned, practiced, and made habitual. The book first shows why most communication fails and what clear communication actually is, then teaches you to think clearly before you speak, structure your message, and say more with less. You will learn bottom line up front, the signal-context-impact structure, and how to signpost a complex message before you deliver it. Listening gets its own chapter as a real skill, not waiting for your turn to talk, alongside avoiding misunderstandings by closing the loop and confirming that what was heard matches what was meant. Later chapters cover communicating under pressure, the common mistakes that quietly undo good intentions, and improving communication daily through deliberate practice. The final chapter applies everything to real life: work, difficult conversations, and leadership, where consistency between words and action makes communication credible. Each of the eleven chapters ends with Key Takeaways you can put to work the same day. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
