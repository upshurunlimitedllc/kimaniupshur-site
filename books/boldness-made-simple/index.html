Boldness Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Boldness is a biblical guide to courage that comes from God, built on Proverbs 28:1 — the righteous are as bold as a lion — with its Scripture quotations from the New International Version. Its fourteen chapters move from what boldness really means and why it is not personality but the Holy Spirit's work, through the God who makes cowards into lions and Jesus, the Bold One, to praying for boldness and speaking the truth of the Word. Later chapters apply that boldness at work, in witness, and in prayer, and then under pressure: bold when opposed, bold to do the hard right thing, and bold steps of obedience. The book closes with boldness joined to humility — the lion who kneels — and the bold finish. Each chapter opens with Scripture and ends with one action step, and an appendix gives straight answers to fourteen questions about boldness, followed by a conclusion and a closing prayer. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
