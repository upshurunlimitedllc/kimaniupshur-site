Bible People & Stories

Hosanna in the highest — and He wept over the city. This Scripture-first guide walks the road of Christ's Triumphal Entry scene by scene: the five-hundred-year-old prophecy of Zechariah 9:9, the borrowed colt and the seven words that released it, the garments spread in the way, the meaning of "Hosanna to the Son of David," the crowds who praised Him, the Pharisees He answered with "the stones would cry out," the tears He shed over Jerusalem, the temple He cleansed that same day, the gap between the King they expected and the King they got, how to recognize your own day of visitation, why crowds turn from Hosanna to crucify — and seven daily practices for living the Triumphal Entry every day of your life.
