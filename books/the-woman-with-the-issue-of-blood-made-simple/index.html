The Woman with the Issue of Blood Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Bible People & Stories

Twelve years of bleeding — healed by one touch of His garment. This Bible-first guide follows the unnamed woman of Mark 5:25-34 and Luke 8:43-48: her twelve-year affliction, her ruined finances, her ceremonial uncleanness under Leviticus 15, her trembling touch of the fringe of Jesus' garment, and His tender word — "Daughter, thy faith hath made thee whole; go in peace." Fourteen chapters cover the anatomy of saving faith, the healing in His wings (Malachi 4:2), the story inside the story with Jairus's daughter, and the pattern for every believer: Touched. Healed. Whole. — plus fourteen reflection questions for personal study or group discussion.
