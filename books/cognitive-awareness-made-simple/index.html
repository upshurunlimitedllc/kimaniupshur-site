Cognitive Awareness Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Cognitive Awareness, by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed., is a biblical guide to noticing the mind you never see — the unexamined thoughts that shape your choices, your mood, and your words in the background. Its twelve chapters move from the mind you never see and the watchtower of the heart, through naming the thought, examining your ways, and the heart that lies, to thinking about these things, watching and praying, and keeping a close watch, then renewal from the inside, the stayed mind, open hands and a quiet mind, and day-and-night meditation. Each chapter ends with 'Your move this week,' Scripture quotations are from the English Standard Version (ESV), and the book closes with a closing prayer. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
