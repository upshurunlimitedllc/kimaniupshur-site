Meal Prep Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Health & Wellness

Meal Prep Made Simple starts from a simple diagnosis: meal prep does not fail because cooking is hard, but because most people never build a repeatable system connecting planning, shopping, cooking, and storage into something that survives a genuinely busy week. This practical guide builds that system step by step. It shows you how to plan a week's meals before you shop, build a genuinely efficient grocery list, and choose the meal prep method that actually fits your life, kitchen, and preferences rather than forcing a single universal routine. From there it covers batch cooking basics, storage with containers, labeling, and food safety, and how to reheat food without ruining it. Because repetition is where most attempts die, separate chapters tackle keeping meals from getting boring, meal prepping on a budget, and cooking for dietary restrictions and preferences. The closing chapters turn the pieces into a repeatable weekly system, name the common mistakes that derail meal prep — the ambitious Sunday that produces one good week and then burnout — and show how to sustain the practice long-term. The promise is modest and honest: cook once, eat well all week, and keep doing it. Every method in the book is tested against one standard — whether it survives an actually busy week, with real time limits, real kitchens, and real appetites, rather than collapsing after a single heroic effort. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
