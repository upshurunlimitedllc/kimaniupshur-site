The Bible

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Bible Study & Scripture

The Bible — A Biblical Guide to God's Living Word — is a book about appetite, written for Christians who own a Bible but rarely open it, and it makes its claim in one sentence: the Bible is God's living Word — breathed out by Him, centered on Christ, opened to us by the Holy Spirit — and it was given to be read, believed, and obeyed. Across fourteen chapters it begins with its foundations: God-breathed (2 Timothy 3:16–17), alive and active (Hebrews 4:12), Christ at the center (Luke 24), and a lamp for your feet (Psalm 119:105) — sixty-six books, one Author, one story, one Hero, in two Testaments that tell one story. Then it turns practical: Read — a simple way in; Believed — trusting what it says; Obeyed — doing what it says (James 1:22). The closing chapters take up the Spirit who illumines (John 16), praying the Scripture, studying without a seminary, the Word in hard times, reading together, and keeping a daily rhythm. Every chapter opens with a passage of Scripture and ends with one clear action step, counted and real. An appendix answers beginners' questions — where to start: Mark, then Philippians, then Genesis, then Romans — and a Closing Prayer ends the book. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
