Victory

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Victory starts from a settled outcome: you are not fighting for victory — you are fighting from it. Kimani Upshur, M.Ed. anchors that claim in two passages returned to throughout the book: “But thanks be to God! He gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ” (1 Corinthians 15:57) and “we are more than conquerors through him who loved us” (Romans 8:37). Victory is therefore not positive thinking, trying harder, or denial that the battle is real; it is a gift God gives, won by Jesus Christ through His death and resurrection, and applied to daily life by the Holy Spirit, to the glory of the Father. Fourteen chapters, each opening with Scripture and ending with one action step, define what victory actually is and explore the finished work, the battle belonging to the Lord, victory over sin's power, fear, and the past, the armor of the victor, winning the mind, victory in suffering, standing when it doesn't feel like victory, the triumph of forgiveness, living from victory rather than for it, and the final victory to come. An appendix gives straight answers on victory, and the book closes with a prayer. Lived this way, obedience becomes gratitude rather than payment, prayer becomes conversation with the Father rather than a performance, and service becomes overflow — the response of a believer standing on finished ground on an ordinary Monday morning, not only on Sunday. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
