The Damascus Road

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

The Damascus Road — Blinded. Broken. Sent. — tells the story of Saul of Tarsus from the Acts 9 road to the apostle Paul. Its Introduction names him plainly: The Man Who Came to Kill. Ten chapters follow the account in order: Saul breathing out threatenings (Acts 9:1–2); the light above the brightness of the sun (Acts 9:3–4); "Who art thou, Lord?" (Acts 9:5); three days of darkness (Acts 9:9); Ananias, the obedient nobody (Acts 9:17); straightway he preached Christ (Acts 9:20); let down in a basket (Acts 9:25); Barnabas and the fearful church (Acts 9:27); from Saul to Paul (Acts 13:9); and the chief of sinners as the pattern of God's mercy (1 Timothy 1:15). The Conclusion turns the story on the reader: Your Own Damascus Road. The book is built to be read one chapter a day — ten chapters, ten days — and every chapter keeps the same steady pattern: Scripture first, then the teaching, Reflection questions, Application steps, and a Prayer. It works in pairs and small groups as well: read the chapter during the week, then gather and share. A Closing Prayer ends the book. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
