Purpose Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Purpose is a biblical guide to living the life you were made for. It starts with the question under every question — why am I here, what am I for — and argues that the world's answers all fail the same way: your purpose is whatever makes you happy, find yourself, follow your dreams. Those slogans have nothing to say about suffering or duty. The Bible's answer starts in a different place: not with you, but with God. In the book's one-sentence thesis, you were made on purpose, by Someone, for Someone — purpose is not something you invent but something you receive: a design placed in you by the Father, purchased for you by the Son, and activated in you by the Holy Spirit. Fourteen chapters build that design out of Scripture: created as God's handiwork (Ephesians 2:10), beginning at the end — fear God and keep his commandments (Ecclesiastes 12:13) — known before birth, and standing under a purpose that prevails over our plans (Proverbs 19:21). The book then works through being called in Christ, saved to serve, living for God's glory, loving God and people, work as worship, seasons and assignments, bearing fruit, purpose when it feels lost, and finishing the race. A closing prayer follows, and an appendix answers the questions people ask about purpose — including that your foundational purpose never changes, while your assignments change with the seasons. It serves anyone asking what their days are adding up to — the teenager choosing a major, the mother up at 3 a.m., the retiree, the executive who got the thing and is still hungry — and offers discovery instead of self-invention. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
