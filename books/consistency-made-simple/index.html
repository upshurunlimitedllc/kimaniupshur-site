Consistency Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Personal GrowthPsychology

Consistency Made Simple starts from a liberating diagnosis: most people already know what they should be doing. They have the goals, the plans, and moments of real clarity — and then they stop, not because they are lazy or do not care, but because consistency is not a personality trait. It is a design problem, and this book shows you how to solve it. Across twelve chapters it redefines consistency, explains why people fail to stay consistent, and dismantles the discipline myth, arguing that motivation is a temporary emotional state designed to fade rather than a reliable engine for results. The alternative is design: shaping identity and self-image, choosing systems over motivation, eliminating friction, and building repeatable habits that survive ordinary days. When failure and slippage come — and they will — the book teaches you to deal with them without letting one bad day unravel everything, and to design your environment so the right action becomes the easier action. It extends the same thinking to consistency in thinking and decisions, and to long-term compounding, where small actions repeated faithfully outgrow bursts of intensity. The conclusion, Build It and It Will Hold, lands the promise of the subtitle: build discipline, eliminate excuses, and become the type of person who follows through every time. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
