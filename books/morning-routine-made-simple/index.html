Morning Routine Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Personal Growth

Morning Routine Made Simple starts from a familiar failure: the borrowed morning. Wake at 5 AM, meditate, journal, exercise, cold plunge — a cinematic routine designed for someone else's life, without your shift schedule, your kids, your commute, or your energy levels. You tried to wear it for a week, concluded you are just not a morning person, and gave up. This book makes a different claim: your morning is not a performance to optimize but the first domino — design the first hour, and the day follows. The difference between a morning that happens to you and one that starts on purpose is not discipline or personality; it is the first fifteen minutes, deliberately owned. Kimani Upshur, M.Ed., who works shifts himself, builds the routine from small blocks anchored to habits you already have, with a bad-day minimum that survives real life. Twelve chapters move from why morning routines fail to the minimum viable morning, anchors and habit stacking, and the first fifteen minutes, then through morning prayer and the Word, planning the day in five minutes, and energy rather than just time. Separate chapters serve shift workers and parents, relocate decisions to the night before, troubleshoot broken mornings, and add a weekly reset, ending with becoming a morning person slowly. Nothing here assumes you wake at dawn; a morning that starts at 2 PM still counts. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
