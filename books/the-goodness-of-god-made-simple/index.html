The Goodness of God

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Faith & Doctrine

"Taste and see that the LORD is good." The Goodness of God opens with what its Introduction calls the most important question you will ever answer: what kind of God do you actually believe in? — because, the book argues, your picture of His goodness sets the ceiling on your faith and your prayer. Twelve chapters build the answer from Scripture: goodness tasted and seen in Psalm 34:8; good from the beginning, when God looked at all He had made; His good character, proclaiming Himself abounding in steadfast love in Exodus 34; every good gift descending from the Father in James 1:17; the goodness that leads home, since Romans 2:4 says it is meant to lead us to repentance; the love that corrects; goodness in the valley and behind the scenes; goodness with a face, in Jesus of Nazareth, who went about doing good; goodness growing in the reader; goodness and mercy following all the days of a life, as Psalm 23:6 promises; goodness that never ends, for His mercy endures forever; and living in His goodness now. A Closing Prayer ends the book, thanking God that He is good — in its words, not sometimes, not partially, not conditionally. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
