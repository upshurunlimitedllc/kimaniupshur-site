Enemies Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Enemies, by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed., is a biblical guide to loving your enemies and overcoming evil with good. It takes up what its Hook calls one of the hardest commands Jesus gave — 'Love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you' (Matthew 5:44) — and works through what that command does and does not mean. Fourteen chapters cover how God loved us first, while we were still sinners (Romans 5:8), knowing the real enemy, praying for persecutors, refusing to repay evil for evil, and overcoming evil with good (Romans 12:21). The Joseph pattern, biblical boundaries, forgiveness before any apology, blessing in action, enemies within the household, and Stephen's prayer each receive a chapter. Each chapter ends with a labelled 'Your Action Step', an appendix answers fourteen questions about loving enemies, and the book closes with a prayer. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
