by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Money & Finance

Personal Finance is a plain-language guide to the real machinery of money, written from the Christian worldview the book itself declares: money is stewardship — you do not own it, you manage it (Matthew 25:14–30), and honest scales matter in every dealing (Proverbs 11:1). It begins with the steward mindset and a one-page money map that shows exactly where you stand, then builds the system in order: a three-number budget of Give, Save, Live; a war plan for killing debt; an emergency fund that absorbs shocks; and credit scores demystified. From there it puts banking and bills on autopilot, explains the insurance you actually need, and lays out unglamorous investing that works for normal people over decades. Later chapters cover buying cars and houses without the traps, taxes translated into plain English, and protecting your money from scams, theft, and the freeze. Everything is gathered into a one-page lifetime money plan in Chapter 13, with a worksheet appendix to complete as you go. Every chapter ends with one actionable step, so the system is built by doing, not just reading. The author states plainly that he is not a financial advisor and that the book is education, not personal advice. Start where you are, work the steps in order, and let the machinery carry what willpower cannot. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
