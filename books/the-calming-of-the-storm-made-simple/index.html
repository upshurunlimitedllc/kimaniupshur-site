The Calming of the Storm

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

The Calming of the Storm — Trusting the Prince of Peace When the Winds of Life Rage — anchors itself in one night on the Sea of Galilee: Mark 4:35–41, with the parallel accounts in Matthew 8 and Luke 8 beside it. Across twelve chapters it follows the story verse by verse: Jesus' promise, "Let us pass over unto the other side"; the great storm that arose; the sleeping Savior; the disciples' cry, "Master, carest thou not that we perish?"; the rebuke, "Peace, be still"; the great calm; and His questions after it — "Why are ye so fearful?" and "What manner of man is this?" The book then widens the lens: storms throughout the Bible, the storms inside us, and what it means to bring your storm to Jesus, until the crossing ends at the other side. Written for stormy seasons and meant to be read in order, one chapter at a time, it begins each chapter with Scripture and ends it with simple practical steps for today — no seminary degree needed, only an open Bible and an open heart. Its frame is Trinitarian throughout: the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, and victory by the Spirit's power, never by willpower alone. A titled Closing Prayer ends the book. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
