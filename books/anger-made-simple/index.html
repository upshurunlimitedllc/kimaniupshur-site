Anger

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Anger isn't the problem. What you do with it is.

Anger is one of the strongest signals your mind and body produce: something matters, a boundary has been crossed, something feels unjust. The trouble is what usually happens next: the explosion that damages relationships you value, or the suppression that stores the pressure until it leaks out as resentment, stress, and blowups at the smallest triggers. There is a better way — and the Bible points to it. Scripture neither shames you for feeling anger nor hands it the keys: "Be angry and do not sin." In Anger, Kimani Upshur, M.Ed. brings the practical tools of anger management together with the wisdom of God's Word, so you can hear the signal, examine it honestly, and answer it well.

You will learn what happens in your body when anger rises — the amygdala hijack that outruns your rational mind — and why the alarm itself is designed, not defective. You will see what righteous anger looks like in Jesus: aimed at evil, paired with grief, and spent on healing. You will map your anger profile and triggers, uncover the stories, desires, and vulnerable emotions — hurt, fear, shame, grief — beneath the heat, and learn the physiological reset: the pause, the breath, the prayer that turns reaction into response. Then the book goes deeper than technique: self-control, in Scripture, is fruit the Holy Spirit grows, and repair after conflict is repentance — confession, responsibility, forgiveness. You will learn to speak in ways that build up, to release vengeance to God, and to channel anger's energy the way He does: slow to anger, abounding in steadfast love.

Written from a Christian worldview, Anger is for anyone tired of being ruled by their temper or by their silence — and for the reader whose anger has already done damage. The way back is honest: confession, amends, help, and the promise that there is no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus. You cannot choose whether anger comes. You can choose — by God's grace — what it makes of you. Take control.

— Anger: The Practical Guide to Anger Management, Emotional Regulation, and Taking Back Control · Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.
