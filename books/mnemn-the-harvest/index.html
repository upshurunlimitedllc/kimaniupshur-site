Mnemn: The Harvest

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

FictionFiction

Three weeks after a Mnemn scout passed through the Saturn system, Jonah Reyes is still in Vey-Anahr, the still-city suspended in the black ocean beneath Enceladus's ice — and the blank resonance key the Warden grew for him will not stop singing the scout's chord. It gets louder every day. Above him, six hundred refugees crowd the St. Brendan, fled from burning Tidehold, while Doss carries a dead man's unopened letter he is afraid to need. Around the city, the warrior Vhar shapes a war-song: we will not be harvested singing. The Mnemn are the Rememberers, built under the law THAT NOTHING BE LOST — and a scout is only ever the beginning. Book Two of the Saturn Cycle, written from a Christian worldview.
