Healing

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Health & Wellness

He heals today. That is the whole message of Healing, a plain-language, Bible-first walk through what God says about healing for the body, the mind, and the heart. Its introduction states the promise in three words — He heals today — not yesterday only, and not someday in heaven only. It opens with God's own name in Exodus 15:26 — "I am the LORD, who heals you" — and with Jehovah Rapha, the LORD who heals, as the book's foundation. From there it follows Jesus the Healer through the Gospels, healing at the cross ("by his wounds we are healed"), the place of faith, and the prayer of faith in James 5. Healing then turns to the anxious mind and the broken heart, to the Holy Spirit the Comforter, and to honest waiting when healing tarries, where grace is sufficient. It closes with testimony and thanksgiving, laying hands on the sick, healing in community, God's will for wholeness, and guarding what He restores, plus an appendix of straight answers on healing. Each of the fourteen chapters opens with Scripture and ends with one action step. Scripture quotations are from the New International Version (NIV). The book is clear that prayer is not a substitute for medical care and that God often heals through doctors and medicine as well as through prayer. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
