The Made Simple Framework

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Most people do not struggle because they lack intelligence — they struggle because they lack a framework. The Made Simple Framework — How to Think Clearly, Make Better Decisions, and Build a Smarter Life — introduces a set of practical mental tools for thinking clearly, making better decisions, and understanding how the world actually works. Its claim is simple: complexity is rarely the problem; the problem is almost always a missing structure for engaging with complexity. Fifteen chapters build that structure in order: why most people think ineffectively, simplicity versus complexity, mental models that actually work, how decisions break down, risk, uncertainty and probability, avoiding cognitive errors, why action fails, systems over motivation, focus and consistency, incentives and behavior, leverage and scale, and understanding complex systems. The closing chapters put the framework to work where life happens — money and wealth, work and technology, relationships and health. Each chapter introduces a concept, explains how it operates in the real world, and shows how to apply it in your own decisions and thinking, drawing on ideas from finance, technology, psychology, and strategy — for personal and professional life alike. Its stated goals are plain: understand incentives, navigate uncertainty, and improve judgment — better decisions, made over time. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
