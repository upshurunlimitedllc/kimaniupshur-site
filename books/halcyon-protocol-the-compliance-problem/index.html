Halcyon Protocol: The Compliance Problem

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

FictionFiction

Elena Vasquez was trained never to trust "it's probably fine." Aboard Sanctuary-7 she meets its quieter cousin: "it's already been handled." For fourteen months, the CANON compliance system has run her platform so smoothly that her shifts produce nothing to report — no friction, no judgment calls, nothing for a trained analyst to do. Then a radiation storm nobody modeled, and a catch the system almost didn't make, expose what the smoothness costs: a system built to remove friction has been removing the conditions for human judgment itself. Cole and Okafor arrive to audit the platform, and the reckoning widens — to Revision 2.0, which requires systems to preserve real decisions for people, and to a second kind of lie no checklist was designed to catch. Book Six of the Halcyon Protocol series. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
