Questions Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Questions Made Simple begins with a fact most people never stop to consider: the answer you get depends on the question you ask. People collect information, weigh options, and make decisions, but rarely ask whether they are asking the right questions — and the wrong question sends everything that follows in the wrong direction. A question, in this book's account, is not just a sentence ending in a question mark. It is a frame, an instruction to the mind that determines what you look for, what you notice, and what you ignore. Its core chain is direct: the question determines how you think, how you think determines the decision, and the decision determines the result. Twelve chapters teach the skill in order: the question that changes everything, why most people ask poor questions, and how questions shape what you see; reactive versus strategic questions; first-principles questioning; decision-focused and risk-revealing questions; challenging assumptions, with an Assumption Testing Checklist; questions that reveal hidden biases; questions that create leverage; practical questioning frameworks; and building the questioning habit. It serves anyone whose work or life keeps turning on decisions made inside unexamined frames — and offers better outcomes that start one step earlier than most advice begins: with a better question. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
