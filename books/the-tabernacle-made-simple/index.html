The Tabernacle

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Bible People & Stories

God commanded Moses to build Him a dwelling — and gave the pattern on the mountain. The Tabernacle, by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed., walks that pattern in twelve chapters: the one gate, the brazen altar of sacrifice, the laver of washing, the candlestick, the table of shewbread, the altar of incense, the veil torn from top to bottom, the Holy of Holies, the ark and the mercy seat where law is covered by blood, the priests clothed for glory and beauty, and the glory filling the house — closing with the truth that you are the temple now. The passages quoted follow the King James tradition. A closing prayer ends the book. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
