Infertility

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Infertility by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed. — published as Infertility Made Simple, 'A Biblical Guide to Hope While You Wait for a Child' — speaks to the ache of the empty nursery: hope that rises every month, then grief with no funeral and no grave. It makes no cruel promises — no baby is guaranteed to anyone who prays hard enough — and offers instead a way to walk through the waiting with God rather than away from Him. Three biblical couples anchor the journey: Hannah, who poured out her soul and was heard (1 Samuel 1); Sarah and Abraham, who waited when it seemed impossible; and Elizabeth and Zechariah, whose story carries the words 'God remembers' (Luke 1). Fourteen chapters move from what infertility actually is to unseen grief, faith that is not denial, the strain infertility puts on a marriage, and a prayerful framework for medical decisions — the book is explicit that it offers biblical encouragement, not medical advice. Later chapters take up adoption as God's own way of making family, praying specific prayers, letting people in, and an identity bigger than a diagnosis. Each chapter closes with a headed 'Your action step'; an appendix answers hard questions directly ('Does God punish people with infertility? No'), and the book ends with a Conclusion and a Trinitarian Closing Prayer. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
