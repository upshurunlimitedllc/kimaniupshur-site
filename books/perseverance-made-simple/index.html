Perseverance

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Almost everybody quits at least once — a diet, a degree, a marriage, a calling. Perseverance asks what keeps a person going when every reason to stop is louder than every reason to continue, and it answers from the Bible rather than from willpower: perseverance is not you holding on to God, but God holding on to you while you keep moving your feet. Its two anchor passages run through the whole book — running with perseverance the race marked out for you (Hebrews 12:1) and the crown of life promised to the one who perseveres under trial (James 1:12). Fourteen chapters take up the real places perseverance is tested: the cloud of witnesses, fixing your eyes on Jesus, wanting to quit, suffering that builds stamina, Job's loss and Joseph's long road, waiting without wandering, praying through, the Father's discipline, the small things, and finishing, as Paul did, at heaven's finish line. A closing prayer ends the book. Written for tired runners — steadfast, unshaken, and unfinished — Perseverance offers fresh-start grace instead of shame, and a biblical way to keep going when quitting feels easier. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
