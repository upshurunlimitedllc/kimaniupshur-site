OODA Loop Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

OODA Loop, by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed., carries the subtitle How to Observe, Orient, Decide, and Act Faster Than Changing Conditions, and it takes its cycle from a fighter pilot who studied why some pilots consistently won dogfights against opponents flying technically superior aircraft: the winner was not outfighting the loser but out-cycling them, moving through observation, orientation, decision, and action fast enough that the opponent was always responding to a situation that no longer existed. Its thirteen chapters explain what the OODA loop actually is, then observe — gathering genuine, unfiltered information — orient, where the real work happens, decide without waiting for certainty, and act and learn from the result, then why speed beats perfection in a changing environment, getting inside an opponent's or rival's loop, avoiding analysis paralysis at the orient stage, using OODA for individual and for team and organizational decisions, common mistakes that break the loop, adapting OODA to genuinely slow-moving situations, and building it into an ongoing habit of mind. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
