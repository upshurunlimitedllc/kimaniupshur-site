Vane

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

FictionFiction

Before the first morning, every living thing had a note, and all the notes together were the Song — a song not about the singers, but about the Son. Vane, the first singer, could hold the highest note without strain, and the Composer entrusted him with the most precious thing in the dawn: the Seed-Song, an unfinished lullaby meant for a people not yet awake. His charge was simple — carry it, and when the time comes, sow it. Never finish it. But a gift that outshines every other voice is its own temptation, and beside him his brother SINGS-ALONE sings the steady note Vane has always stood on. What happens when the brightest voice reaches for the one note that was never his? The prequel to the Saturn Cycle, written from a Christian worldview.
