Beloved

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Between Ecclesiastes and Isaiah, God placed a love poem in the Bible — eight chapters of longing, delight, and devotion between a man and a woman, unashamed and unabashed. Beloved walks you through the entire Song of Solomon in plain, warm language: from first longing to the wedding feast, through the night of absence, to love as strong as death. In Beloved, Kimani Upshur, M.Ed., guides you through all eight chapters with three movements — Seek. Savor. Stay. — showing you how love begins in pursuit, learns delight within God's design, and endures as a sealed, guarded, unquenchable vow. You will discover why desire is holy and not shameful, what the poem's refrain — do not awaken love before its time — means for singles and married readers alike, how to behold your spouse with delight instead of evaluation, and why the Song's only mention of God comes at its summit: the very flame of the LORD. Every chapter is grounded in Scripture — verified against the biblical text, honest about the hard passages (the refrain's call to patience, the night of absence, the poem's most physical imagery, and the questions of authorship are faced directly, not dodged), and clear about where interpretation begins. The church's historic reading of the Song as picturing Christ and his church is named honestly as an interpretive layer — never forced, never hidden. No invented stories. No avoided difficulties. Just the Song as it was written: the greatest of songs, about the strongest of loves. Whether you are married, single, waiting, or remembering, this book will show you why God was not embarrassed to put a love poem in his holy book — and what it means for the way you love.
