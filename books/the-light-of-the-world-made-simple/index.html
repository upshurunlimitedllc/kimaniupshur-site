The Light of the World

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Jesus said it in a crowded room: “I am the light of the world.” The Light of the World — A Biblical Guide to Walking in the Light of Christ — opens that claim and follows it through the whole Bible. It begins where the Introduction begins — with the Word who lights every room. The life that is the light. The Father of Lights, who does not change. The Spirit who shines within. And the book's steady confidence, from John 1 onward: darkness never wins. Twelve chapters walk the subject in order — the claim that shook the darkness, the life that is the light, the way you walk, your lamp and your path, cities on hills, shining where you are, night seasons when the light feels far away, the lampstands and the coming light of Revelation's new creation, and keeping the lamp burning to the end. The book calls itself devotional but practical, and it reads that way: an action step in every chapter applies the light to ordinary life. A “Straight Answers on the Light” section takes up the reader's questions directly, and a closing prayer ends the book. Scripture quotations are from the New International Version (NIV). Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
