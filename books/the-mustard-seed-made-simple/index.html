The Mustard Seed

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

You do not need big faith. You need real faith, even if it is small. The Mustard Seed — Small Faith, Great God, and the Kingdom That Grows — builds on the three places Jesus taught it: the parable of the seed that becomes a tree where birds lodge in its branches, the promise that faith as a mustard seed moves mountains, and the word that uproots the mulberry tree. Matthew 13, Matthew 17, and Luke 17 supply the foundation, and every chapter returns to them. Fourteen chapters follow the seed through its whole life: the kingdom is like a mustard seed; the least of all seeds; when it is grown; why the disciples could not; seed sown, not stored; the soil of the heart; hidden growth and the Holy Spirit; mustard-seed prayers; small obedience and great harvest; and the mustard-seed life, from seed to legacy. The book's own pattern runs through every chapter — Scripture first, then reflection, then application, closing with a “Put It to Work” — and it ends with a closing prayer and an appendix of thirty days of mustard-seed prayers. Scripture quotations are from the King James Version (KJV). Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
