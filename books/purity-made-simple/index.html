Purity

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Purity is a biblical guide to a clean heart in a compromised world. It begins where Jesus begins: 'Blessed are the pure in heart, for they will see God' (Matthew 5:8). Purity, in this book, is not mainly about missing out; it is about seeing — seeing God more clearly than the compromised ever will. Its second anchor is David's prayer, 'Create in me a pure heart, O God' (Psalm 51:10): a pure heart is something only God can make, so the reader brings the surrender and God does the creating. Purity is also wider than sex — it covers thoughts, eyes, words, and motives — and the book is explicit that it is not a shame campaign and not a willpower program. Scripture quotations are taken from the New International Version, with some passages paraphrased for clarity and marked as such. Fourteen chapters work through what purity actually is, the battle for the mind, guarding the eyes, God's design for sex, fleeing temptation on the Joseph pattern, purity in dating and in marriage, and purity of speech. Later chapters take up failure and cleansing, accountability, the Spirit's power rather than willpower, and living set apart every day. Each chapter ends with one action step. An appendix answers fourteen honest questions about purity, and a conclusion and closing prayer complete the book. It serves believers tired of hiding compromises — including readers who have already failed and need to hear that failure is not disqualification, because hearts can be cleansed, renewed, and set apart. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
