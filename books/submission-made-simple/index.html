Submission

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Submission reclaims a word many people fear. In Submission, Kimani Upshur, M.Ed., shows from Scripture that submission is not being less, being silenced, or being pushed around: it is being ordered — arranged under the right authority, the way the Greek word hypotasso pictures soldiers in formation, ready rather than weak. The anchor is James 4:7: "Submit yourselves, then, to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you." Submission begins with God first, and only then flows outward into every other relationship. Across fourteen chapters, the book works through what submission really means, how Jesus Himself showed the way, and strength under control, then applies yielding to marriage, children and parents, the church, citizens and rulers, and the workplace. It is equally clear about limits: a chapter on when submission stops guards readers where human authority asks disobedience to God, with honest counsel for anyone in an abusive or crisis situation to seek help. Further chapters confront pride, the enemy inside, practice the quiet art of yielding and forbearance and forgiveness, and end where Scripture ends the subject — humble yourself, and God will lift you up. An appendix, Fourteen Honest Questions About Submission, and a closing prayer complete the book. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
