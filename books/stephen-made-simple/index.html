Stephen

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Bible People & Stories

Stephen is a Bible-first walk through the life and death of Stephen, the first martyr of the church, as told in Acts chapters 6 and 7. Kimani Upshur, M.Ed., opens with the man with the shining face — "his face as it had been the face of an angel" (Acts 6:15, KJV) — and follows him from being chosen to serve tables to his dying prayer of forgiveness. The twelve chapters move in order: chosen for service, full of faith and of the Holy Ghost, and given wisdom no one could resist; then the long sermon Stephen preached before the council, worked through in stages — Abraham and the promise, Joseph suffering before glory, Moses rejected by his own, and the tabernacle, the temple, and the Most High. The closing chapters reach the rebuke of stiff-necked hearts, heaven opened with the Son of Man standing, the stones, and the crown and the seed, with Saul standing at his feet. Each chapter begins with Scripture, followed by teaching, reflection questions, application steps, and a prayer, in the book's stated pattern of Scripture first, then reflection, then application, then prayer. A conclusion, Live Like Stephen, gathers the call to be faithful, forgiving, and crowned, and a Closing Prayer ends the book. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
