Patience Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Patience asks the question underneath every long season of life: what are you waiting for right now — a diagnosis, a prodigal, a promise you are sure God made and just as sure He has forgotten — and how is the waiting changing you? Kimani Upshur's answer is that waiting is not wasted. In Scripture, patience is long-suffering: faith stretched across time, the strength to keep loving, trusting, and obeying without quitting or turning bitter — and it is never manufactured by trying harder, because patience is fruit the Spirit grows (Galatians 5:22), not a personality trait you either have or lack. Fourteen chapters follow the Bible's own school of waiting: what patience actually is, the God who is patient with you, Abraham after waiting patiently, Joseph in the pit and the prison, Job and the perseverance James points to, the Psalms of waiting, Isaiah on strength renewed, and Jesus, the patient Savior. The book then turns practical — patience with people, patience in suffering, patience for the harvest, patience with your temper — and ends looking forward to the Lord's return. A conclusion, an appendix of straight answers on patience, and a closing prayer complete the book. Scripture quotations are from the New International Version, with some passages paraphrased for clarity and marked as such. For anyone in a season that will not hurry, Patience offers the Bible's own reassurance: the wait is not empty, and it is not evidence that God has forgotten you. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
