Why You Still Feel Stuck

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Personal Growth

You’re trying. You’re thinking. Planning. Waiting for clarity. And still… nothing changes. You’re not lazy. You’re stuck. Why You Still Feel Stuck breaks down what’s really happening when you can’t move forward—no matter how much you think about it. This isn’t about motivation. It’s about understanding the hidden patterns that keep you in the same place. Inside, you’ll learn: • Why overthinking creates the illusion of progress • How decision avoidance keeps you stuck without realizing it • Why clarity doesn’t come before action—it comes from it • The mental loops that trap you in hesitation • Simple ways to break the cycle and start moving again Most people wait until they feel ready. That moment never comes. Progress starts when you stop waiting and start moving. Break the loop. Make the move. Change the direction.
