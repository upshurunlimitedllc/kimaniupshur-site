The Queen of Sheba Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Bible People & Stories

She came with hard questions — and left breathless. This Scripture-first guide follows the Queen of Sheba through 1 Kings 10:1-13 and 2 Chronicles 9:1-12: the report she heard concerning the name of the LORD, the great train of camels, gold, spices, and precious stones, the hard questions Solomon answered every one of, the wisdom and the house that left her with no more spirit in her, her confession — "Blessed be the LORD thy God" — and the exchange of gifts that favored her beyond all asking. Every chapter points to Jesus Christ, the greater Solomon: the Lord and Savior, the Son of God and the Son of Man, through whom we come to the Father by the Holy Spirit. Sought. Tested. Amazed.
