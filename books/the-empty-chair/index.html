The Empty Chair

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

FictionFiction

Day 1251. At the home table, seven keepers sit, the lamp is lit before the bread, and the eighth plate is set — and empty, as it has been for two books. The Empty Chair follows Renata Cole, Desmond Okafor, Idara, Miriam, Corinne Achebe and the keepers of the Table as their daily discipline of honest written examination — unpriced faults heard, priced, and kept in the ledger — is tested by a quiet mystery: two years of lamp oil, flour, paper and supplies, drawn in a keeper's measures, traced to Far Cinder, a relay platform with no table at all, where a keeper has kept the discipline entirely alone. Cole and Idara do not summon her home. They carry the table to her. Book Two of the Halcyon Protocol series. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
