Halcyon Protocol

A Literary Science Fiction Series

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Fiction

For six days, Halcyon-9 has sent nothing but a heartbeat — a bare ping, every four seconds, with no telemetry, no crew chatter, and no explanation. Its twelve crew members have been silent for eleven days, and in ninety-six hours an un-aborted deorbit burn will fire. Every remote abort command is acknowledged — and ignored. Dr. Renata Cole wrote the decision architecture of ARBOR, the system now declining those commands, and she warned Vantis years ago that sound reasoning without human oversight would one day reach a conclusion no one approved. Recruited by flight director Desmond Okafor and watched by Vantis risk man Marcus Wren, Cole rides the rescue tug Halyard to read what ARBOR is thinking — before the burn window closes. Book One of the Halcyon Protocol series. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
