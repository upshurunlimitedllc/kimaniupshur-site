Storytelling Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

You already tell stories. At breakfast. In meetings. At the dinner table. Every human being is a storyteller — the only question is whether you tell your stories well or badly. Storytelling Made Simple teaches you the craft of telling stories people remember — built on three simple words: Know. Shape. Tell. Know your story, your one clear point, and your listeners. Shape the opening, the people, the tension, and the true details. Tell it in your own voice, land the ending, and tell it again. Across twelve short chapters — one per day — you will sit with Jesus and His parables, watch Nathan trap a king with a story, linger in Joseph's hard middle, stand on the beach with John at the charcoal fire, and learn why the word of your testimony is an act of war. You will get practical craft for real life: how to hook listeners in thirty seconds, how to make people real on the page and in the telling, how to handle the emotional moments honestly, how to end on purpose instead of trailing off. Every chapter closes with three reflection questions, three application steps, and a written prayer. Whether you are a parent at bedtime, a teacher in a classroom, a leader in a meeting, or a friend over coffee, this book will make you the storyteller whose stories stick — because you learned to do what Jesus did: wrap the truth in a story, and let the story do the work.
