Turning Points Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Turning Points carries the subtitle “A Biblical Guide to Life's Defining Decisions” and its motto, “Every turn is a doorway.” Its introduction, opening with Joel 3:14 and the valley of decision, declares the method plainly: everything here is Bible-first, with no secular decision-making system dressed in verses, and everything is Trinitarian — the Father who orders our steps, Jesus Christ the way, the truth, and the life, and the Holy Spirit who guides into all truth. Each chapter opens with Scripture and ends with one action step. Fourteen chapters walk the great turns of Scripture: Abram's “Go” in Genesis 12, the turn that renames Jacob at the Jabbok, Moses at the burning bush, Ruth's turn that stays in Ruth 1:16, the turn of repentance in David's Psalm 51, the turn in the whisper, the turn of the denier Peter, the Damascus turn of Saul, and the prodigal's turn home. Along the way it teaches that a believer rarely gets the full map before moving: faith at a turning point is certainty about the Guide, not certainty about the road. The book then turns practical — how to know which way, choose life, the daily turn, and the last great turning — and closes with an Appendix, “Straight Answers to Honest Questions,” a conclusion, “Walk Through the Doorway,” and a Closing Prayer. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
