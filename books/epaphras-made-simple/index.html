Epaphras Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Bible People & Stories

"Always laboring fervently in prayers." This Scripture-first guide follows Epaphras through Colossians 1:7-8, Colossians 4:12-13, and Philemon 23: the beloved fellow servant who carried the gospel of Jesus Christ to Colossae, the faithful minister who taught the churches of the Lycus valley, and the intercessor who wrestled in prayer for the saints that they might stand perfect and complete in all the will of God — even from a prison cell in Rome. Every chapter points to Jesus Christ, the Lord and Savior through whom we pray, and to the ministry of intercession every believer is called to. Taught. Prayed. Labored.
