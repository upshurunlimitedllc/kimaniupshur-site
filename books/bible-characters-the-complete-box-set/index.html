Made Simple: Bible Characters — The Complete Box Set

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Box Sets & CollectionsBox Set

Made Simple: Bible Characters — The Complete Box Set gathers ten complete books on the men and women of Scripture into one volume: Samuel, Mary Magdalene, Ruth, Nehemiah, Solomon, Moses, Peter, David, Paul, and Joseph. Each book stands on its own and follows its figure through the whole biblical arc — Samuel hearing God's voice as a boy at Shiloh and anointing Israel's first kings; Ruth's loyalty, Nehemiah's rebuilding, and Moses leading Israel out of Egypt; David and Solomon in triumph and failure; Peter sinking, being restored, and preaching at Pentecost; Paul, and Joseph, whose story runs from the pit to Pharaoh's palace. The set's own front page states its terms plainly: ten books, one price. Read straight through, the collection traces a single thread — ordinary, flawed people met by a faithful God, whose stories still teach what walking with Him looks like today: hearing and obeying His voice, praying in desperation and in praise, repenting, leading, enduring, and trusting His promises when the outcome is not yet visible. Each constituent book keeps the focus on the Scripture text itself, in the order the Bible tells it, with the teaching drawn out of the passage rather than imposed on it. The Samuel book, for example, opens in the tabernacle at Shiloh, with the boy running three times to Eli before learning to answer, 'Speak, Lord, for your servant hears.' Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
