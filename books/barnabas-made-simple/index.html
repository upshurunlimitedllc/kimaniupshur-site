Barnabas

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Bible People & Stories

Barnabas follows Joseph of Cyprus, the Levite the apostles renamed son of encouragement, through the book of Acts and shows how his giving, vouching, and raising up of others shaped the early church. Fourteen chapters trace his sold field in Acts 4:36-37, his courage in vouching for Saul in Acts 9:26-28, his sending to Antioch and his fetching Saul from Tarsus in Acts 11, the famine relief, and his being set apart by the Holy Spirit in Acts 13:1-3. The book continues through the first missionary journey in Acts 13 and 14, the Jerusalem Council in Acts 15:1-35, the sharp disagreement in Acts 15:36-41, his lapse in Galatians 2:13, and the restored Mark of 2 Timothy 4:11, closing with a call to live as an encourager today and a closing prayer. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
