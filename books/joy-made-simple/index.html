Joy

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Joy, by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed., published as Joy Made Simple, 'A Biblical Guide to Unshakable Joy,' is written for the believer whose joy has leaked out somewhere between the diagnosis, the bills, and the long wait: someone who believes, prays, and goes to church, yet no longer shines. Its opening note sets the terms of the journey — read one chapter a day across fourteen days, with your Bible open, praying as you read — and its introduction names the enemy plainly: the joy thieves are everywhere, and they are losing. The fourteen chapters build the case in order. Joy is not happiness, and the joy of the Lord is strength; joy is fruit — grown by the Holy Spirit as the believer abides, not manufactured by willpower or positive thinking. The book then locates joy where Scripture locates it: rejoicing in the Lord (Philippians 4:4), counting trials as joy (James 1:2), in Jesus, the Man of Sorrows who had joy, in the Holy Spirit as the gardener of joy, and in prayer, worship, thanksgiving, a guarded thought life, giving, and serving. A chapter on the joy killers — grumbling, comparison, and bitterness — and a chapter on joy in the waiting lead to the close, 'Living From Joy — The Unshakable Life'. An appendix, 'Straight Answers on Joy', takes up honest questions such as whether it is wrong for a Christian to feel sad, and the book ends with a Conclusion, 'The Well Never Runs Dry', and a Closing Prayer. Scripture quotations are taken from the New International Version, with some passages paraphrased for clarity and marked as such. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
