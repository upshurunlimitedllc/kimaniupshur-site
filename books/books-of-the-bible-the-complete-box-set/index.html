Made Simple: Books of the Bible — The Complete Box Set

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Made Simple: Books of the Bible — The Complete Box Set gathers ten complete books, each walking through a book or section of the Bible, into one volume: Proverbs, James, Ecclesiastes, Psalms, Romans, The Minor Prophets, Jude, The Book of Acts, Genesis, and Hebrews. The set's own front page states its terms plainly: ten books, one price. The collection ranges across both Testaments — Genesis and the beginnings of creation, fall, and flood; the wisdom of Proverbs and Solomon's honest search in Ecclesiastes; the one hundred fifty Psalms; the twelve Minor Prophets from Hosea through Malachi, whose first chapter here is 'The God Who Warns Before He Strikes'; Paul's letter to the Romans and the birth of the church in Acts; James on faith that works, Jude's twenty-five verses urging readers to contend for the faith, and Hebrews on the supremacy of Christ. Each constituent treats its biblical book on its own terms — its context, its argument, and what it asks of the reader — with the passage itself kept at the centre, as in the Proverbs constituent, which opens with wisdom calling aloud in the public square (Proverbs 1:20-21). Scripture quotations in the set are from the New International Version. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
