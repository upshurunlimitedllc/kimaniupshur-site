Password Security Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Your bank. Your email. Your photos. Your entire digital life — guarded by a password you chose in thirty seconds five years ago. Most of us lock our front doors and leave our accounts wide open. Password Security Made Simple closes the gap with three words anyone can live by: Lock. Separate. Verify. Lock every account with a real password and every device with a real barrier. Separate your keys so one stolen password can never open every door. Verify everything — with two-factor authentication, scam-proof instincts, and the habit of testing every suspicious message before you trust it. Across twelve short chapters — one per day — you will walk through your digital house door by door: building strong passphrases you can actually remember, ending password reuse forever, spotting phishing emails and scam calls before they cost you, turning on two-factor authentication without the fear, choosing a password manager that remembers everything for you, preparing backup codes and recovery plans for the day you get locked out, sharing access wisely with family, and securing the phone that holds it all. Every chapter gives you three reflection questions, three concrete action steps you can do today, and a written prayer. No jargon. No fear-mongering. No guarantees — just honest, practical habits that make you a hard target, written in plain language for non-technical readers. Whether you are protecting your own accounts or helping an aging parent protect theirs, this book will leave your digital life locked, separated, verified — and your heart at peace.
