The Completion

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

FictionFiction

Two years after the answering went out from Sareen's teaching water, every song is supposed to be going home. Mira's saying is chalked outside the song-vault — every song returns, every singer is sent — but Mira herself, now eight, carries a song that will not resolve, a world's song has stopped altogether, and the council aboard the St. Brendan debates for three hours what a stopped song means. Sael, the fleet's great teacher, is dimming like an evening. Jonah Reyes, long since released by Sael, faces one more sailing: out to the silent world, with the Warden riding his shoulder and the dawn coming up from below. The finale of the Saturn Cycle, written from a Christian worldview. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
