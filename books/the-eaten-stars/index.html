THE EATEN STARS

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

FictionFiction

Three months after the Scattering, 107,411 souls gather in the dark around Vesper, the last free star. Two suns are already gone — devoured by the Hollow, a vast darkness that eats stars and comes for the light. Ader burned bright for nine days before it went out, taking four million souls. The Hegemony holds the home systems, and all that remains of free Christendom is running dark aboard the Pilgrim Fleet. Captain-Pastor Jonah Hale of the Covenant’s Lamp orders one defiant night: the Vesper Vigil, when 38,000 people fill the Lantern dome, name their dead, and sing. Commodore Petra Voss, who counts the fleet’s survival in fuel and months, warns that light is exactly what the Hollow hunts. And Wayreader Selah Rennick must read the lane out of Vesper by Scripture, because in the Hollow’s wake instruments lie. Book One of The Pilgrim Fleet.
