Sleep Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Health & Wellness

You're not tired because you're busy — you're tired because your sleep is broken, and the broken parts are fixable. Sleep Made Simple shows how sleep actually works and how to repair it. Part I covers the biology: what sleep is, the sleep stages and why they matter, and the two systems that control sleep — the circadian rhythm and sleep pressure, the homeostatic sleep drive. Part II diagnoses what is breaking your sleep: light, screens, and circadian disruption; stress, cortisol, and the wired-but-tired trap; and the lifestyle factors that quietly wreck the night. Part III turns to repair — building a sleep foundation, shaping the sleep environment, and knowing what to do on the nights when sleep will not come. Part IV follows sleep into the day, through cognitive performance, physical health, and protecting your sleep for the long term. The argument running through all twelve chapters is simple: sleep is not a lifestyle preference but the foundational biological process on which health, performance, mood, and decision-making depend — and exhaustion accepted as normal is usually a disrupted system that can be changed, starting tonight. Chapter 6 names the everyday saboteurs — caffeine and alcohol among them — and the closing chapters report what repaired sleep returns: clearer thinking, steadier emotions, greater creativity, more effective learning, and better physical performance and recovery. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
