Made Simple: Advanced Thinking

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Made Simple: Advanced Thinking is a six-book set — six books, one price — gathering six volumes of The Made Simple Framework on how to think clearly under uncertainty. First Principles Thinking Made Simple breaks problems down to what is fundamentally true, challenges inherited assumptions, and rebuilds solutions from the ground up. Inversion Made Simple thinks backward: its introduction asks why thinking backward changes everything, and its final chapter argues that success often comes from avoiding failure. Second-Order Thinking Made Simple follows consequences past the first result — incentives, chain reactions, and delayed outcomes in business, economics, and personal decisions. Antifragile Thinking Made Simple asks why stress can make you stronger, and how to build careers, health, and systems that are not broken by volatility. Optionality Made Simple — subtitled How Smart Decisions Preserve Future Opportunity — shows how keeping options open beats betting everything on a single prediction. Thinking in Bets Made Simple treats every decision as a bet: uncertainty is always present, luck and skill must be separated, and decisions are judged by probabilities and process, not by one outcome. Together the six books hold 77 numbered chapters, each written in the Framework's plain language, without academic jargon. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
