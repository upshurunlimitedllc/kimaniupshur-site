Decision Architecture Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Personal Growth

Decision Architecture Made Simple, subtitled 'How Systems, Environments, and Choice Design Shape Human Decisions,' argues that no choice is made in a vacuum: defaults, option sets, sequencing, framing, friction, and information presentation quietly shape what gets chosen. Across an introduction and thirteen chapters, it draws on Thaler and Sunstein, Kahneman and Tversky, and research on status quo bias and choice overload to show why context dominates intention, then applies the architecture to digital products, organizations, and public policy, weighs the ethics of choice design, and closes with designing your own decision architecture. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
