The Lord's Prayer Study Workbook

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Jesus' disciples asked Him a simple question: "Lord, teach us to pray." This is His answer — line by line. The Lord's Prayer Study Workbook is a guided companion to The Lord's Prayer Made Simple by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed. Across 6 guided sessions, you'll walk slowly through the prayer Jesus taught — "Our Father in heaven," "Your kingdom come, Your will be done," "Give us today our daily bread," "Forgive us our debts, as we forgive," "Lead us not into temptation, deliver us from evil" — and close by learning to pray it daily: "For Yours is the kingdom." Each session follows the same simple rhythm: Read the verse slowly, Observe what it actually says, Apply it specifically and honestly to your life, and Pray it back to God. Every session ends with a callout to go line by line through that session's verse in the free Lord's Prayer Deep Dive app. Work at your own pace — one session a day, or one a week. There are no grades here.
