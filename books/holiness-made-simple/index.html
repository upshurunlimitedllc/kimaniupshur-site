Holiness

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Holiness by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed. — published as Holiness Made Simple, 'A Biblical Guide to Living Set Apart for God' — reclaims a word that makes many people nervous. In Scripture, holiness is not first a dress code or a list of rules: it is first a description of God — qadosh in Hebrew, hagios in Greek, 'set apart' in both — and then an invitation to become like Him: 'Be holy, because I am holy' (1 Peter 1:15–16). Fourteen chapters build the picture in order: what holiness really means, the holy, holy, holy God, being called out without being called weird, Jesus the Holy One, and the Holy Spirit the Sanctifier. The book then gets specific — conviction rather than condemnation (Romans 8:1), holy bodies, minds, words, and relationships, holiness in a sexually saturated culture, and holy money — before closing on progress, not perfection, and the end of the story: a holy people. Every chapter closes with a headed 'Your Action Step', and an appendix, 'Straight Answers on Holiness', takes up fourteen common questions straight from Scripture. The book's three-word hook is its whole argument: set apart for Him. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
