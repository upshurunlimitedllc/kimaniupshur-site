Spiritual Warfare

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Faith & Doctrine

Spiritual Warfare teaches the real fight: not against your spouse, your boss, or your neighbor, but against a defeated yet still dangerous enemy who works through lies, temptation, accusation, and deception — “for our struggle is not against flesh and blood” (Ephesians 6:12). The outcome of the war is already decided; Jesus Christ won it at the cross, and the believer's work is to enforce that victory one day at a time. Fourteen chapters walk the full armor of God piece by piece: knowing your real enemy, the belt of truth, the breastplate of righteousness, the shoes of the gospel of peace, the shield of faith, the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the Word of God. The book then moves from armor to action: prayer as warfare, authority in Jesus' name, resisting the devil until he flees, the battlefield of the mind, worship as warfare, and the victory already won. Built on Scripture rather than speculation — its quotations are from the New International Version — it offers no scare stories and no demon-chasing instructions, only what the Bible actually says, stated plainly: who the enemy is, how he works, what God has given you to stand, and how to walk in Christ's victory. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
