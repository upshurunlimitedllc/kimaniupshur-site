Mastering AI

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Tech & AI

Mastering AI is a practical, plain-language guide to learning and using artificial intelligence for your own benefit — to transform your work, boost your productivity, and future-proof your career. An introduction, “Welcome to the AI Revolution,” argues that modern AI tools understand natural language, so you do not need to be a programmer or a data scientist to use them well. Nine chapters then build the skill in order. The book explains AI fundamentals honestly — what large language models, image generators, and AI assistants can and cannot do, including hallucinations and the limits of machine “understanding” — then helps you choose and set up a first tool such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Microsoft Copilot. A full chapter teaches the art of prompting through context, task clarity, format specification, and constraints and tone, with advanced techniques such as role assignment, chain-of-thought prompting, few-shot examples, and iterative refinement. Later chapters apply AI to writing, research, and everyday work, show how to build effective workflows and follow best practices, survey industry-specific applications, and troubleshoot common mistakes. Worked examples and sample prompts appear throughout, so a beginner can follow an approach step by step, try it in a first conversation, and refine the result instead of starting from a blank page. A final chapter looks at the future of AI and your career, and a conclusion sends you off to begin. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
