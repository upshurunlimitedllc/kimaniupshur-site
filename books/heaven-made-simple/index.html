Heaven

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

¿Lo prefieres en español? Lee El Cielo Hecho Simple, la edición en español

Faith & Doctrine

Heaven is the home you were made for — a real place, not a state of mind, a boring eternity, or an automatic destination. Heaven replaces the foggy postcard most people carry with the Bible's own picture, starting from Colossians 3:1-2: set your hearts and minds on things above, where Christ is. Its introduction clears away three foggy ideas first — heaven as a state of mind, heaven as boring, and heaven as automatic. Fourteen chapters build that picture in order: what heaven really is; God the Father on the throne; Jesus, who went to prepare a place (John 14); and the Holy Spirit, heaven's down payment in believers now. It walks through what the Bible says heaven looks like, the end of tears and death, who will be there, whether we will know each other, what we will do there, rewards, and the new earth — then turns practical: how heaven changes how you live today, how to be sure you are going, and the way home. An appendix answers fourteen questions about heaven, and a closing prayer gathers the whole journey. Each of the fourteen chapters ends with a headed "YOUR ACTION STEP." Scripture quotations are taken from the New International Version (NIV), with some passages paraphrased for clarity and marked as such. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
