Persuasion Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Personal Growth

Every mind that changed was changed on purpose. Persuasion Made Simple starts there: someone decided what to say, how to frame it, when to say it, and to whom — and this book gives you that underlying science of how minds actually change, without manipulation. Persuasion here is not winning arguments or transferring information; it is changing what people believe and do, and it begins with understanding the audience rather than constructing the cleverest argument. An introduction and twelve chapters build the subject in order: what persuasion really is — including Aristotle's three modes of ethos, pathos, and logos — the psychology of belief change, credibility and trust, emotion, the architecture of argument, framing and language, social proof and authority, overcoming resistance, and context and timing. Later chapters apply persuasion across contexts and teach readers to defend against it, before a final chapter on what actually matters, with a core insight, an ethics of persuasion, and a practical summary. For communicators, leaders, and negotiators who want influence that lasts, Persuasion Made Simple offers honest persuasion — accurate information, genuine emotional stakes, and respect for the other person's own conclusions — over tactics that win a yes and lose the relationship. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
