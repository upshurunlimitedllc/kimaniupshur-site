THE LAST CHART

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

FictionFiction

For twenty months there was peace. Forty keepers now hold forty lane-stations where Caleb Voss once kept Farlight alone, and the Covenant’s array sweeps the lanes in gold. Then Dr. Ilsa Venn’s red-flagged tightbeam burns in from the survey line, nineteen hours old: the moving dark has jumped her charted vector. Her two years are gone. The dark’s leading edge will cross the Halden lane in six weeks and the main convoy lanes in ten — and an eleven-hauler Halden ore convoy, full crews aboard, is already in its path. Worse, the map every ship sails by has run out: June Okafor’s chart holds twenty-two true intervals, then blank dark where her father died mapping. While the fleet scatters the convoy and musters, June sails back into the dark’s wake to finish the chart — because no ship crosses without one. Book Three of the Beacon Saga. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
