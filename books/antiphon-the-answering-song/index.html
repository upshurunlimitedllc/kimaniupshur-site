Antiphon: The Answering Song

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

FictionFiction

Three years after the duet ended the war, peace at the Sareen anchorage has a sound: reef-ships growing new spars, the Loom's forges ringing, children in the creche-pools — noise that frightens no one. Then Vera Okafor filters the deep array and hears the impossible: Tarsus IV, the Hollow, a world flayed to the mantle and drained of its oceans, singing its own long-kept lullaby back to itself. Jonah Reyes understands the difference at last — between being heard and being answered. Earth, Luna and the Belt never heard the duet; three billion people think the war simply stopped. So the St. Brendan loads her song-vault for the homeward voyage. Book Four of the Saturn Cycle, written from a Christian worldview. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
