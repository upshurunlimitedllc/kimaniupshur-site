Proverbs

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Bible People & Stories

Proverbs walks through the Bible's most practical book with a simple conviction: before you make the next decision, a voice is already speaking. 'Out in the open wisdom calls aloud, she raises her voice in the public square' (Proverbs 1:20–21). The book's hook is four words: wisdom cries aloud — listen. Scripture quotations are from the New International Version. Fourteen chapters follow the book of Proverbs through its great themes: the fear of the LORD as the beginning of knowledge, trusting in the LORD, getting wisdom at any cost, and guarding your heart; the straight path of purity, the rival calls of wisdom and folly, the tongue that carries life and death, diligence against the sluggard, money God's way, and choosing your friends; then pride before a fall, honest lips and honest scales, correction as kindness, and the virtuous life of Proverbs 31. Each chapter ends with one action step — not ten — because wisdom is practiced one decision at a time. An appendix of straight answers on Proverbs and a closing prayer complete the book. It serves readers who want daily, usable wisdom — including its own simplest next step: read one chapter of Proverbs a day, and in a month you will have walked through the whole book. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
