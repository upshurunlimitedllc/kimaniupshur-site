Trauma Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Health & Wellness

Trauma starts with what trauma actually does to a person. The opening chapters define trauma, trace how it affects the brain and body, and map the spectrum of traumatic experience — including post-traumatic stress, complex trauma, and what trauma does to relationships. The second half turns to recovery: the biology of healing, evidence-based trauma treatment, and the work of processing what happened, then building safety and stability. It closes with post-traumatic growth — the path forward. An introduction, eleven chapters, and a conclusion, in plain language for readers who want to understand their own experience or help someone through theirs.
