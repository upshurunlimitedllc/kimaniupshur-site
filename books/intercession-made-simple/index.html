Intercession

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Prayer & Worship

Intercession: A Biblical Guide to Praying for Others with Power opens with a scene and a summons: somewhere a marriage is cracking, a teenager is drifting, a neighbor is in a hospital bed — and God is looking for someone willing to stand between that need and heaven and not move until He moves. That, the book teaches, is intercession: praying for somebody else with the same urgency you would pray for yourself. In fourteen chapters it defines intercession, explains why God looks for intercessors, and walks through the intercessors of the Bible, Jesus the great Intercessor, and the Holy Spirit's help in prayer. It then applies the practice room by room: praying for your family, your church, leaders and authorities, your nation, your enemies, and the lost. The final chapters cover persistence — keep asking, keep seeking, keep knocking — fasting and intercession, and what to do when the answer tarries. The book's Introduction states its apparatus plainly: each of the fourteen chapters opens with a Scripture and ends with one action step, and the counted text bears that out. An appendix of straight answers on intercession and a closing prayer complete the book. Scripture quotations are from the New International Version (NIV). Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
