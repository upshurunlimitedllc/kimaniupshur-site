Peace

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Peace begins with a question most people have never asked out loud: what would your life feel like if you were actually at peace — not vacation peace or caught-up-on-sleep peace, but the kind that holds when the doctor says 'we need to talk,' when the money runs out before the month does, when the person you love most is walking away? The world's answer is to manage the noise: meditate it away, think positive, try harder to relax. This book starts somewhere different. Peace, Kimani Upshur argues from Scripture, is not a skill you perform; it is a Person you receive and a fruit the Spirit grows — 'the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace' (Galatians 5:22). Fourteen chapters build that case in order: what peace actually is, Jesus' peace versus the world's peace, why peace with God comes first, peace as a fruit rather than a feeling, the command 'do not let your hearts be troubled,' the peace exchange of praying it out, peace in the storm, the Holy Spirit as God's peace-giver, and a mind set on God. Later chapters take up peace in the waiting, peace at night, peace with people, thanksgiving as the guard at the door, and living from peace as a daily practice. A conclusion, an appendix of straight answers on peace, and a closing prayer complete the book. Scripture quotations are from the New International Version, with some passages paraphrased for clarity and marked as such. For readers living in the low hum of a heart that won't quite settle, Peace offers the Bible's stronger promise: a peace with weight to it, standing guard over heart and mind. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
