Pilgrimage Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Pilgrimage — the journey itself is worship — treats the whole Christian life as a road. You did not choose it, the Hook says; it chose you the day you first believed, and from that day everything has been a journey toward a city you have never seen. Three times a year God's people once walked to Jerusalem singing the Psalms of Ascent, Psalms 120 through 134, and this book walks those songs with the reader who feels stuck, circling a wilderness, or still packing while everyone else seems to be arriving. Fourteen chapters follow the road in order: Abraham's call to go, the gladness of setting out, the Keeper who never sleeps, highways in the heart, the identity of strangers and pilgrims, the wilderness road and the Valley of Weeping, Ruth's 'Where you go, I will go,' the city with foundations whose builder and maker is God, running with endurance, sowing in tears, the night watch, and arriving home. A conclusion, an appendix of pilgrimage questions and answers, and a closing prayer complete the book. The book states that its Scripture quotations are the author's own paraphrase and rendering of the biblical text, drawn from the public domain tradition. Pilgrimage is for believers learning to pack light, walk with others, rest without guilt, and keep climbing — because the One waiting at the end is already walking beside them. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
