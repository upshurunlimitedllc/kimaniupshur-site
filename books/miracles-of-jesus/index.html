Miracles of Jesus

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

12 Books in One Volume

Miracles of Jesus brings all twelve Miracles of Jesus children's books together in one volume for young readers ages 6–12 — twelve miracles of Jesus from the Gospels, retold for children and gathered in order: Bartimaeus Sees · Be Opened · Jesus Calms the Storm · Jesus Touched Him · Jesus Walks on Water · Just Say The Word · Talitha Koum! · The Coin In The Fish · The Five Thousand Fed · The Hem of His Robe · The Man Who Couldn't Talk · Waiting By The Pool.

A blind man who would not be quiet. A storm stilled, and a sea walked on. A little girl woken at the words "Talitha Koum." Five thousand people fed, a woman healed by touching the hem of His robe, a coin found in a fish's mouth — every story shows children who Jesus is, and what He can do.

Each of the twelve books is complete: ten short chapters, a Note to Grown-Ups, and its own Gospel Invitation — "A Special Invitation: Give Your Life to Jesus" — so every book closes with an invitation to know Jesus Christ.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
