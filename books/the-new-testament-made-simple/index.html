The New Testament

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Bible Study & Scripture

Twenty-seven books, one story — the story that saves. The New Testament, How the Gospels, the Church, the Letters, and the Return of Jesus Change Everything, is a guided survey of the whole second half of the Bible for ordinary readers who want to understand God's Word and live it out. After an opening chapter on what the New Testament actually is, the book walks it in order: the four Gospels by their portraits — Matthew, the King has come; Mark, the Servant who suffers; Luke, the Son of Man for everyone; John, the Son of God — then Acts, where the church is born, and Romans, the gospel explained. The letters come next in their families: letters to the churches, the gospel applied; letters to leaders, faithful to the end; Hebrews — Jesus is better; and the general letters, faith that works. Revelation closes the canon and the book: the return of the King. Two final chapters turn the survey into practice — how to read the New Testament, and how to pray it — and the conclusion issues the New Testament's own invitation: come and see. Scripture quotations are from the New International Version (NIV). Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
