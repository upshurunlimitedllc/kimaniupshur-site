Hard Decisions Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Personal Growth

Hard Decisions Made Simple by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed. — 'How to Choose When There Is No Perfect Answer' — begins where hard choices actually begin: both options carry real costs, there is no clearly right answer, and you cannot keep waiting. Across an introduction, eleven chapters, and a conclusion, the book first works through why hard decisions are hard — the trap of waiting for certainty, and the way fear distorts how the options get evaluated. It then turns practical: clarifying what you actually value before weighing trade-offs, recognizing what the data cannot tell you, and running a pre-mortem — imagining that it is a year later, the decision has turned out badly, and asking what went wrong — to surface the concerns that optimism and commitment suppress. Later chapters separate reversible decisions from irreversible ones, weigh the role of other people, and follow the choice through to making the decision and living with its consequences. The standard the conclusion sets is not perfection but good enough: a clear-eyed decision, made on your real values, that you can stand behind once the outcome arrives. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
