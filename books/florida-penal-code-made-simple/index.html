Florida Penal Code Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Most people have no idea how the criminal justice system actually works — until it touches their life. Florida Penal Code Made Simple changes that. Florida’s criminal law lives in Title XLVI of the Florida Statutes, and this book explains it in plain language for ordinary readers: how the code is organized, who enforces and prosecutes the law, and how a case moves step by step from arrest to verdict. You will learn how Florida classifies crimes — capital, life, and first-, second-, and third-degree felonies, first- and second-degree misdemeanors, and noncriminal violations — and what each degree really means for punishment, including the Criminal Punishment Code scoresheet, the 10-20-Life mandatory minimums, habitual-offender and prison-releasee-reoffender enhancements, and the death penalty process. The book walks through the process itself — arrest, first appearance within 24 hours, charging decisions, arraignment, discovery, trial, and sentencing — then covers the major categories of crime, key concepts like intent, self-defense, and entrapment, what happens after a conviction, and the rights you must know, from searches and silence to victims’ rights under Marsy’s Law. Florida realities get their own plain treatment: Stand Your Ground, the 2023 permitless concealed carry law, and sealing or expunging a record. Every chapter ends with ‘Know the Law’ takeaways and ‘Protect Yourself’ action steps, built on a simple framework: Know. Comply. Protect. This is general information, not legal advice, and the book says so plainly. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
