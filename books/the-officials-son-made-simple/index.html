The Official's Son Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Bible People & Stories

"Go thy way; thy son liveth" — believed from seventeen miles away. This Scripture-first guide walks through the nobleman of Capernaum (John 4:46-54), phrase by phrase: a royal official's desperate journey to Cana, his plea "come down ere my child die," Christ's searching rebuke "except ye see signs and wonders," the word spoken from a distance, the faith that believed before it saw, the seventh hour when the fever left, and the household that came to Christ because one father would not let go. Thirteen chapters cover who the nobleman was, the cry of a dying hope, the rebuke that was mercy, the word that crossed seventeen miles, faith before evidence, heaven's timestamp, faith that spreads through a whole house, why John chose this as the second sign, Christ's lordship over distance, the believer's walk home on a word, Trinitarian prayer to the Father through the Son by the Holy Spirit, Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior — Son of God and Son of Man — a pattern for praying like the nobleman today, a closing prayer, and reflection questions for every chapter.
