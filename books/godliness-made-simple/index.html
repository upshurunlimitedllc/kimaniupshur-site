Godliness

Christian Living

Godliness by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed. — published as Godliness Made Simple, 'A Biblical Guide to a Godward Life' — defines godliness (eusebeia) as a life that leans toward God: toward His will, His Word, and His glory, in reverence and obedience. Its anchor is 1 Timothy 4:8: godliness is 'profitable unto all things, having promise of the life that now is, and of that which is to come.' Fourteen chapters cover what godliness means, its value, training yourself (1 Timothy 4:7), the Father who is worthy, Jesus the pattern, the Holy Spirit the power, godliness with contentment, the form of godliness without the power, and the Godward life in the heart, the home, at work, and in speech and conduct, finishing godly. Each chapter opens with Scripture and ends with one action step. An appendix answers fourteen straight questions, and the book closes with a prayer and a personal invitation to give your life to Jesus Christ, with the Sinner's Prayer. Scripture quotations are from the King James Version. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
