Waits-for-Morning

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

FictionFiction

Yesterday, the first singer fell — and the low fields stopped singing. Doss, a sower who knew every stalk's voice like a shepherd knows his flock, wakes beside his wife and his children, Beck and Ivy, to silence, and to a grey unmaking still spreading outward from where the dropped Seed-Song struck the earth. What was dropped cannot be picked up again. So Doss does the only faithful thing left: he counts, he rations, he keeps — guarding the granary for a frightened camp — and then, in secret, he risks the kept seed on one hidden furrow. Waits-for-Morning: The Long Dawn is the companion to Vane: The Falling — the story of ordinary people learning to wait for a morning that will not come in their lifetime. Written from a Christian worldview. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
