Noah

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Bible People & Stories

Noah — Obedience That Builds Before the Flood Comes — tells the story of the man who built an ark on dry land. Warned by God, Noah preached righteousness for a hundred and twenty years to a world that mocked him, hammered timber while the skies stayed blue, and walked through the door of God's vessel before the flood came. The book sums up his story as warning before judgment, grace before wrath, and a covenant rainbow after the storm. Across twelve chapters, it follows Noah from the grace he found and his walk with God, through the corruption of the world, the warning and the hundred and twenty years, and the ark built by faith, to Noah the preacher of righteousness, the animals coming two by two, and the flood and the shut door. It closes the account with the God who remembers, a sacrifice on dry ground, the covenant rainbow, and Noah's own failure set against God's faithfulness — then a conclusion, 'Enter the Ark,' and a Closing Prayer that names Jesus Christ the ark of our salvation. Each chapter follows the same pattern: Scripture first, then the teaching, Reflection questions, Application steps, and a prayer to the Father, through Jesus Christ, by the Holy Spirit. Noah is for believers learning to obey God the same day the lesson is learned, long before the rain starts. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
