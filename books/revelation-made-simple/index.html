Revelation Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Bible Study

Revelation is the Bible book everyone is curious about and half afraid of — and most people meet it through charts, predictions, and fear instead of through the book itself. Revelation Made Simple changes that.

In twelve short chapters, Kimani Upshur walks you through all twenty-two chapters of Revelation in order — the vision of Christ, the seven letters, the throne and the Lamb, the seals, the trumpets, the dragon and the beasts, the harvest, the bowls, the fall of Babylon, the return of the King, and the new heaven and new earth. Calmly. Plainly. Without sensationalism, date-setting, or fear-mongering.

Built around three words — Worthy. Coming. Home. — this is the book for anyone who has ever wanted to understand Revelation but didn't know where to start. Read one chapter a day for twelve days, and finish able to pray the Bible's last prayer with joy instead of anxiety: "Come, Lord Jesus!"
