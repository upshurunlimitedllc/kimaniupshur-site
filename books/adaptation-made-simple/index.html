Adaptation

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Change is constant, but most people are not prepared for it. Adaptation — “How to Adjust, Grow in Faith, and Thrive in a Changing World” — begins there and presses the harder question: not only how to adjust, but what, and Who, does not change while all else does. “For I the LORD do not change” (Malachi 3:6, ESV), and Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today and forever (Hebrews 13:8). In an introduction, ten chapters, and a conclusion, Kimani Upshur, M.Ed. teaches it as a set of capacities — perception, interpretation, and adjustment — sustained by feedback and learning. Each pairs the mechanics with a faith section and a closing prayer: the deepest adaptation, becoming a new creation in Christ; the unchanging God beneath constant change; Israel looking back toward Egypt, and Paul’s forward lean; God’s Word as the clearest feedback; Joseph — God meant it for good; Jesus’ new wine in fresh wineskins — the core held fast, the forms free to flex; Abraham going out, not knowing where he was going; the people of God — history’s most adaptive system; Paul’s learned contentment in every season; and a mind renewed day by day. The conclusion rests on God’s promise — “Behold, I am doing a new thing” (Isaiah 43:19) — and its back matter invites you to give your life to Jesus Christ. Adjust intelligently — and trust completely. All Scripture is quoted from the ESV. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
