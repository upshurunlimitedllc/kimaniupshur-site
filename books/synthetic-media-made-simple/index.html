Synthetic Media Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Tech & AI

Synthetic Media Made Simple opens with a 2017 university demonstration that taught a computer to make a former president appear to say words he never said — and asks what a world does when seeing is no longer believing. In an Introduction titled "Reality Has Left the Chat" and eleven chapters, the book explains what synthetic media actually is and the technology behind it, then takes each form in turn: deepfakes and manipulated video, AI-generated images, cloned voices and audio fakes, and synthetic text written to deceive. It traces how fabricated media spreads, and gives you working habits for detection and verification — how to slow down, check sources, and test what a clip is asking you to feel. It maps the legal and ethical landscape as it stands, examines what synthetic media does to democracy when anyone can manufacture evidence, and closes with practical protection for yourself and your organization. The Conclusion weighs synthetic media as a disruption on the scale of the printing press and photography: a technology whose safeguards — institutional, legal, and educational — are being built in real time, by the people living through it. Plainly written for a general audience, this is a field guide to keeping your judgment intact when the evidence itself can be manufactured. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
