The Devil Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Faith & Doctrine

*The Devil Made Simple: Who Satan Is, What He Can't Do — and Why the Cross Already Won

Most Christians make one of two mistakes about the devil: they ignore him completely, or they see him behind everything. The Devil Made Simple* offers the biblical middle — a sober, plain-language portrait of the adversary: his names and what they reveal, his origin as a created being, his fall, his three great strategies (deception, accusation, temptation), his real limits, his crushing defeat at the cross, and his certain end.

With twelve short chapters — each built on Scripture, with reflection questions, practical steps, and prayers — Kimani Upshur walks you through who Satan is, how he works, what he cannot do, and why you never have to fear him. Neither obsessive nor dismissive, this is the calm, confidence-building guide every believer needs.

Know. Resist. Trust. Stand firm and fear nothing.
