Reconciliation

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Family & Relationships

Reconciliation begins with a phone call you have not made, a chair at the table that stays empty, and a relationship you now walk around the way you walk around a wet spot on the floor. This book starts where Scripture starts: "All this is from God, who reconciled us to himself through Christ and gave us the ministry of reconciliation" (2 Corinthians 5:18). Across fourteen chapters, it walks the work in order — first be reconciled to God, then go and be reconciled to the person who has something against you. It takes up the hard conversation, forgiving before they apologize, and repentance that actually restores. It shows trust rebuilt brick by brick, one block laid at a time, and it faces the hardest case honestly: what faithfulness looks like when the other person will not come, when reconciliation stays one-sided, and when boundaries must remain because reconciliation does not mean returning to danger. Family and marriage each get their own chapter, before the book turns to the church's reconciling witness, the final reconciliation, and living as a peacemaker. Each chapter ends with one action step — not five — and an appendix answers fourteen honest questions about reconciliation, followed by a closing prayer. Scripture quotations are from the New International Version (NIV). Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
