Order Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Order is a biblical guide to a decent and ordered life. Its starting point is honest: your life feels chaotic because, in some area, it is — and chaos is not God's will for you. 'Let all things be done decently and in order' (1 Corinthians 14:40) was not just church policy; it is a window into God's character. He is not the author of confusion but the architect of order, and the book's governing image makes the work concrete: every area of life is either a garden or a wilderness. Gardens are planned, planted, tended, and weeded; wilderness happens by default. Scripture quotations are paraphrased in plain language unless otherwise noted, and readers are encouraged to test everything against God's Word for themselves. Ten chapters take the areas in turn. After establishing the God of order, the book brings order to the mind, to time, to the home, to money, to work, to worship, and to relationships — each with practices a reader can use within the week. A late chapter sounds the necessary warning, When Order Becomes an Idol: divine order serves love and produces peace, and an order that makes a person harsh, anxious, or judgmental is not God's. The conclusion gathers the aim in three words: Ordered, Peaceful, Purposeful. It serves believers whose calendars, finances, homes, or minds have gone wild in some department and who want progress rather than perfection — a life ten percent more ordered is ten percent more peaceful, tended one area at a time with the Master Gardener's help. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
