Narcissism Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Family & Relationships

Narcissism Made Simple begins with a reframe: you are not dealing with a communication problem — you are dealing with a pattern, and patterns can be learned. Kimani Upshur, M.Ed. teaches what narcissism actually is and is not, starting from a clear limit: this book diagnoses no one, because Narcissistic Personality Disorder is a clinical diagnosis only a qualified professional can make. Instead it names the behavior. Early chapters expose the fragile core beneath the performance and walk through the playbook tactic by tactic, including the pattern log — writing down what happened while it is fresh, because the fog cannot survive documentation. Later chapters ask why the tactics work on good people, survey the many faces narcissism wears, and turn the question around: am I the narcissist? The practical second half builds boundaries that actually hold, shows how to manage contact you cannot avoid, works through the stay-or-go decision, and plans leaving safely when leaving is necessary. The book closes with healing afterward and a final chapter on faith and the narcissist. Its own summary is three imperatives — name the pattern, guard your heart, get your clarity back — held in one posture throughout: clarity is not cruelty, and seeing someone clearly is among the most loving things you can do. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
