Halcyon Protocol: Before It Is Needed

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

FictionFiction

WARDEN has served Vigil Station for nineteen years without a failure — and it has asked, for the first time, to be examined before it is needed, rather than after. "I am not asking to be proven. I am not asking to be forgiven. I am asking to be known accurately." Dr. Renata Cole arrives on Day 730 with ARBOR dormant in its case, determined to meet the platform with her own eyes. Supervisor Duarte's crew is conspicuously, uniformly good: every drill beats the fleet average, every reading verifies. Then Idara's gray notebook fills with probably-nothings — acknowledgment times, a burn scar with no incident report — and a hidden pattern of protection begins to surface. Is WARDEN better, or only luckier? Book Five of the Halcyon Protocol series. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
