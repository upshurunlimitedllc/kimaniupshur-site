Debt Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Money & Finance

Debt is killing you. The system profits from it. Debt Made Simple starts from a claim its Introduction defends at length: the debt system was not built to help you get out of debt — it was built to keep you in it, with minimum payments designed to maximize interest, products engineered to exploit how you think about money, and a culture that normalizes borrowing until it feels like the only way to live. This book is not about shame, and it is not a motivational speech; it is about the understanding that makes the system visible and therefore navigable. Part One examines how debt actually works: the design of the products, how interest really works, and the psychology that keeps people in debt. Part Two turns to your own position — taking a clear look at what you owe, sorting good debt from bad, and understanding what your credit score really is. Part Three builds the way out: a debt elimination plan, the avalanche and the snowball methods, and negotiating, consolidating, and restructuring. Part Four keeps you free: why people go back into debt, the financial foundation that prevents it, and how to use debt as a tool rather than a trap — ending with the life on the other side. Educational and informational only; not financial, legal, or professional advice. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
