Environmental Psychology Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Environmental Psychology Made Simple: How Your Environment Shapes Your Thinking, Behavior, Emotions, and Decisions, by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed., starts from a simple observation: you did not choose most of the environments you inhabit, but those environments are choosing things for you — what you notice, how much energy you have, and which decisions feel easy or hard. Across an introduction and thirteen chapters, the book explains what environmental psychology actually is and how physical settings shape the brain, then examines light, sound, color, and smell; the psychology of space and place; the city mind; nature and restoration; work and home environments; digital and social environments; and the way retail spaces and architecture are designed to steer behavior. A final chapter turns the research toward the reader's own life — designing a personal environment — and a conclusion, 'The Designed Life,' draws the argument together. It is written for readers who want to understand why places affect them the way they do, and how to arrange the spaces they can control so those spaces work for them rather than against them. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
