The Cross

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

The Cross — A Biblical Guide to Christ's Finished Work — opens with the cross's own last word on its title page: "It is finished." Everything in the Christian faith, its Introduction insists, stands on one piece of ground: the cross of Jesus Christ. Across thirteen chapters it builds the doctrine in order: why the cross was necessary (Hebrews 9:22); Jesus the Lamb of God (John 1:29); how He bore our sins (1 Peter 2:24); and "It is finished" itself — tetelestai, paid in full (John 19:30). It unfolds the great exchange (2 Corinthians 5:21), justification by His blood (Romans 5:9), forgiveness once for all (Hebrews 10:10, 14), and the blood of the new covenant (Matthew 26:28). It shows the cross as victory over the powers (Colossians 2:15), makes us dead to sin and alive to God (Romans 6:6), and turns doctrine into discipleship: take up your cross (Luke 9:23), and boast only in the cross (Galatians 6:14). The Conclusion — The Empty Cross and the Risen Lord — sums it up: "God the Father planned it. Jesus the Son finished it." Every chapter closes with one "Your step:" action — thirteen in all. An appendix gives straight answers about the cross, and a Closing Prayer ends the book. Scripture quotations are from the NIV. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
