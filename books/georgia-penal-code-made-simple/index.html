Georgia Penal Code Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Georgia’s criminal law fills thousands of pages — written by lawyers, for lawyers. Georgia Penal Code Made Simple translates it into plain English. The book works from the real code — the Official Code of Georgia, Title 16, Crimes and Offenses, with Title 17’s criminal procedure beside it — and walks you through what the code is and how it works, the difference between misdemeanors and felonies (a misdemeanor carries up to twelve months; a misdemeanor of a high and aggravated nature carries more), and the offenses Georgia singles out as its ‘seven deadly sins.’ Twelve clear chapters follow the criminal process step by step from arrest to sentencing, explain how charges and penalties are determined, and cover the most common categories of crimes, the key legal concepts that decide cases, what happens after a conviction, the constitutional rights you should know, and the myths that get people in trouble. Georgia-specific laws get direct treatment, from constitutional carry to the citizen’s arrest repeal, and the book closes with how to think clearly under pressure. Every chapter ends with ‘Know the Law’ takeaways and ‘Protect Yourself’ action steps, built around a simple triad: Know. Respect. Protect. This is general education, not legal advice, and the book says so plainly. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
