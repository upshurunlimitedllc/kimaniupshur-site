Identity Theft

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Identity Theft: A Biblical Guide to Guarding Your Name and Your Future draws a sharp line. Your identity in Christ — forgiven, adopted, sealed by the Holy Spirit — can never be stolen, hacked, or sold. But your Social Security number, bank accounts, and medical file can be, and thieves use them to open credit cards, file false tax returns, and leave debts and ruined credit behind. The book treats your name, credit, accounts, and family records as a stewardship God has entrusted to you, and teaches you to guard them wisely. It explains how thieves get your information and the warning signs to watch for, then gives the practical defences: credit freezes and fraud alerts, passwords and two-factor authentication, and protection against tax and medical identity theft. If the theft has already happened, it lays out what to do in the first hour, a full recovery plan, and how to dispute debts that are not yours. It also covers protecting your children's identities, protecting elderly parents, shrinking your digital footprint, and recognising the scams that lead to identity theft. An appendix answers common questions directly, every chapter ends with a Your Action Step, and the book closes by framing vigilance as stewardship — as Nehemiah's people prayed to God and posted a guard — ending with a closing prayer. Scripture quotations are taken from the New International Version. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
