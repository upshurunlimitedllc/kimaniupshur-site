Single Parent Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Family & Relationships

You are doing the job of two — and this book begins by saying so. Single Parent is a simple, honest guide to the mechanics of solo parenting, written from a Christian worldview the author states openly: Psalm 68:5 calls God “a father to the fatherless, a defender of widows,” and Chapter 13 is devoted to faith and hope for the hard days. The opening chapter insists you are enough, then the book gets practical. It builds a real budget on one income, a calendar that runs itself, and discipline without the guilt spiral. It sets co-parenting boundaries — or shows how to do it fully solo — and helps you build your village, date as a single parent, and answer the kids' emotions and hard questions. Household systems go on autopilot, self-care is treated as necessity rather than selfishness, and the juggle of work and childcare gets its own chapter. A legal-basics chapter covers custody, support, and knowing your rights, with the book's own clear disclaimer that it is general information, not legal advice. An appendix gives quick answers to the questions every single parent asks, and the conclusion looks down the years: the days are long, the years are short, and every packed lunch is a brick in something permanent. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
