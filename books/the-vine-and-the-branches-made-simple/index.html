The Vine and the Branches

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

The Vine and the Branches, by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed., works slowly through Jesus' words in John 15:1–11, spoken on the last night before the cross — its own pattern is Connected. Pruned. Fruitful. Built as one chapter a day for ten days, its ten chapters cover the true vine, the husbandman, the pruning, being clean through the word, abiding in Christ, the withered branch, asking what you will, bearing much fruit, and continuing in His love. The scriptures quoted are in the King James style, and the book closes with a conclusion and a closing prayer. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
