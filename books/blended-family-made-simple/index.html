Blended Family Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Family & Relationships

You are not running a damaged-goods household — you are building something new out of pieces life scattered, and God specializes in exactly that. From putting your marriage first to the step-parent’s slow, sacred work, this Scripture-grounded guide shows you how to lay a new covenant foundation instead of living as a sequel to the old one. Drawing on blended families in the Bible — Joseph, Moses, Esther — it offers a hopeful, God’s-way path to building one family from many pieces.
