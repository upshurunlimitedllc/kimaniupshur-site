Forty Tables

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

FictionFiction

Day 1101. In Miriam's hand, the assignments go up in the archive hall: forty tables, sixty platforms, 312 souls. Each departing keeper carries the same kit — a gray notebook, the examination schedule for the second cycle, Okafor's stew recipe sealed in wax, and the eighth plate, wrapped separately in cloth. No table departs without the plate. Forty Tables follows the scattering of the practice, from Hearth-1 to Clearwell, as reports come back by wire and the second cycle of caretaker-system examinations begins. But a discipline carried by forty different hands does not stay identical: shortened examinations, findings written down as "all nominal," and keepers learning what the keeping costs far from home test whether the practice can survive being shared. Book Three of the Halcyon Protocol series. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
