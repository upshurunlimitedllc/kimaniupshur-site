*AI Safety Made Simple — What AI Can and Can't Do, and How to Stay Safe

The phone rings. It's your granddaughter's voice — except it isn't her. It's a machine.

AI is already in your life: in your phone, your inbox, your kids' homework, and the scams targeting your savings. You don't need a computer science degree to stay safe. You need a calm, plain-language guide.

AI Safety Made Simple* gives you exactly that: what AI actually is (and isn't), how the voice-clone and fake-video scams work and the exact moves that stop them, how to protect your data and your kids, how to use AI wisely at work and home, and the verification habit that defeats nearly every trick. No hype, no horror movie — just Learn. Guard. Use.

Written by educator Kimani Upshur, M.Ed., in the warm, direct style of the Made Simple Framework. One short chapter a day for twelve days — about ten minutes each.
