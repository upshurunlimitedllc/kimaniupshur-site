Who Controls the Future

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Tech & AI

Who Controls the Future, by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed., opens with a plain answer: nobody controls the future — but some people, companies, and governments have more influence over it than others, and that influence is concentrating now, at unprecedented speed and scale, around artificial intelligence. Its six chapters examine the race for AI, how incentives shape the future, systems and technological power, competition and strategy, and risk and uncertainty in the AI era — closing with why clear thinking matters more than ever. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
