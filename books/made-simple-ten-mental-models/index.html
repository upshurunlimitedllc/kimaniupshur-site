Made Simple: Ten Mental Models

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Mental Models Made Simple: How to Think Clearly, Avoid Cognitive Traps, and Make Better Decisions Using Powerful Thinking Frameworks is a plain-language guide to the frameworks that shape good judgment. It begins by asking why thinking frameworks matter, what separates intelligence from judgment, and where intuition reaches its limits. Its core models are first principles thinking, which breaks a problem down to what is actually true; inversion thinking, which approaches a problem backwards and uses the pre-mortem to imagine failure before it happens; second-order thinking, which follows consequences past their first effects; and opportunity cost, which counts what every yes gives up. It then turns to decision thinking: probabilistic thinking about odds and uncertainty, incentives and human behavior, and systems thinking about the structures beneath events. The final part applies the models where readers actually decide — mental models for work, for money, and for life decisions — and closes with a final chapter on building a personal thinking toolkit of your own. The book is written for educational and informational purposes in the direct, jargon-free style of the Made Simple Framework: frameworks that work, explained clearly, with examples drawn from real situations, so that any serious reader can engage with ideas usually locked inside technical literature. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
