Hebrews

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Bible People & Stories

Do not go back — Jesus is better. Hebrews walks through the letter to the Hebrews in plain language for believers who are tempted to quit, the same pressure its first readers faced: Jewish Christians mocked, losing property, and wondering whether to return to the old religion. Its introduction frames the whole letter around one bottom line — hold fast to your confession — and insists Hebrews is about God's grip on you, not only your grip on God. Its thirteen chapters follow the letter's argument: God has spoken by His Son (Hebrews 1:1-2); so great a salvation; faithful over the house; the rest that remains; milk, meat, and maturity; the priest forever; the better covenant; the better sacrifice; once for all; draw near and hold fast; faith; run with endurance; and hold fast to the end. The word "better" runs throughout — better hope, covenant, promises, sacrifice, and country — because everything the old system pointed to has arrived in Christ, who sympathizes with our weakness and always lives to intercede. The book reads Hebrews' five warnings as guardrails, not threats, and an appendix answers twelve honest questions about Hebrews before a closing prayer. Each chapter ends with a single "Your Action Step." Scripture quotations are from the Holy Bible, New International Version (NIV). Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
