Trials Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Trials opens with a question every believer asks in a hard season — where is God in this? — and answers it with the Word of God. Grounded in James 1:2–4, where the testing of faith produces perseverance and leaves the believer mature and complete, not lacking anything, the book teaches that a trial is not punishment: Jesus Christ already bore the penalty, and the Father refines the children He loves. Across an introduction and eleven chapters, it moves through counting it all joy, the furnace of faith, and the seasons when God feels silent, then to Jesus walking in the fire — where the fiery-furnace account is handled carefully: God does not promise every believer the same exit without a mark; He promises His presence in the flames, the promise Hebrews 11 holds alongside saints who endured by faith. Further chapters follow the Holy Spirit as Comforter, what trials produce, the trap of bitterness, enduring to the end, helping others through their trials, and praying through the trial itself. A guide in the introduction sends readers to the chapter that meets them today — Chapter 4 in the trial, Chapter 7 after it, Chapter 10 when someone they love is suffering. The book concludes with “Tested. Proven. Pure.” and a Closing Prayer, calling readers to surrender their verdict about the trial, forgive, and let their comfort become comfort for others. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
