Eternity

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Faith & Doctrine

This life is a doorway, not a destination. Eternity opens with what the Bible says plainly: "the things which are seen are temporal; but the things which are not seen are eternal" (2 Corinthians 4:18, KJV). Everything here has an expiration date — your body, your house, your bank account, your reputation — and one day every person will step through that doorway. The only question that matters is what waits on the other side. This book is about the forever God has prepared for those who belong to Him: not a guess, not a wish, not a hopeful theory built on human imagination, but the settled testimony of God's Word, sealed by the blood of Jesus Christ. Its eleven chapters work through why eternity is certain; setting your mind on things above; the Father's house; Jesus Christ, the way to forever; the Holy Spirit, the seal of eternity; the new heaven and the new earth; the judgment seat of Christ; the other destination, faced honestly; living eternity today; readiness for that day; and the resurrection body. Every chapter begins with Scripture and stays with Scripture, and gives the reader a Scripture to stand on, a truth to settle, and a step to take today. The book closes with a prayer for those who have never trusted Christ and those renewing their surrender. Scripture quotations are from the King James Version of the Bible. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
