Salvation — By Grace, Through Faith. Rescued. Redeemed. Restored. Fourteen chapters answer the most important question you'll ever answer — are you saved? — straight from Scripture: what salvation actually is, why we need saving, what the cross accomplished, how to receive salvation by grace through faith (not by works), repentance and the new birth, assurance, baptism and the saved life, good works, sharing the gospel, and eternal life starting now.
