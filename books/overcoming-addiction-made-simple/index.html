Whom the Son Sets Free Is Free Indeed

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Health & Wellness

Whom the Son Sets Free Is Free Indeed, subtitled Overcoming Addiction, is built on one thesis, stated in its opening pages: the only way to overcome addiction is by the power of the Holy Spirit, when God breaks the stronghold and sets the captive free — not by willpower, not by shame. Its fourteen chapters move from The Truth About Chains through repentance, the renewal of your mind, and walking in the Spirit, to confession, an escape plan, community and accountability, what to do when you fall, and a new identity in Christ. Each chapter is built on a passage of Scripture, including John 8:36 and Zechariah 4:6, and ends with one clear action step. An appendix answers fifteen honest questions, covering rehab, medication, relapse, and cravings. The book is explicit that it does not replace medical detox, a pastor, or a counselor. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
