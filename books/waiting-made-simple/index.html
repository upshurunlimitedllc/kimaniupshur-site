Waiting Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Waiting is a biblical guide to waiting on the Lord with strength. Its introduction, “The In-Between Is Not Empty,” speaks to the reader between a promise and its fulfilment — test results pending, applications under review, a calendar still blank — and insists that God is in the in-between. Its two anchor passages are Isaiah 40:31, on those who wait on the Lord renewing their strength, and Psalm 27:14: wait on the Lord, be of good courage, and He will strengthen your heart. Across fourteen chapters, the book teaches that waiting is not wasting. It follows the night watches and waiting quietly, then walks through Abraham and Sarah waiting on the promise, Joseph waiting in prison, and David anointed but not yet king. It faces the silence of heaven, retells Elijah's seven times of watching for rain, and counts the cost of not waiting, before asking what waiting produces and pointing to the blessed hope. The final chapter rests on Habakkuk 2:3: though it linger, wait for it, because it will certainly come. An appendix of straight answers on waiting, a conclusion titled “Patient. Trusting. Expectant.,” and a closing prayer in the in-between give the reader a way to endure the wait actively. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
