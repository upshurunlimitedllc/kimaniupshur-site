No King in Israel

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Judges is the book nobody wants to preach — and the book everybody needs to read. Assassinations and tent pegs. A fleece and three hundred trumpets. A rash vow and a tragic daughter. A strongman who could tear lions apart but couldn't master his own appetites. A shrine built from stolen silver. A civil war that nearly erased a tribe. The book of Judges is the wildest story in the Bible: raw, violent, uncomfortably honest about what happens when everyone does what is right in their own eyes. But beneath the chaos runs a rhythm you will never forget: Fall. Cry. Deliver. Israel falls into sin. Israel cries out under oppression. God delivers them through flawed, unlikely rescuers — again and again and again. And through it all grows a quiet ache for the one thing Judges never provides: a true King who will not fail. In this accessible twelve-chapter walk through all twenty-one chapters of Judges, Kimani Upshur guides you through every story with honesty and clarity — never glorifying the violence, never minimizing the horror, never forcing neat lessons the text never intended. You'll meet Othniel, Ehud, and Shamgar; Deborah, Barak, and Jael; Gideon and his three hundred; Abimelech the anti-judge; Jephthah and his terrible vow; Samson in all his tragic strength; Micah and his homemade god; and the dark final chapters that show exactly where self-rule leads. Along the way, you'll discover the mercy that won't quit — the God whose heart breaks at the groaning of the oppressed, who cannot bear their misery, who keeps sending rescuers to rebels. And you'll see how every failed judge points to the true King who finally came. Honest about the darkness. Clear about the mercy. Pointed at the King.
