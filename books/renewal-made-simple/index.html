Renewal

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

Christian Living

Renewal is a biblical guide to being made new again in Christ. It opens with a familiar exhaustion: waking up tired after eight hours of sleep — not in the body, but in the soul. Scripture names it the old wearing out, and sets a promise against it: 'the inward man is being renewed day by day' (2 Corinthians 4:16), because God's mercies are new every morning (Lamentations 3:23). Renewal is not self-improvement or a stronger version of you; it is what happens when God makes what is worn and withered new again, beginning with the renewing of the mind (Romans 12:2). Scripture quotations are paraphrased in the author's plain words unless otherwise noted. The book is built for doing: each of its fourteen chapters follows the same pattern — a Scripture anchor, a clear teaching, and a Renewal Practice at the end. The chapters move from the God who makes all things new through new mercies, the Holy Spirit as the agent of renewal, repentance as the doorway, restored years, renewed strength in waiting, the washing Word, worship, forgiveness, and fellowship, to staying fresh in a lifelong rhythm. An appendix of fourteen questions for journaling or small-group discussion, a conclusion, and a closing prayer complete the book. It serves believers who feel stale rather than rebellious, and who want a daily rhythm of renewal rather than a single emotional moment. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
