Government Jobs Made Simple

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

The quiet power of public service, from application to pension. This guide maps the landscape — federal, state, and local — then walks the slow-but-navigable federal hiring process: USAJOBS, the federal resume (a different animal), assessments, interviews, and background checks. You'll get special paths like military and fellowships, honest talk about pay and pensions, thriving in government culture, and a career plan from application to retirement.
