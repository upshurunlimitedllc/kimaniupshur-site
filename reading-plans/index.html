Each plan walks through a few passages of Scripture day by day, with a short teaching, a question to reflect on, and a prayer. Plans are published in English (King James Version) and Spanish (Reina-Valera 1909). Start with any plan, and read at your own pace — nothing to download, nothing to buy, and nothing required.
