The Hem of His Robe

The Woman Who Just Touched

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

Twelve long years — and all it took was a touch. This children's retelling follows the woman who spent everything on doctors, pressed through the crowd thinking 'If I just touch His robe,' and felt healing power flow. When Jesus asks 'Who touched Me?' she discovers that He wanted more than her healing — He wanted her. Each short chapter ends with a simple Do or Pray activity about faith that reaches out.
