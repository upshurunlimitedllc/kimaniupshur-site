A Little Cake First

Elijah and the Widow Who Gave What She Had

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

A handful of meal in a barrel, a little oil in a cruse, and two sticks gathered for what she believes is a last meal — that is where this story begins.

A Little Cake First: Elijah and the Widow Who Gave What She Had walks through 1 Kings 17:8–16 in ten read-aloud chapters, from the dried-up brook where "And the word of the LORD came unto him, saying," to the widow at the gate of Zarephath who gave what she had before she saw how God would provide. Children hear God's "Fear not," watch the widow make the little cake first, and learn that the barrel of meal did not waste and the cruse of oil did not fail, day after day, according to the word of the LORD. Giving is taught as faith in the God who has already spoken — never as a way to buy His help — and the promise is daily bread kept by God's faithfulness, never riches. Every chapter ends with a Do and a Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For younger children ages 6-8.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
