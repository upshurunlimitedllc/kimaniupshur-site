Teach Me Thy Way

Psalm 86 and the United Heart

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

Some prayers ask for things. David's prayer in Psalm 86 asks to be taught.

Some prayers ask for things. David's prayer in Psalm 86 asks to be taught. Teach Me Thy Way: Psalm 86 and the United Heart walks through Psalm 86:1–13 in order, across ten read-aloud chapters — the pupil's side of the desk, a child of God asking, "Teach me thy way, O LORD" — and promising the glad response: "I will walk in thy truth." Children hear David call himself poor and needy and be welcomed, not scolded; they learn that God keeps His servant and shews mercy every day, for "thou, Lord, art good, and ready to forgive". They ask for the undivided heart the lesson needs — a heart united to fear God's name with loving awe, never terror — and they see God's great mercy reach down to the deepest trouble and deliver. Forgiveness here is God's gift through Jesus Christ, never earned by the asking or the walking. Every chapter ends with a Do and a Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For younger children ages 6-8.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
