Let All the People Praise Thee

Psalm 67 and the Blessing That Reaches the Ends of the Earth

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

One short psalm holds one of the biggest prayers in the Bible — a prayer that begins with God's own people and reaches all the way to the ends of the earth.

Let All the People Praise Thee stays inside Psalm 67 — seven verses, taken in order — across ten read-aloud chapters. The prayer opens, "God be merciful unto us, and bless us," but it does not stay small: it asks that God's way be known upon earth and His "saving health among all nations," until all the people praise Him and the whole earth fears Him. Children learn the blessing the way the psalm teaches it — never as a payment God owes, but as a gift to thank Him for — and they learn that fearing God means awe before a great and good God. Every chapter ends with a Do and a Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For younger children ages 6-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
