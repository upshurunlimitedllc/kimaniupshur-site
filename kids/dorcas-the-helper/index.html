Dorcas the Helper

The Woman Who Made Coats

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

The woman who sewed — and the coats that told her story. Dorcas the Helper retells Acts 9:36–42 for children: Dorcas (Tabitha), a disciple in Joppa 'full of good works and acts of charity,' sews garments for the poor and gives away coats and tunics to widows and poor families. In this telling, a nine-year-old girl named Layla watches Dorcas sew, helps deliver the coats, and receives a honey-colored coat with blue buttons of her own (the book's Note to Grown-Ups notes that the Bible does not name the individual recipients of Dorcas's garments). Then Dorcas becomes sick and dies. She is laid in an upstairs room, and the widows show Peter the coats and clothes she made. Peter — summoned from nearby Lydda — sends everyone out, kneels, and prays: 'Tabitha, get up.' She opens her eyes, sits up, and is presented alive, and many in Joppa believe in the Lord. Each of the ten chapters closes with a Do activity, a Pray prayer, and a Treasure Verse keyed to Acts 9 — teaching kids that helping others is how love looks in action. Scripture quotations are from the ESV. The book closes with a Special Invitation to give your life to Jesus. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
