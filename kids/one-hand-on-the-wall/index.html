One Hand on the Wall

Nehemiah 4 and Building While the Enemy Watched

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

The wall was only half built when the laughter started — and the builders kept building, with a trowel in one hand and a sword at their side.

One Hand on the Wall: Nehemiah 4 and Building While the Enemy Watched stays inside one chapter of the Bible — Nehemiah 4, the threatened middle of the building — across ten read-aloud chapters. Children meet Nehemiah leading the families rebuilding Jerusalem's broken wall while Sanballat mocks them as feeble and Tobiah jokes that even a fox could knock their wall down. They hear the mockery turn into conspiracy, and they watch Nehemiah answer the way he always does: he prays, he sets a watch, and he tells tired builders hauling rubbish, "remember the Lord, which is great and terrible." Half the people work while half stand guard, every builder works with one hand on the work and a weapon in the other — and the wall rises anyway, because, as Nehemiah says, "our God shall fight for us." Children learn that mockery often arrives before anything harder does, and that a question meant to shrink you is not the truth about you. Every chapter ends with a simple Do and a short Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For children ages 6-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
