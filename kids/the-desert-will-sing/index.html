The Desert Will Sing

Isaiah 35 and the Highway God Builds Through the Wilderness

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

"The wilderness and the solitary place shall be glad for them; and the desert shall rejoice, and blossom as the rose." The promise is not that the desert will try harder — it is that God Himself will change it.

The Desert Will Sing: Isaiah 35 and the Highway God Builds Through the Wilderness stays inside one chapter — Isaiah 35 — across ten read-aloud chapters, travelling with Amara and Mateo, who live at the edge of the dry land. Children follow the chapter's own movement: the land transformed and blossoming, weak hands strengthened and fearful hearts answered — "he will come and save you" — blind eyes opened and deaf ears unstopped, waters breaking out in the wilderness, and a highway built through it, the way of holiness, until the ransomed come home singing. A desert cannot make itself blossom, and this book never pretends otherwise: every change in it is something God does. The healings are presented as God's promised wonders — begun in the ministry of Jesus and awaiting their fullness when all things are made new — never as a promise that God owes any child an immediate healing on demand, and the overlooked of the chapter, the blind, the deaf, the lame, and the speechless, stand at the front of God's list, where Isaiah puts them. Every chapter ends with a simple Do and a short Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For younger children ages 6-8.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
