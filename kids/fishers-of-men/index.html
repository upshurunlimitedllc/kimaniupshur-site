Fishers of Men

Come Follow Me

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

The true story of the day Jesus called rough fishermen to follow Him — and turned them into fishers of men. Retold carefully from Matthew 4 and Luke 5, children meet Peter's boat, the deep-water catch that filled sinking nets, Peter's 'I'm not good enough,' and Jesus' world-changing invitation: 'Do not be afraid; from now on you will be catching men.' Each chapter ends with a simple Do or Pray for young hearts.
