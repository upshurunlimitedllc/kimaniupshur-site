When the Kindness of God Appeared

Titus 3 and Saved by Mercy, Not by Our Works

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

Everything in this book hangs on two words in the middle of a sentence: "But after that."

When the Kindness of God Appeared: Titus 3 and Saved by Mercy, Not by Our Works unpacks one great sentence of Paul's — Titus 3:4–7 — phrase by phrase and in Paul's own order, across ten read-aloud chapters. Children meet Mara and Jonah inside a letter Paul wrote to a young pastor, and they hear our old story admitted honestly first, exactly as Paul admits it — we were foolish too — before the turn: "But after that the kindness and love of God our Saviour toward man appeared." Then the centre of the sentence, kept at full weight: "Not by works of righteousness which we have done, but according to his mercy he saved us, by the washing of regeneration, and renewing of the Holy Ghost" — the Spirit shed on us abundantly through Jesus Christ our Saviour, until believers stand justified by His grace and made heirs according to the hope of eternal life. The book's one claim is stated as plainly as Paul states it: the cause of salvation is mercy, not works. Every chapter ends with a simple Do and a short Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For older children ages 9-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
