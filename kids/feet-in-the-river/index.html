Feet in the River

Joshua 3 and the Day the Jordan Stood Still

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

The river was at flood, the promise was on the other side — and God's plan began with wet feet.

Feet in the River: Joshua 3 and the Day the Jordan Stood Still stays deliberately inside Joshua 3, and nowhere else, across ten read-aloud chapters. Children camp with Israel beside a Jordan that overfloweth all its banks — close enough to see the promised hills, too flooded to cross — and learn to wait for God's word before anyone splashes in on their own ideas. Then the sequence unfolds in the chapter's own exact order: watch for the ark; hear the promise, that as soon as the soles of the priests' feet "shall rest in the waters of Jordan, that the waters of Jordan shall be cut off"; and watch the priests step in first, carrying the ark of "the Lord of all the earth," before the water moves at all. The priests stand firm in the middle of the riverbed while the whole nation passes over on dry ground: the step is the people's part, and the wonder is entirely God's. The book never turns the crossing into a formula — God freely chose this order for this crossing — and it never hurries past the standing still, which it names as holy, other-serving steadfastness. Every chapter ends with a simple Do and a short Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For children ages 6-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
