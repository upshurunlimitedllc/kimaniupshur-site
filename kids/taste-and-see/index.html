Taste and See

Psalm 34 for Young Tasters

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

"O taste and see that the LORD is good." David did not only sing about God's goodness from far away — he tasted it himself, and he invites every young taster to do the same.

Taste and See: Psalm 34 for Young Tasters walks through Psalm 34:1-10 in the psalm's own order, across ten read-aloud chapters. Children learn to bless the LORD at all times, to pray and be delivered from their fears, to look to God and be lightened, to cry out and be heard, and to trust the God whose angel camps around those who fear Him. The book is honest about how tasting works: God is not a wishing machine, He sometimes answers slowly or differently, and the blessing is found in trusting Him — with His own nearness as the chief gift. Every chapter ends with a simple Do and a short Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For children ages 6-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
