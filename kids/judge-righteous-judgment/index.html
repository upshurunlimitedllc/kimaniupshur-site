Judge Righteous Judgment

John 7 and Seeing Past the Surface

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

"Judge not according to the appearance, but judge righteous judgment."

Judge Righteous Judgment: John 7 and Seeing Past the Surface stays inside John 7:14–24 across ten chapters, with the one work of John 5 told as Jesus Himself tells it. At the feast, the crowds whisper opposite verdicts about Jesus — some said "He is a good man", others that "he deceiveth the people" — both built on surfaces: a healing done on the Sabbath, an unhonoured hometown, no famous schooling. Jesus does the opposite of whispering: He teaches in the open, points to the Father who sent Him, and lays out the evidence from the crowd's own practice — they circumcise on the Sabbath without blame, yet are angry at Him "because I have made a man every whit whole on the sabbath day". Then He gives the rule whole, and this book carries it through the doorway of today's feeds, clips, and headlines: the pause between seeing and deciding, one true scale for friend and stranger alike, deeds weighed honestly and hearts left to God — firm about truth, gentle with people, cruel to no one. Judging righteous judgment is never condemnation of persons; it is the refusal to sentence anyone for the side of them you happened to see. Every chapter ends with a Do and a Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For older children ages 9-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
