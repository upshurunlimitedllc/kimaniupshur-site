Prepared Abundantly

1 Chronicles 22 and the House David Gathered For but Would Not Build

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

King David longed to build God's house more than anything — and God told him no. So the old king spent his last strength on the next best thing: he got everything ready for the son who would build it.

Prepared Abundantly: 1 Chronicles 22 and the House David Gathered For but Would Not Build stays inside 1 Chronicles 22 across ten chapters — the chapter of the great getting-ready. Children watch David name the place before a single wall rises: “This is the house of the LORD God, and this is the altar of the burnt offering for Israel.” Then comes the gathering — masons and hewn stone, iron and brass too heavy to weigh, cedar in abundance from far away — until the Bible sums the old king up in one heartbeat of a sentence: “So David prepared abundantly before his death.” The book's second half follows David's charge to young Solomon: the desire that was in David's mind and the word of the LORD that refused it, the promised son of rest who would build the house, the blessing and the prayer for wisdom, the command to be strong and of good courage, and the final call that still rings: “Arise therefore, and be doing.” Children learn that preparing is not failing — that gathering, blessing, and charging the next builder is faithfulness in its own right, even for a work you will never finish or see. Every chapter ends with a Do and a Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For older children ages 9-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
