We Will Drink No Wine

Jeremiah 35 and the Family That Kept Its Father's Word

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

"We will drink no wine" — four short words, spoken by a whole family with one voice.

We Will Drink No Wine: Jeremiah 35 and the Family That Kept Its Father's Word walks through Jeremiah 35 in order, across ten chapters. God sends Jeremiah to fetch the Rechabites — a tent-dwelling family, not kings or priests — into a chamber of His own house, and to set pots full of wine and cups before them. The family refuses, calmly and together: their forefather Jonadab had commanded his sons to drink no wine, build no houses, sow no seed, and plant no vineyards — and generations later, his descendants still keep his word, dwelling in tents all their days. Then God holds the family up before a nation that would not listen to Him: "The words of Jonadab the son of Rechab, that he commanded his sons not to drink wine, are performed" — "but this people hath not hearkened unto me". The book ends with God's free promise to the family, grace and never wages: "Jonadab the son of Rechab shall not want a man to stand before me for ever." Obedience in this book is the glad response of God's people, never a way to earn His love; salvation is His free gift through Jesus Christ. Every chapter ends with a Do and a Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For older children ages 9-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
