My Heart Is Fixed

Psalm 57 and Singing in the Cave

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

David sang in the cave before the rescue came. His heart made its decision in the dark — and the singing followed.

My Heart Is Fixed: Psalm 57 and Singing in the Cave walks through David's cave song verse by verse, across ten read-aloud chapters. Children meet David in a hard season: hunted by King Saul, hidden in a cave — a hiding place, not a home — in the days the psalm's own heading names, "when he fled from Saul in the cave." The song starts low, asking God for mercy; it hides in the shadow of His wings; it cries out to God Most High; and it names the hard things honestly — nets, pits, and a bowed-down soul. Then, right in the middle of the psalm, the heart stands up: "My heart is fixed, O God, my heart is fixed: I will sing and give praise." Children learn what fixed means — a heart set on God like a tent peg driven deep, because of who God is and not because circumstances have improved — and they follow the song upward until David is no longer looking at the cave at all, but praying, "Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens." One promise is never made, and the book says so plainly: singing does not make trouble go away. The psalm ends with God exalted, not with Saul defeated — and praise is chosen before the rescue arrives. Every chapter ends with a simple Do and a short Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For children ages 6-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
