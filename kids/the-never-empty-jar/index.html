The Never-Empty Jar

The Widow's Miracle

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

Nothing left — until she cried out. This children's retelling of the widow's oil follows her desperate plea, Elisha's strange question 'What do you have?', borrowed jars by the dozen, the shut door, and the pour that wouldn't stop. Each short chapter ends with a simple Do or Pray activity, teaching kids that God multiplies what we give Him.
