Andrew Ran to Tell

John 1 and the First Disciple Who Brought Somebody

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

Andrew was not the loudest disciple, and he is not the most famous. He is the one who brought his brother.

Andrew Ran to Tell: John 1 and the First Disciple Who Brought Somebody stays inside one passage — John 1:35-42 — across ten read-aloud chapters, and stops exactly where the passage stops. Children stand by the Jordan with two of John the Baptist's disciples and learn that everything in this story starts with listening. They hear John say, "Behold the Lamb of God," and watch the two follow Jesus: "What seek ye?" — "Rabbi, where dwellest thou?" — "Come and see." Andrew spends the day with Jesus, and then the book shows the first thing he does with it: he runs to find his own brother Simon and brings him to Jesus, where Simon receives a new name — Cephas, "A stone." The pattern is the passage's own: listen, follow, abide, tell, bring. And its comfort is plain for every child who is not loud, not grown-up, and not sure they know enough: sharing Jesus requires knowing one thing for sure, and loving the person nearest you. Every chapter ends with a simple Do and a short Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For children ages 6-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
