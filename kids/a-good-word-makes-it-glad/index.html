A Good Word Makes It Glad

Proverbs 12 and Medicine for a Heavy Heart

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

Some mornings a heart feels like a wet coat — heavy, droopy, and hard to carry around. Proverbs has a verse for exactly those mornings, and it is short enough to carry in your pocket.

A Good Word Makes It Glad: Proverbs 12 and Medicine for a Heavy Heart lives inside a single verse — Proverbs 12:25 — across ten chapters, taking it apart piece by piece the way you would take apart a clock to see how it works: “Heaviness in the heart of man maketh it stoop: but a good word maketh it glad.” Children follow Maya, a seven-year-old with two braids who loves drawing, puddles, and her grandmother's kitchen, as she lives every piece of the verse: what heaviness feels like, how it makes a heart stoop, the little turning word “but,” and what a good word really is — true, kind, and spoken on purpose by someone who noticed. This is not a book about pleasant talk in general; it stays with the verse's own diagnosis and dose — heaviness named without shame, and gladness that rises by stages in the very same heart. By the last chapter, children are learning the whole verse by heart, so the medicine is theirs to carry — for their own heavy days, and for the heavy days of everyone they love. Every chapter ends with a Do and a Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For younger children ages 6-8.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
