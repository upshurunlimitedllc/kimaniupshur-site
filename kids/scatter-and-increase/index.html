Scatter and Increase

Proverbs 11 and the Hand That Grows by Giving

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

Some verses sound backwards the first time you hear them — scatter what you have, and somehow end up with more.

Scatter and Increase: Proverbs 11 and the Hand That Grows by Giving lives inside one surprising verse: "There is that scattereth, and yet increaseth; and there is that withholdeth more than is meet, but it tendeth to poverty." Across ten read-aloud chapters, children meet two hands — the open hand that scatters seed wide, and the closed hand that holds more than is meet — and learn through the verse's own sowing pictures that seed is not lost when it leaves the hand, that a field can be full from scattered seed, and that God's arithmetic is different from ours. The book teaches the paradox of Proverbs 11:24 alone: the open hand as obedience and trust, never as a way of getting things back. Every chapter ends with a Do and a Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For younger children ages 6-8.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
