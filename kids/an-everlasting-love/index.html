An Everlasting Love

Jeremiah 31 and a Love That Does Not End

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

"Yea, I have loved thee with an everlasting love." God did not say that to people who felt loved — He said it to His people far from home.

An Everlasting Love: Jeremiah 31 and a Love That Does Not End is built on one verse — Jeremiah 31:3 — and on that verse alone as its claim from Jeremiah 31, across ten read-aloud chapters. Children sit with Amara and Josiah among the exiles far from home and hear the LORD's own declaration at full strength: "The LORD hath appeared of old unto me, saying, Yea, I have loved thee with an everlasting love: therefore with lovingkindness have I drawn thee." They learn what everlasting means — a love with no end date, not merely a long-lasting one — and how that love does its work: drawn, not dragged, the way Hosea pictured it, with cords of a man and bands of love. Verses from Deuteronomy, Psalms, Isaiah, Hosea, Lamentations, John, Romans, and 1 John gather around the anchor, and the book stays honest to the last chapter: feelings change, but God's love rests on His declaration, not on a mood — His or ours. Every chapter ends with a simple Do and a short Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For older children ages 9-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
