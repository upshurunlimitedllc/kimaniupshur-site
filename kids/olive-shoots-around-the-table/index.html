Olive Shoots Around the Table

Psalm 128 and the Blessings of a God-Fearing Home

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

"Like olive plants round about thy table." Psalm 128 is a road song — a psalm of only six verses with a whole blessed home inside it.

Olive Shoots Around the Table: Psalm 128 and the Blessings of a God-Fearing Home walks through the whole psalm slowly, verse by verse, the way pilgrim families sang it — a Song of degrees — on the road up to Jerusalem. Children meet the psalm's portrait of a God-fearing home: one who fears the LORD and walks in His ways, work that feeds a family — "happy shalt thou be, and it shall be well with thee" — a wife like a fruitful vine, children like olive plants around the table, the good of the city, children's children gathered close, and "peace upon Israel." The book teaches the psalm as what it is: the pattern of God's blessing in His good design, a picture to love, to thank God for, and to grow toward — not a promise that every family will match it in every detail, and families whose pictures differ, in shape, season, or sorrow, are addressed with deliberate kindness. Every chapter ends with a simple Do and a short Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For younger children ages 6-8.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
