This Is the Day

Psalm 118 and Rejoicing in the Day the LORD Has Made

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

This Is the Day — Psalm 118 and Rejoicing in the Day the LORD Has Made — tells Psalm 118:24 for younger children (about ages 6–8), opening the verse up slowly, word by word: "This is the day which the LORD hath made; we will rejoice and be glad in it." This — today, the day the reader is standing in, not someday. The day which the LORD hath made — a day as a made thing, crafted on purpose by the LORD, never an accident or a leftover. And we will rejoice and be glad in it — rejoicing as the reader's chosen answer, given inside the day itself, even on a hard day. Every supporting word comes from Psalm 118 alone: giving thanks to the LORD morning by morning, the LORD as strength and song, His light, and trusting in Him rather than in people. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For children ages 6-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
