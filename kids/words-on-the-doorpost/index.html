Words on the Doorpost

God's Words in Our House

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

Some words are too important to hear once and forget. God told His people to keep His words on their hearts, talk about them all day long, and write them on the very doorposts of their houses.

Words on the Doorpost: God's Words in Our House follows Deuteronomy 6:4-9 in its own order, across ten read-aloud chapters, with Amara (7) and Eli (6) and their parents carrying the rhythm into an ordinary family's day: hearing that the LORD our God is one LORD, loving Him with all the heart, teaching His words diligently, and talking of them when you sit in the house, when you walk by the way, when you lie down, and when you rise up. Deuteronomy 11:18-20 closes the book with the same charge repeated — God's words laid up in the heart like treasure. Every chapter ends with a simple Do and a short Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For children ages 6-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
