Moses Came Down Shining

Exodus 34 and the Face That Shone Because He Had Talked with God

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

Moses came down the mountain carrying the tables of stone — and he was the very last person in Israel to find out what had happened to his face.

Moses Came Down Shining: Exodus 34 and the Face That Shone Because He Had Talked with God stays inside Exodus 34:29–35 across ten read-aloud chapters, and says only what the passage itself says. Children hear the long, surprising sentence the whole book is built on: Moses "wist not that the skin of his face shone while he talked with him." They watch Aaron and all the children of Israel see the shining and be afraid to come near, hear Moses call to them and tell them everything the LORD had spoken, and follow the quiet rhythm of the vail — the KJV's own spelling, kept throughout — on when Moses spoke with the people, and off when he went in before the LORD, again and again. The book adds nothing to the wonder: no mechanism, no claim about how or why beyond the text's own words, "while he talked with him." What children carry away is twofold — the humility of a man who was the last to know what God had done in him, and the pattern of a life that goes in before the LORD with nothing hidden and comes out to people with His words. Every chapter ends with a simple Do and a short Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For younger children ages 6-8.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
