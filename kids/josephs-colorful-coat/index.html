Joseph's Colorful Coat

God's Plan Is Bigger Than the Pit

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

The coat of many colors was only the beginning. This children's retelling follows Joseph the dreamer — from his father's favor to the pit, from betrayal to being sold — the first chapters of a story about God's faithfulness in hard places. Each short chapter ends with a simple Do or Pray activity for young hearts learning that God is with us even when life isn't fair.
