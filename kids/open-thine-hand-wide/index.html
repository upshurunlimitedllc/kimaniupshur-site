A hand can shut — or it can open wide. Open Thine Hand Wide: Deuteronomy 15 and the Hand That Must Not Shut stays inside five verses, Deuteronomy 15:7–11, taken in order across ten read-aloud chapters. A poor brother stands at the gate, and God's command is plain: do not harden your heart, do not shut your hand — "open thine hand wide" — and give him "sufficient for his need." Children learn to give without a grieving heart, and to hear why the giving never runs out of people to help: "the poor shall never cease out of the land." The blessing God puts on open hands is taught as His free kindness, never a payment or a bargain — and the poor brother is a brother throughout, treated with full dignity: having little is not a shame, and needing help is not a failure. Every chapter ends with a Do and a Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For younger children ages 6-8.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
