Manna in the Morning

How God Fed His People in the Desert

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

Fresh out of Egypt, God's people were hungry in an empty desert — and grumbling. Manna in the Morning retells Exodus 16 for children ages 6–12 in faithful paraphrase: the impossible promise of bread rained from heaven, the ground covered in sweet white flakes at sunrise, the lesson of gathering only today's portion, the double gift on the sixth day, and forty years of mornings God never missed. Each of the ten short chapters ends with a simple Do or Pray, making it a perfect breakfast-time read-aloud. A gentle, wonder-filled story that teaches worried hearts to trust God one day at a time.
