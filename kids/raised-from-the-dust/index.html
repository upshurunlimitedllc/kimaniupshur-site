Raised from the Dust

Psalm 113 and the LORD Who Stoops to Lift

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

The highest God in heaven stoops down — and lifts the poor out of the dust.

Raised from the Dust: Psalm 113 and the LORD Who Stoops to Lift walks through all nine verses of Psalm 113 in order, across ten read-aloud chapters. The psalm begins with praise before anyone is in trouble — "Praise ye the LORD. Praise, O ye servants of the LORD, praise the name of the LORD" — a name blessed from this time forth and for evermore, praised from the rising of the sun to its going down. Then the book asks the psalm's own question, "Who is like unto the LORD our God, who dwelleth on high?", and follows the LORD who humbleth Himself to behold the things that are in heaven and in the earth — who "raiseth up the poor out of the dust," lifteth the needy out of the dunghill, and sets him with princes, and who makes an empty house a joyful mother of children. The lifting in this book is the psalm's own: from lowliness, not from danger — and God is praised first simply because of who He is. Every chapter ends with a Do and a Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For younger children ages 6-8.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
