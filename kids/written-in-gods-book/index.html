Written in God's Book

Malachi 3 and the Scroll of Remembrance

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

In a town where talk about God had grown tired and complaining, a few people kept speaking of Him faithfully — and heaven was listening, and heaven kept what it heard.

Written in God's Book: Malachi 3 and the Scroll of Remembrance stays inside Malachi 3:16–18 across ten chapters. Around the faithful remnant, the community's words have soured — serving God, people say, gains nothing; the proud seem to do well and the wrongdoers seem to escape. But "they that feared the LORD spake often one to another" — and the book follows God's response, link by link: "the LORD hearkened, and heard" them; "and a book of remembrance was written before him for them that feared the LORD," and for them that thought upon His name; and the promise that on the day He makes up His jewels, they shall be His, spared as a father spares his own serving son. The sparing is a Father's free mercy, never a wage earned by good conversation. And the ending distinction — "Then shall ye return, and discern between the righteous and the wicked, between him that serveth God and him that serveth him not" — is God's to draw on His own day; children are taught to leave the final sorting to Him and spend their energy on the remnant's pattern instead: fearing the LORD, thinking upon His name, and speaking often one to another. No readable contents are invented for God's book, in words or in pictures — Scripture gives it none, and its pages are depicted blank throughout. Every chapter ends with a Do and a Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For older children ages 9-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
