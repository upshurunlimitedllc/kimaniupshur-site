Be Strong and Courageous

Joshua 1 and the New Leader

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

Moses is dead, and the Jordan is still ahead. Be Strong and Courageous stays inside Joshua 1:1–9 — the commissioning moment alone — as God speaks to Joshua, Moses' successor: the promise that every place the sole of his foot treads is given to him; the borders from the wilderness to the great sea; and the promise underneath the whole charge, “as I was with Moses, so I will be with thee: I will not fail thee, nor forsake thee.” Three times the charge comes — “Be strong and of a good courage” — tied to observing the law without turning to the right hand or the left, and to keeping the book of the law in his mouth, meditating on it day and night. There is no Jericho campaign and no conquest narrative here; the book lives in the charge itself and the reason inside it: “for the LORD thy God is with thee whithersoever thou goest.” Moses is honoured in memory and never presented as a living actor, and God is presented by light and voice only, never as a depicted figure. Every quotation is taken word-for-word from the King James Version, each a verbatim part of one single verse. Every chapter ends with a Do and a Pray — one simple action, and one short prayer in quotes to read together. For children ages 6-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
