Philip and the Ethiopian

The Chariot Ride That Changed Everything

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

Philip and the Ethiopian retells Acts 8:26–39, the chariot ride where a confused reader met a ready explainer and the good news about Jesus changed everything. In this telling a nine-year-old boy named Kofi rides along beside the Ethiopian official — A Note to Grown-Ups discloses that the Bible does not mention a boy in the story, while every event follows Acts: the angel's direction to the desert road, the official reading Isaiah's scroll, Philip running to the chariot, the question 'Do you understand what you are reading?', Philip telling about Jesus, the water, and the baptism, until the official goes on his way rejoicing. The ten chapters each end with a 'Do' section and a 'Pray' section and a Treasure Verse that names the passage it comes from; the book's Scripture Note for Families states that the story is told in faithful paraphrase in simple words for young readers, and its copyright page states that Scripture quotations are from the ESV® Bible (The Holy Bible, English Standard Version®). The book closes with 'A Special Invitation: Give Your Life to Jesus', including The Sinner's Prayer and a 'What Comes Next?' section. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
