We Cannot But Speak

Acts 4 and Peter and John Before the Council

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

A man who had never walked was leaping in the temple — and the men who healed him spent the night in a cell for it.

We Cannot But Speak: Acts 4 and Peter and John Before the Council retells Acts 4 across ten read-aloud chapters, heard at a family kitchen table where Dad reads Acts aloud to Micah and Selah. Children meet the man lame from birth, carried every day to the temple gate called Beautiful, and watch Peter — who had no silver and no gold to give — lift him up in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth. Then they follow Peter and John into trouble: arrested for teaching that Jesus had risen, set in the midst of the council, and filled with the Holy Ghost to answer. Peter's words carry the whole book: the stone the builders set at nought has become the head of the corner; there is no other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved; and when the council commands them to stop speaking, the fishermen answer, "We can not but speak the things which we have seen and heard." Children learn why people who have been with Jesus speak about Him, even when it costs them. Every chapter ends with a simple Do and a short Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For children ages 6-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
