The Idol That Fell Down

Dagon on His Face

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

The Philistines thought they had captured God. They carried His ark into their temple like a trophy and set it beside their idol — and in the morning, the idol was on the floor.

The Idol That Fell Down: Dagon on His Face retells 1 Samuel 5:1-7 across ten read-aloud chapters. Set beside Dagon in the night, the ark of God needs no army and no helpers: on the first morning the statue lies face-down before it, and on the second morning it lies fallen and broken — because Dagon is only a statue, a thing of stone made by men's hands that cannot see, hear, speak, or help. The men of Ashdod reach the story's conclusion themselves: the hand of God is heavy here. Children carry away the book's one great truth: there is no god like God. Every chapter ends with a simple Do and a short Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For children ages 6-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
