What's Heaven Like?

What the Bible Says About Our Forever Home

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

What is heaven really like — and who will be there? This book answers children’s biggest questions straight from the Bible: a Father’s house with many rooms, a city of gold with gates of pearl, a crystal river and the tree of life, and best of all, Jesus Himself, waiting with open arms. It speaks honestly and gently about tears, death, and the people we miss, and shows children the simple way home: not by being perfect, but by believing in Jesus. Each short chapter ends with a prayer to repeat or one small thing to do, giving kids ages 6–12 a forever home to hope for — and little tastes of it to enjoy today.
