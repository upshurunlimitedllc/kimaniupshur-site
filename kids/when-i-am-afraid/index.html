When I Am Afraid

Psalm 56 and David's Words for Scared Days

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

When I Am Afraid — Psalm 56 and David's Words for Scared Days — gives children David's own sentence for fear: “What time I am afraid, I will trust in thee.” David was afraid in Gath, and he said so. Across ten chapters the book walks through Psalm 56:3–4 and 8–11 with named, ordinary fears — a move, a new school, a clinic visit, being lost at the market — and the tenderness of a God who keeps our wanderings and puts our tears into His bottle. Fear is met with trust here, never scolded as a failure of faith: David trusted God and cried, in the same psalm, in the same breath. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For children ages 6–12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
