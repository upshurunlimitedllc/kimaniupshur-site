Help Us, O LORD Our God

2 Chronicles 14 and Asa's Prayer When the Army Was Too Big

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

An army of a million men was marching toward Judah, and King Asa's whole army was barely half its size. Asa had prepared for years for a day like this — and when it came, he did not trust his preparation. He prayed.

Help Us, O LORD Our God: 2 Chronicles 14 and Asa's Prayer When the Army Was Too Big walks through 2 Chronicles 14 across ten chapters, with its centre at one of the great prayers of the Bible. Children first meet Asa in the quiet years — ten years of rest in which “Asa did that which was good and right in the eyes of the LORD his God,” clearing away the wrong altars and building fenced cities while the land had rest. His mustered army of five hundred and eighty thousand was a prepared army, and that is exactly what makes his prayer so strong when Zerah's thousand thousand came down into the valley of Zephathah: “LORD, it is nothing with thee to help, whether with many, or with them that have no power: help us, O LORD our God; for we rest on thee, and in thy name we go against this multitude.” Preparation had met its limit, and Asa rested on God instead of arithmetic. The battle's outcome is told briefly and gently, at the text's own weight — the LORD smote, the Ethiopians fled, and Judah came home. Children learn that praying like Asa means working honestly with what God has given, then trusting God and not the numbers when the trouble is too big. Every chapter ends with a Do and a Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For older children ages 9-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
