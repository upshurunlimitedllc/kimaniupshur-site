God Hears You

Talking to God Anytime

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

The best listener is always listening. This gentle children's book teaches kids they can talk to God anytime, anywhere, about anything — with chapters on praise (telling God He's great), sorry (the clean-heart prayer), thanks (the happy prayer), and asking boldly. Each chapter ends with a simple Do or Pray activity, because prayer is something you practice, not just something you learn.
