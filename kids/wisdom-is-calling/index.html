Wisdom Is Calling

Proverbs 8 and the Voice at the Crossroads

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

Wisdom does not whisper or hide — she stands in the open places and calls out loud, and children can learn to know her voice.

Wisdom Is Calling: Proverbs 8 and the Voice at the Crossroads stays inside Proverbs 8:1–11 and 32–36, where Wisdom speaks in her own voice, across ten read-aloud chapters. Children meet her where she stands — in the high places, at the gates of the city, at the crossroads of a noisy street full of competing voices — calling to everyone, speaking excellent things and a mouth that speaks truth without twisting, and pricing her gift above silver, choice gold, and rubies: "For wisdom is better than rubies." She is pictured the way Proverbs pictures her — God's wisdom in a woman's voice, so that children can learn to recognize her — never presented as God, and never worshiped; the favour promised at the chapter's end is "favour of the LORD." The closing verses are carried at full weight: "Blessed is the man that heareth me, watching daily at my gates," the promise that "whoso findeth me findeth life, and shall obtain favour of the LORD," and — gently, but without removal — the solemn warning at the close, set before children as a protective fence rather than a threat. Every chapter ends with a Do and a Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For younger children ages 6-8.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
