The Empty Tomb

Easter Morning Surprise

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

The Empty Tomb tells the resurrection story of John 20:1–18 for children, just as Mary Magdalene lived it. A Note to Grown-Ups opens the book, and then the story begins on the saddest morning: Mary comes to the tomb and finds the stone gone. Children run with her to tell the disciples, watch the race to the tomb, and see the folded cloth. They stay with Mary as she weeps, meet the two angels, and hear the "gardener" speak her name. The book gives Mary a companion for the road: Zara, a nine-year-old girl who helps her carry the burial spices — a storytelling friend the Note to Grown-Ups openly explains, since the Bible doesn't name a child at the tomb. Ten short chapters carry the story from "The Saddest Morning" to "I Have Seen the Lord." Every chapter has a Treasure Verse, one illustration, and ends with something to Do and a prayer to Pray. The story is retold in faithful paraphrase, with one short verse quoted word-for-word from the ESV. At the back, A Special Invitation: Give Your Life to Jesus presents the gospel for young readers, with The Sinner's Prayer and simple What Comes Next steps. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
