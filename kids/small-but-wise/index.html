Small but Wise

Proverbs 30 and Four Little Teachers

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

"What can you do? You're so little." Agur answers with four teachers — every one of them smaller than a child's hand.

Small but Wise: Proverbs 30 and Four Little Teachers is built on Proverbs 30:24-28, and on those five verses alone at its centre, across ten read-aloud chapters. Maya, who is seven and the smallest person in her class, meets the four little teachers in her Grandma Rose's garden: "There be four things which are little upon the earth, but they are exceeding wise:" Children study them one at a time — the ants, "a people not strong, yet they prepare their meat in the summer" (preparation); the conies, a feeble folk who make their houses in the rocks (shelter); the locusts, who have no king, yet go forth all of them by bands (teamwork); and the spider, who takes hold with her hands and is in kings' palaces (quiet industry). Each creature keeps exactly the trait its own verse gives it — no trait is borrowed from another — and the book is careful about what it is not: Agur's chapter, not the sluggard's rebuke. Wisdom itself is the LORD's gift, and small is not the same as foolish. Every chapter ends with a simple Do and a short Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For younger children ages 6-8.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
