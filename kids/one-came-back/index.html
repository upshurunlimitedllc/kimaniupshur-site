One Came Back

Luke 17 and the Ten Who Were Healed and the One Who Returned

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

"Were there not ten cleansed? but where are the nine?" Ten men were healed on the road to Jerusalem — and only one of them turned around.

One Came Back: Luke 17 and the Ten Who Were Healed and the One Who Returned stays inside Luke 17:11–19 across ten read-aloud chapters. Ten men with leprosy stand far away — separated from home and family — and cry out, "Jesus, Master, have mercy on us." Jesus sends them to the priests before anything has changed, and "as they went, they were cleansed." Then the book slows down where Luke slows down: "one of them, when he saw that he was healed, turned back," and "fell down on his face at his feet, giving him thanks" — "and he was a Samaritan," a stranger. Children hear Jesus' question, and His word to the one who returned: "thy faith hath made thee whole." The nine are never mocked in this telling; they are a mirror, not a joke, and the story's invitation is not shame but return. The hard edges are handled at the text's own weight for the youngest readers, and every chapter ends with a Do and a Pray. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For younger children ages 6-8.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
