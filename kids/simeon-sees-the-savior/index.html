Simeon Sees the Savior

The Old Man Who Waited

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

Simeon Sees the Savior retells Luke 2:25–35 for children. Simeon is the righteous old man who waited his whole life for God's promise — the Holy Spirit had told him he would not die before he had seen the Christ — and a seven-year-old boy named Pablo, the book's young witness, watches wide-eyed as the promise comes true. Across ten short chapters the story moves from The Old Man Who Waited and God's Promise to The Special Day, Into the Temple, and A Baby Arrives, until Simeon takes the child Up in His Arms and sings, My Eyes Have Seen! The closing chapters, A Light for Everyone, Mary Marvels, and Waiting Is Never Wasted, draw out the book's refrain: waiting on God is never wasted. Every chapter ends with both a Do activity and a Pray prayer, plus a Treasure Verse naming its passage. Scripture quotations are from the ESV® Bible; the story itself is told in faithful paraphrase, as the book discloses. The book closes with A Special Invitation: Give Your Life to Jesus, including the Sinner's Prayer. Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
