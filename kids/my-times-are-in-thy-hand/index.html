My Times Are in Thy Hand

Psalm 31 and Trust Measure in Seasons

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

David prayed Psalm 31 with enemies all around him and a net hidden on his path — and in the middle of the trouble he handed God the thing we most want to keep for ourselves: his times.

My Times Are in Thy Hand: Psalm 31 and Trust Measure in Seasons walks through Psalm 31 across ten chapters, in the psalm's own movement from trouble to trust. Children meet a psalm full of trouble first — sickness, sorrow, enemies, a net laid secretly in the path — and hear David's cry, “Pull me out of the net,” answered by his surrender: “Into thine hand I commit my spirit: thou hast redeemed me, O LORD God of truth.” At the psalm's centre stands the sentence the book is named for: “My times are in thy hand: deliver me from the hand of mine enemies, and from them that persecute me.” This is not a lesson about schedules; it is a transfer of ownership, signed in the middle of trouble — seasons of waiting, seasons of hiding in the secret of God's presence, seasons of courage, all of them held. God's hand in this book is the psalm's own picture of care that holds and carries; the Father Himself is never pictured, only His keeping. The psalm ends by turning its trust outward into an invitation — “O love the LORD, all ye his saints” — and a charge for every reader still in a hard season: “Be of good courage, and he shall strengthen your heart, all ye that hope in the LORD.” Every chapter ends with a Do and a Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For older children ages 9-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
