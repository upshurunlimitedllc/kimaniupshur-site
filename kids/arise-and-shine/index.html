Arise and Shine

Isaiah 60 and the Glory That Rises on God's People

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

“Arise, shine.” God speaks two small words to a city sitting in the dark — and the light He promises is a sunrise nobody has to build.

Arise and Shine: Isaiah 60 and the Glory That Rises on God’s People stays inside Isaiah 60, with its anchor at verses 1–3 and 19–20, across ten read-aloud chapters. Children meet a city sitting low in real darkness — trouble, sadness, and a future hard to see — and hear God’s promise spoken over it before the people have fixed anything: “Arise, shine; for thy light is come, and the glory of the LORD is risen upon thee.” The book presses Isaiah’s order at every turn: God rises, then His people arise; God shines, then His people shine — the city’s shining is only ever the reflection of a glory that rises on it from outside itself. The nations come to the light, kings and children come home, gates stand open that never close, and the song ends where Isaiah ends it: the sun no longer needed, and mourning finished. This is Isaiah’s own light — not the creation light of Genesis 1 and not the Light of John 1, which belong to other books. Every chapter ends with a simple Do and a short Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For older children ages 9-12.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
