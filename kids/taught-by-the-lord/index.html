Taught by the LORD

Isaiah 54 and the Children Whose Peace Is Great

by Kimani Upshur, M.Ed.

For Children • Ages 6–12

The Teacher of God's children is God Himself — and the peace He promises them is great.

Taught by the LORD: Isaiah 54 and the Children Whose Peace Is Great walks through Isaiah 54:10–14 across ten read-aloud chapters, with verse 13 at its heart: "And all thy children shall be taught of the LORD; and great shall be the peace of thy children." Everything stands on verse 10's ground: the covenant of peace holds even if the mountains depart, resting on the LORD "that hath mercy on thee" — never on a child's performance. Children see the city the chapter pictures — stones with fair colours, foundations of sapphires, windows of agates, gates of carbuncles, borders of pleasant stones — framed always as the text's pictured promise, a portrait of the completeness of God's restoring love, never as a claim about where children live today. They hear verse 14's settled will: established in righteousness, far from oppression, free from fear. And the chapter's seal is taught carefully: "No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper" means that opposition will not finally succeed against God's people — not that it will never form. The Teacher is God Himself, by His Word and His Spirit; parents, pastors, and teachers are His honoured instruments, the people who read the Word aloud and walk children to it — never His replacements. Every chapter ends with a Do and a Pray to share together. Every Scripture quotation is word-for-word from the King James Version (KJV). The book closes with a special invitation for children to give their lives to Jesus. For younger children ages 6-8.

Written from a Christian worldview — Scripture first.
