For Churches & Small Groups

Upshur Unlimited, LLC was founded on one principle: deliverance and redemption by Jesus Christ. These collections were written to put short, Scripture-rooted teaching into the hands of the whole church.

Each complete box set gathers ten Made Simple books in one purchase — $39.99 a set. Churches equip their people the simple way: every member gets their own copy, about $4 a book, so the whole class or group reads and keeps the same teaching. At Christmas, a set is a gift a church can give its teachers, its teams, and its newest believers — one that keeps teaching long after the season ends.
