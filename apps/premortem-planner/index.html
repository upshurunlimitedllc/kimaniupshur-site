Pre-Mortem Planner

It's one year from now and your project has failed. Write the story of why — while there's still time to prevent it. Then turn every cause into a preventive action with an owner and a deadline.

Step 1 — The project

What are we stress-testing?

Name the project or plan you want to protect, and the date it's supposed to succeed by.

Step 2 — The failure story

It failed. Here's why.

Imagine the target date has passed and the project is dead. Be brutally honest — optimism is not allowed in this step. Answer these in your story:

  • What went wrong first?
  • What warning signs did everyone ignore?
  • What finally killed it?

Step 3 — The causes

What caused the failure?

Pull the distinct causes out of your story. List at least 3 — one per line of thinking. Be specific: "we ran out of money" beats "things went badly."

Step 4 — Prevention

Turn each cause into a plan

For every imagined cause of failure, write one preventive action, name an owner, and set a deadline (before your target date). A cause without an owner is a wish.
