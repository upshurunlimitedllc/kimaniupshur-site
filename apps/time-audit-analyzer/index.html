Time Audit Analyzer

Find out where your hours actually go, score how your time aligns with your priorities, and build a concrete reallocation plan.

Step 1 of 3

Your top 3 priorities

Name the three things your time should be serving. Be specific ("finish the novel draft", not "writing").

Step 2 of 3

Where do your hours go?

Enter your best estimate of hours per week for each category. Use a real 7-day log if you have one, or reconstruct a typical week from estimates — honest guesses beat no data.

Weekly total: 0 of 168 hours
Step 3 of 3

What does each category serve?

For each category with hours logged, say which priority it mostly serves. "Maintenance" = necessary upkeep (sleep, chores). "Drain" = adds little to what matters.
