If you may act on thoughts of harming yourself

Please reach out right now — you matter, and help is available:

  • Call or text 988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US, 24/7)
  • Text HOME to 741741 — Crisis Text Line
  • Call 911 if you are in immediate danger

Dark days are real — and so is God’s nearness in them. Let these anchors hold you.
