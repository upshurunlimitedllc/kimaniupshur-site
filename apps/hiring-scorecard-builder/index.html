Hiring Scorecard Builder

Hire on evidence instead of gut feel. Define what the role truly needs, score every candidate against the same criteria with the same scale, then compare — and check your biases before you decide.

Step 1 — Define the role's must-haves

List 5–7 must-have criteria. Weights show what matters most — they don't need to total 100; the app normalizes them.

Step 2 — Score each candidate

Score from interview evidence only — what they showed and said, not the résumé. Pick one rating per criterion; the anchors below the scale describe what each level looks like. Add evidence notes to defend each score later.
