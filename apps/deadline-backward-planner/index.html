Deadline Backward Planner

Name the deliverable and its immovable deadline. This tool schedules every phase backward from that date — with an automatic 20% buffer — so you finish early, not late.

Step 1 — The deadline

What must be delivered, and when?

Days reserved at the very end for final review and fixes. (Enter 0 to skip.)

Step 2 — The phases

Break the work into phases

List the major phases in the order you will do them, with your honest estimate of working days for each. The tool schedules them backward from the deadline and inserts a buffer automatically.

Step 3 — Your reverse timeline

Your plan

Read top-down: the deadline is at the top, the start date at the bottom. Assumes calendar days. Buffer blocks are highlighted.

Scope-cut options — reclaim days

Tick phases to cut, or shrink the buffer, then recalculate. Cutting scope is a last resort; do it deliberately, not by accident.
