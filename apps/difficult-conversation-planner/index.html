Difficult Conversation Planner

The conversation you're avoiding won't get easier by waiting. Plan it now: your goal, your opener in your own words, their likely reactions with your responses ready, and a clear success criterion plus exit line.

Step 1 — The goal

What is this conversation really about?

A hard conversation goes sideways when the goal is vague. Name it precisely — and name what you've been avoiding saying.

Step 2 — The opener

Say it in your own words

Write the first 2–3 sentences you'll actually say. A strong opener: states your purpose, stays specific, owns your part, and invites them in — not a lecture.

Step 3 — If they say X, I say Y

Prepare for the three most likely reactions

Think about how this person actually reacts under pressure — defensiveness, deflection, tears, silence, counterattack. Write what they'll probably say, then write your calm planned response. This is the whole game.

Step 4 — Success & exit

Know when you've won — and how to leave

Decide before you walk in what "good enough" looks like, and have a graceful exit line ready so you can close the conversation on your terms instead of trailing off.
