The Trinity, made simple

What it IS: One God, three distinct Persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Each one is fully God, eternal, and equal. They love one another and work together.

What it is NOT: It is not three gods. It is not one Person wearing three masks. The Son and the Spirit are not lesser than the Father.
