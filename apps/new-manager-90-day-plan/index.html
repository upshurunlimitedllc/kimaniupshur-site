Milestone reviews

Block 60 minutes alone for each review. Answer honestly — these prompts are how you catch drift early.

Day 30 — Do I understand?

  1. Can I explain what each report does and what success looks like for them?
  2. What surprised me most about the team or the work?
  3. What is my manager's honest read on my first month?
  4. What is my one biggest risk right now?

Day 60 — Is it working?

  1. Is feedback flowing both ways yet, or am I still guessing?
  2. Which relationships are strong? Which need repair?
  3. What one system, ritual, or process did I measurably improve?
  4. Who is thriving, and who am I avoiding a hard conversation with?

Day 90 — What's next?

  1. Would the team say we are clearer and more effective than 90 days ago?
  2. What are our goals for the next 90 days?
  3. What kind of manager am I becoming — and what must I develop next?
  4. What will I stop doing so I can lead instead of do?
