Good Friday Vigil

The cross of Christ, hour by hour.

Watch with Jesus, station by station

From the third hour, when He was crucified (Mark 15:25), to the moment He bowed His head, the cross unfolded across one long day. Move through the stations slowly. Read the passage, sit with the reflection, and mark each station as you pray.
