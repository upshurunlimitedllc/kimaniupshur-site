Grace vs. Works

Untangling faith, grace, and obedience biblically.

The biblical picture

Grace

  • God's free gift — unearned
  • Received by faith
  • Forgives and saves completely
  • Produces gratitude and obedience

Works

  • Our effort and obedience
  • Cannot earn salvation
  • Flow from salvation, not for it
  • Faith without them is dead
“For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast.”
— Ephesians 2:8–9 (KJV)
“For as the body without the spirit is dead, so faith without works is dead also.”
— James 2:26 (KJV)
