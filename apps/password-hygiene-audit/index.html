    Educational note: This tool gives general security guidance for educational purposes only — it is not professional cybersecurity advice. Two habits matter more than anything here: use a reputable password manager so every account gets its own strong password, and turn on two-factor authentication (preferably an authenticator app) for your email, banking, and social accounts. When in doubt, go to the company’s site directly instead of clicking links in unexpected messages.
