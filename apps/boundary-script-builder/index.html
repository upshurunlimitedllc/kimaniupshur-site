Boundary Script Builder

Take a 10-question assessment to find where your boundaries leak, then get word-for-word scripts for your top two gaps. This is practical communication help — it helps you say what you need clearly and kindly. Boundaries work best when you're consistent: one script won't fix a pattern overnight.

1. The boundary check

Answer honestly about how things usually go — not how you wish they went. Your answers auto-save.

2. Your top boundary gaps

Pick up to two areas to build scripts for. Your two highest scores are pre-selected — change them if a different area matters more right now.

3. Your boundary scripts

Fill in the blanks to personalize each script. Practice them out loud — the first time you say a boundary shouldn't be the moment you need it.

Boundary rehearsal checklist

Wallet card — core phrases

Print this card and keep it handy for the moment a boundary is needed.

  • "No is a complete sentence."
  • "I can't take that on right now."
  • "That doesn't work for me."
  • "Let me think about it — I'll get back to you tomorrow."
  • "I'm not available then, but here's what I can do."
  • "I hear you. My answer is still no."
