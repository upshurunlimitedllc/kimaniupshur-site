Active Listening Drills

Listening is the most underrated communication skill — and the most trainable. Work through 6 realistic scenarios: read what the speaker says, write your paraphrase, label their likely emotion, then compare against model answers and get scored on three sub-skills.

How it works

Each scenario scores you on three sub-skills:

  • Paraphrasing (0–3 pts) — can you reflect back what was said, accurately and in your own words?
  • Emotion labeling (0–2 pts) — can you name what the speaker is likely feeling?
  • Withholding judgment (0–2 pts) — can you pick the response that listens instead of judges?

7 points per scenario · 42 total. Your answers save as you go.
