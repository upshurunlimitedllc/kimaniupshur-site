The decision logic, in one picture

  1. Free money first: an employer match is an instant 50–100% return. Nothing beats it.
  2. Triple tax advantage next: the HSA is the only account with deductible contributions, tax-free growth, and tax-free withdrawals for medical costs.
  3. Then pick your tax timing: Roth (pay tax now) if you're in a lower bracket today than you'll be in retirement; Traditional/pre-tax (deduct now) if you're in a high bracket today and expect a lower one later.
  4. Fill the big bucket last: the 401(k) holds the most ($24,500 for 2026), so it absorbs everything after the match.
  5. Overflow goes taxable: a regular brokerage account has no limits — and no special tax breaks either.
