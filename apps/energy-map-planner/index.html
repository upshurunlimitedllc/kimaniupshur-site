Energy Map Planner

Build a weekly schedule that places your hardest work inside your personal peak-energy windows. Take the 2-minute quiz, rate your energy, add your tasks — and get a printable weekly map plus rules to protect your peaks.

Step 1 — Your chronotype (about 2 minutes)

Answer honestly based on your natural rhythm, not your alarm clock.

Step 2 — Rate your energy blocks

How is your energy typically in each part of the day? (1 = drained, 5 = firing on all cylinders)

Step 3 — Your real tasks

List the recurring tasks or projects competing for your week, and rate how much brainpower each takes.

Step 4 — Your energy-mapped week

We placed your tasks automatically: hard tasks into Peak blocks, medium into Steady, easy into Low. Adjust anything with the dropdowns, then print.
