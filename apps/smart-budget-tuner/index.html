Smart Budget Tuner

Enter your take-home pay and what you actually spend. This tool scores your current split against a 50/30/20-style target (needs / wants / savings), then gives you sliders to rebalance — with concrete trade-off suggestions when savings fall short.

Educational tool only — not professional financial advice. This models your numbers against a common budgeting framework; it doesn't know your obligations, debts, or goals. Treat targets as starting points, not prescriptions.

Step 1 — Your real numbers

Use a typical month. Take-home pay = what actually lands in your account after taxes. Leave categories at 0 if they don't apply.

Step 2 — Tune it with sliders

Drag any slider to change that category's planned amount. Everything below updates live: your split, your score, and trade-off ideas.
