Paycheck Flow Planner

Enter your pay schedule and your bills. Get a paycheck-by-paycheck map showing exactly which bills each paycheck covers — and where the tight weeks are before they surprise you.

Educational modeling, not professional financial advice. This tool maps bill timing against paydays with simple rules. It does not move money, contact billers, or replace a budget or a financial professional.

Step 1 — Your pay

The standing cushion you want left untouched in the account — your overdraft shield. $200–$500 is a common starting point.

Step 2 — Your bills

Every recurring bill with a due date: rent, utilities, phone, insurance, loan minimums, subscriptions. Enter the day of the month it is due (1–31); months without that day use the last day of the month.
