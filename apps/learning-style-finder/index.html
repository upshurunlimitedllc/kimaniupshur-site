How this works

Preferences, not labels

You will answer 15 scenario questions. Twelve reveal which ways of engaging with new material you prefer — seeing, hearing, reading/writing, or doing. Three reveal your ideal focus environment.

An honest note before you begin: large reviews of learning research find no reliable evidence that matching instruction to a fixed “learning style” improves learning. Preferences are real, but they are not fixed traits — and most people learn well through several channels. What the evidence does support is how you study: spaced practice, retrieval practice, interleaving, and dual coding. Your playbook below is built on those methods, tuned to your preferences.
