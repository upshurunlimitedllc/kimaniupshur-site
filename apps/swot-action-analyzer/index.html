SWOT Action Analyzer

Turn a vague SWOT brainstorm into three strategic actions you'll actually take. Brainstorm each quadrant, score every item on impact × feasibility, then let the app cross-match strengths–opportunities and weaknesses–threats into ranked moves.

Step 1 — Brainstorm the four quadrants

Add at least 2 items per quadrant. Tap a prompt chip to use it as a starter, then make it your own.

Step 2 — Score every item

Rate each item: Impact (how much it matters) and Feasibility (how doable it is for you) from 1 to 5. Priority = Impact × Feasibility.

Step 3 — Pick your top 3 moves

The app cross-matched your highest-scored items. Click to select exactly 3 strategic moves — the top 3 are pre-selected.

Step 4 — Make each action real

For each of your 3 actions, define the first step, the owner, and a deadline. Then print or save your one-page plan.
