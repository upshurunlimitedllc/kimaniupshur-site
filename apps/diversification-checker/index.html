Diversification Checker

Enter what you own and this tool hunts for hidden concentration risk: one holding dominating everything, two funds secretly owning the same stocks, a whole portfolio betting on one sector or one country.

Educational purposes only — not financial or investment advice. This models general diversification concepts from the information you enter. It is not a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold anything. Consider talking with a qualified financial professional before making changes.
