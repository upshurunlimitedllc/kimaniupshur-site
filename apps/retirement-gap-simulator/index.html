Assumptions behind the math

  • Growth compounds monthly at your expected return. "Need" = 25× your target annual retirement spending (the 4% withdrawal rule of thumb).
  • Returns are nominal (not inflation-adjusted); contributions stay flat — no raises modeled.
  • Ignores taxes, investment fees, employer-match details, pensions, and Social Security.
  • Conservative beats precise: if you're borderline, plan as if returns come in 1–2% lower.
