Pentecost Reflection

The coming of the Holy Spirit, remembered and celebrated.

Walk through Acts 2, movement by movement

“I will pour out my Spirit on all flesh, and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy.” — Acts 2:17 (ESV)

Pentecost is not only a birthday to remember — it is a promise still open. Walk the five movements of that day, reflect, and ask the Spirit to fill you afresh.

A closing prayer

Come, Holy Spirit. Fill me as You filled that room. Give me boldness to speak of Jesus, purity to be a fit dwelling, and power that is Yours and not mine. As at Pentecost, begin with me — and let it spread. Amen.
