Problem Reframer

The problem as you stated it is rarely the real problem. Restate it five different ways, score each frame on solvability and importance, and walk out with the frame worth solving — plus concrete first steps.

Step 1 — State it

What's the problem?

Say it the way you've been saying it to yourself — rough and honest is fine. You'll reshape it in the next step.

Step 2 — Five reframes

Say it five different ways

Each lens below bends your problem into a new shape. Write a fresh version of your problem through each one — the one that surprises you is often the right one.

Step 3 — Score & pick

Which frame is worth solving?

Rate each reframe on solvability (how much can you actually do about this?) and importance (how much would solving this matter?). 1 = low, 5 = high. Total = solvability × importance.

Live ranking

Step 4 — First steps

Make the winning frame actionable

A reframe is only useful if it changes what you DO. List at least two concrete first steps toward the frame you chose — small enough to start this week.
