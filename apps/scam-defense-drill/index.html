MY SCAM REFLEX CARD

Five checks before I send money or share information

  1. 1. STOP — pressure is the weapon. Real emergencies still give you time to think. Scammers manufacture urgency ("today!", "don't tell anyone!") so you act before you verify.
  2. 2. VERIFY independently. Never use the phone number, link, or reply address in the message. Look up the company yourself and call the number on your card, statement, or official website.
  3. 3. Money rules. No legitimate business asks for gift cards, wire transfers, crypto, or payment-app payments to fix a problem. Never deposit a check and send part of it back.
  4. 4. Lock your info. Never share passwords, PINs, Social Security numbers, or verification codes. Real banks, the IRS, and tech companies will never ask for them by text, email, or call.
  5. 5. Report it. Forward scam texts to 7726 (SPAM) and report to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. Reporting protects the next person.
