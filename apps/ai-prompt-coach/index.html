Better prompts get better answers. Answer a few guided questions and this tool assembles a clear, copy-paste AI prompt — then shows you why each part works so you can write strong prompts on your own.

Honest framing first: AI tools are powerful drafting partners, but they can be confidently wrong, make up facts, and reflect biases. Never paste private information into an AI tool, and always verify important facts yourself. This coach teaches how to ask well — it does not connect to any AI service.
