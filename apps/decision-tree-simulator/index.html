Decision Tree Simulator

Sketch your choices, the chance events under each, and the payoffs — then let expected-value math point to the strongest path. Probabilities that don't add to 100% are normalized for you.

Educational tool only. Expected values describe averages over many repeats, not guarantees for a single decision — and payoffs here are your estimates, not predictions.

Your choices (branches)

Each choice gets its own chance outcomes: a name, your estimated probability (%), and the payoff in dollars (negative for losses). One outcome at 100% = a certain payoff.

Results

Gut check

Expected value is the long-run average. Does the recommended path feel right for one real decision?
