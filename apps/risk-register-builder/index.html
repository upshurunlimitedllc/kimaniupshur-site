Risk Register Builder

List everything that could go wrong with your project, score each risk by likelihood and impact, and leave with a prioritized mitigation plan. What gets written down gets managed.

Step 1 — List the risks

Name each thing that could go wrong. Rate likelihood (1 = rare, 5 = almost certain) and impact (1 = minor annoyance, 5 = project fails). Score = likelihood × impact.

Step 2 — Score and rank

The math ranks your risks so your attention goes where it matters most.

Your Risk Register

Step 3 — Mitigation plan

For each risk below (highest score first), write a mitigation action, an owner, and an early-warning sign — the signal that tells you the risk is starting to happen. High and critical risks are highlighted: do not skip them.
