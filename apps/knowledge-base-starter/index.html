Knowledge Base Starter

Design your personal note-taking system in one sitting: answer a few questions, get a recommended structure of 5–7 categories, starter templates for each, and a 15-minute weekly review ritual.

Step 1 — What do you want to remember?

Check everything you want your system to hold:

How much do you expect to capture in a typical week?

When you need something later, how do you usually look for it?

Where do your notes live right now?

Check at least one thing you want to remember first.

Step 2 — Your recommended structure

Based on your answers, here is a 5–7 category system. Simple rule: every note lives in exactly one category; link between notes instead of duplicating them.

Why these categories? “Inbox” catches everything unprocessed so nothing gets lost. “Projects” holds active work. The rest mirror what you said you want to remember — filing by topic, not by date, is what makes things findable months later.

Step 3 — Starter templates

One template per note type. Copy one into your notes app of choice and fill it in — the structure does the organizing for you.




















Step 4 — Your 15-minute weekly review ritual


A system only works if you maintain it. Once a week, set the timer and walk the checklist. Pick a fixed day and time — put it on your calendar now.




15:00























Step 5 — My system on one page


Your complete reference: categories, templates, review ritual, and the four rules that keep it working. Print it and keep it where you take notes.













A note on tools: this app designs your system, not your software. Any notes app with folders/tags and search will do — the categories, templates, and weekly review are what make it work, not the brand on the tin. For educational purposes only; adapt freely to what you already use.
