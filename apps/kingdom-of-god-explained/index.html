Kingdom of God Explained

The kingdom Jesus preached, made simple.

\u201CThe time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand: repent ye, and believe the gospel.\u201D
\u2014 Mark 1:15 (KJV)

The \u201Ckingdom of God\u201D was Jesus' favorite subject. Simply put: God's rule \u2014 His will being done on earth as it is in heaven. It is already here wherever Jesus reigns, and still coming in fullness. Explore His parables:

\u201CThy kingdom come. Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven.\u201D
\u2014 Matthew 6:10 (KJV)
