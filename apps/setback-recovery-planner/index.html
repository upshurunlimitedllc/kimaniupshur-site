Setback Recovery Planner

A setback is a detour, not a destination. Name what happened, rate where it hit hardest, and get a structured 30-day comeback plan — stabilize first, then rebuild, then launch forward.

Educational framing: This planner is an educational self-reflection and planning tool — not professional financial, career, legal, or mental-health advice. Big setbacks often deserve real support: consider a financial counselor, career coach, or licensed mental-health professional where the stakes are high.
