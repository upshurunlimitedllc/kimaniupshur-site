Quarterly Goal Sprint Planner

Run your goals in 12-week sprints. Pick 1–3 goals, define a weekly leading indicator for each (the controllable action, not just the outcome), then score yourself every week. Includes a week-6 pivot-or-persevere checkpoint and a 10-minute weekly scoring ritual.

Step 1 — Set Up Your Sprint

Your Goals (1–3)

For each goal, name the leading indicator — the controllable weekly action that drives the outcome. Not “lose 10 lbs” (outcome) but “4 workouts” (indicator).

Weekly 10-Minute Scoring Ritual

Do this at the same time every week — for example, Friday at 4:00 PM. Start the timer, work the steps, stop at zero.

10:00
  1. Score the week (2 min): Tap each cell in the current week column above — green if you hit the weekly target, red if you missed.
  2. Note what happened (3 min): In one sentence per goal, write what helped or what got in the way. Keep it short.
  3. Plan next week (4 min): Schedule the leading-indicator action on your calendar before this timer ends. If it is not scheduled, it will not happen.
  4. Protect the streak (1 min): Look at your overall score. Anything below 70% gets one adjustment next week — smaller target, earlier time slot, or a removed blocker.

Week-6 Mid-Sprint Checkpoint

At the halfway mark, answer these honestly. The question is not “am I perfect?” — it is pivot or persevere?

1. Is each goal's leading indicator actually within your control?

2. Are you hitting at least 70% of your weekly targets so far?

3. What keeps getting in the way? (The real obstacle, not the excuse.)

4. What is one change that would make the next 6 weeks easier?

5. Pivot or persevere?
