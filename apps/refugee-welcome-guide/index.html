Refugee Welcome Guide

Loving the stranger among us (Leviticus 19:34).

Welcome the stranger the way God welcomed us

“You shall treat the stranger who sojourns with you as the native among you, and you shall love him as yourself.” — Leviticus 19:34 (ESV)

New neighbors arriving from hard places do not need our pity — they need friends. Tap everything you could genuinely offer, and the app builds your personal welcome plan.

What I can offer

Your plan will appear here.

First-week kindnesses that matter most

• A warm meal and an unhurried welcome at your table.
• A tour: grocery store, bus stop, school, library, clinic.
• Your phone number, written down, with “call anytime.”
• Learn ten words of their language — effort speaks louder than fluency.
• Remember: friendship over months matters more than a big first week.
