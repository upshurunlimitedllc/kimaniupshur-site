How the math works

  • Cost uses a 1.3× fully-loaded multiplier on salary (benefits + overhead) and 2,080 work hours/year.
  • Meetings per year: daily = 250, weekly = 50, every 2 weeks = 26, monthly = 12.
  • Reclaimable savings assume: cancel = 100% of cost, make-async = 50%, shorten = 25%, keep = 0%.
  • These are planning estimates for educational purposes — not payroll figures.
