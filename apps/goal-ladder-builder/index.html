Goal Ladder Builder

Reverse-plan a 5-year vision down to this quarter's milestones using a stair-step ladder. Start at the top, walk backward — 5 years, 3 years, 1 year, this quarter — and get a dependency check plus a starter action list.

Step 1 — Your 5-year vision

Write it as if it already happened. Be concrete: who are you, what are you doing, what does life look like five years from today?

Step 2 — Walk backward down the ladder

For each rung, answer the question and add 2–3 measurable milestones — things you could prove with a number, a date, or a finished deliverable.
