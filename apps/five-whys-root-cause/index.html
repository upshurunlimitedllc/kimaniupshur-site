Five Whys Root-Cause Finder

Drill past symptoms to the real root cause of any problem with a guided "why?" chain — up to five levels deep, with evidence at every step — then target your fix at the cause, not the symptom.

Be specific and factual. "Sales dropped 30% in Q3" beats "sales are bad." Include what happened, and when.

Step 2 — Ask why, up to five times

Each "why" digs one level below the previous answer. Back every answer with evidence — facts, data, observations — not guesses. The app will nudge you if an answer looks like a restatement or a guess.

Step 3 — Confirm the root cause

A real root cause passes two tests: (1) fixing it would prevent the problem from recurring, and (2) it points to something you can actually change — a process, a decision, a habit.

Step 4 — Countermeasure plan

Fix the root cause, not the symptom. Each countermeasure needs an owner and a way to verify it worked.
