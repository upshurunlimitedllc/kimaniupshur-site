Salary Offer Evaluator

A $120,000 salary with a 10% bonus and no equity is not the same as $105,000 with RSUs, a 6% match, and cheap health insurance. Enter both offers and the tool translates everything into one comparable annual number — adjusted for cost of living — so you can see the real difference.

Educational modeling only — not financial or career advice. Equity is valued conservatively, bonuses are discounted, and cost-of-living indexes are rough estimates. Numbers help you compare; they can't capture everything (see the non-money factors below).
