Stage-Fright Exposure Ladder

Fear shrinks with graded exposure: face slightly scarier speaking situations, one rung at a time, until your brain learns the stage is safe. Build your ladder, rehearse with the timer and pre-talk routine, and log every attempt.

Educational tool only — not professional psychological advice. If anxiety severely limits your life, consider working with a licensed professional.

Build your fear ladder

8 rungs, from easy to terrifying. Edit any rung to match your real fears, then rate your anxiety for each from 1 (calm) to 10 (panic). Aim for a smooth climb — no jumps bigger than 2–3 points.

Anxiety jumps of more than 3 points between rungs make exposure backfire. Lower a higher rung or raise a lower one to smooth the climb.
