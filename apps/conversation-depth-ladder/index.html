Conversation Depth Ladder

Move conversations from small talk to meaningful connection — one rung at a time. Pick your setting, climb the 4-rung question ladder, and use the transition phrases and green-light / yellow-light cues to go deeper gracefully.

1 · Pick your setting

Where is this conversation happening? Your ladder is tailored to the setting.

Transition phrases — going deeper gracefully

Use one of these to bridge between rungs. A good transition names what they just said, then opens the next door.

        Practice drill — rehearse going deeper

        Pick any 3 questions from your ladder and one transition phrase. Then rehearse each one out loud — transitions included. Saying it once beforehand makes it natural in the moment.

        Pick 3 different questions for the drill.

        Say the transition, then the question — out loud, in your own words. Then move on.

        Drill complete

        You just rehearsed 3 real questions and a graceful transition. In your next conversation, listen for one green light — then climb one rung. That is the whole skill.
